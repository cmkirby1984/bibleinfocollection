# Miracles of the Bible Index

A comprehensive guide to all miracles in Scripture, organized by type and location, demonstrating God's power over nature, disease, demons, and death.

---

## What Are Miracles?

**Definition:** A miracle is a supernatural act of God that transcends natural laws, demonstrating His power and authenticating His messengers.

**Purpose of Miracles:**
1. Reveal God's glory and character
2. Authenticate God's messengers (prophets, apostles, Jesus)
3. Demonstrate God's compassion and mercy
4. Confirm the truth of God's Word
5. Point to spiritual realities

**Jesus' Miracles Specifically:**
- Proved He was the Messiah (John 20:30-31)
- Demonstrated the kingdom's arrival (Matthew 12:28)
- Fulfilled prophecy (Isaiah 35:5-6; Matthew 11:4-5)
- Revealed His divine nature and authority

## Old Testament Miracles

### Creation (Genesis 1-2)
- **The ultimate miracle:** God speaks the universe into existence from nothing
- **Significance:** Establishes God's absolute power and sovereignty

### The Flood (Genesis 6-9)
- **Miracle:** Worldwide flood, Noah's ark preserves life
- **Significance:** God's judgment on sin, salvation through faith

### Tower of Babel (Genesis 11)
- **Miracle:** Confusion of languages
- **Significance:** God limits human pride and rebellion

### Destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah (Genesis 19)
- **Miracle:** Fire and brimstone from heaven
- **Significance:** God's judgment on wickedness

### The Burning Bush (Exodus 3)
- **Miracle:** Bush burns but isn't consumed; God speaks to Moses
- **Significance:** God's holy presence, call to deliver Israel

### The Ten Plagues on Egypt (Exodus 7-12)
1. Water to blood
2. Frogs
3. Gnats
4. Flies
5. Death of livestock
6. Boils
7. Hail
8. Locusts
9. Darkness
10. Death of firstborn
- **Significance:** God's power over Egyptian "gods," liberation of His people

### Parting the Red Sea (Exodus 14)
- **Miracle:** Sea divides; Israel crosses on dry ground; Egyptians drown
- **Significance:** God's salvation and judgment; baptism typology

### Manna from Heaven (Exodus 16)
- **Miracle:** Daily bread from heaven for 40 years
- **Significance:** God's provision; points to Christ, the Bread of Life

### Water from the Rock (Exodus 17; Numbers 20)
- **Miracle:** Moses strikes rock; water flows for thirsty Israel
- **Significance:** Christ is the Rock struck for us (1 Corinthians 10:4)

### Pillar of Cloud and Fire (Exodus 13, 40)
- **Miracle:** God's presence guides Israel day and night
- **Significance:** God's continual presence and guidance

### Mount Sinai Theophany (Exodus 19-20)
- **Miracle:** Thunder, lightning, trumpet, God's voice from mountain
- **Significance:** God's awesome holiness; giving of the Law

### Aaron's Rod Buds (Numbers 17)
- **Miracle:** Dead staff produces almonds overnight
- **Significance:** God's choice of Aaron's priesthood

### Bronze Serpent (Numbers 21)
- **Miracle:** Look at bronze serpent and be healed from snake bites
- **Significance:** Type of Christ lifted up (John 3:14-15)

### Balaam's Donkey Speaks (Numbers 22)
- **Miracle:** Donkey sees angel and speaks to Balaam
- **Significance:** God can use anything to accomplish His purposes

### Jordan River Parts (Joshua 3)
- **Miracle:** Jordan stops flowing; Israel crosses on dry ground
- **Significance:** God with Joshua as He was with Moses

### Fall of Jericho (Joshua 6)
- **Miracle:** Walls collapse after marching and shouting
- **Significance:** Victory through obedience; God fights for His people

### Sun Stands Still (Joshua 10)
- **Miracle:** Sun stops in the sky for about a day
- **Significance:** God controls creation; gives complete victory

### Gideon's Fleece (Judges 6)
- **Miracle:** Dew on fleece but not ground; then reverse
- **Significance:** God confirms His call and promises

### Samson's Strength (Judges 14-16)
- **Miracle:** Supernatural strength to kill lion, break ropes, defeat armies
- **Significance:** God's power through dedicated servant

### Fire from Heaven (1 Kings 18)
- **Miracle:** Elijah's sacrifice consumed by fire despite being soaked
- **Significance:** The LORD is God; Baal is nothing

### Drought and Rain (1 Kings 17-18)
- **Miracle:** 3.5 years without rain; then rain at Elijah's prayer
- **Significance:** God controls nature; judgment and blessing

### Widow's Oil and Flour (1 Kings 17)
- **Miracle:** Oil and flour don't run out during famine
- **Significance:** God provides for those who trust Him

### Widow's Son Raised (1 Kings 17)
- **Miracle:** Elijah raises dead boy to life
- **Significance:** God has power over death

### Elijah Fed by Ravens (1 Kings 17)
- **Miracle:** Ravens bring bread and meat daily
- **Significance:** God provides in impossible ways

### Elijah Taken to Heaven (2 Kings 2)
- **Miracle:** Chariot of fire; Elijah ascends in whirlwind
- **Significance:** God's special servants; no death (like Enoch)

### Jordan Parts for Elijah and Elisha (2 Kings 2)
- **Miracle:** Jordan divides when struck with cloak
- **Significance:** God's power transferred to Elisha

### Purifying Poisoned Water (2 Kings 2)
- **Miracle:** Elisha purifies bad water with salt
- **Significance:** God heals and purifies

### Bears Maul Mockers (2 Kings 2)
- **Miracle:** Bears attack youths who mocked Elisha
- **Significance:** God protects His servants; respect for prophets

### Widow's Oil Multiplied (2 Kings 4)
- **Miracle:** Small jar of oil fills many jars
- **Significance:** God provides abundantly for the faithful

### Shunammite Woman's Son Raised (2 Kings 4)
- **Miracle:** Elisha raises dead boy to life
- **Significance:** Reward for hospitality; God's power over death

### Poisoned Stew Made Safe (2 Kings 4)
- **Miracle:** Flour thrown in deadly stew makes it safe
- **Significance:** God protects His servants

### Feeding 100 Men (2 Kings 4)
- **Miracle:** 20 loaves feed 100 with leftovers
- **Significance:** Foreshadows Jesus' feeding miracles

### Naaman Healed of Leprosy (2 Kings 5)
- **Miracle:** Syrian commander healed after washing in Jordan
- **Significance:** God's power available to Gentiles; humility and obedience required

### Gehazi Struck with Leprosy (2 Kings 5)
- **Miracle:** Elisha's servant gets Naaman's leprosy for greed
- **Significance:** God judges deception and greed

### Axe Head Floats (2 Kings 6)
- **Miracle:** Iron axe head floats in water
- **Significance:** God cares about small problems; nothing too hard for Him

### Elisha Sees Angelic Army (2 Kings 6)
- **Miracle:** Servant's eyes opened to see horses and chariots of fire
- **Significance:** God's invisible protection; more with us than against us

### Aramean Army Struck Blind (2 Kings 6)
- **Miracle:** Elisha prays; enemy army blinded then sight restored
- **Significance:** God controls battles; mercy to enemies

### Dead Man Raised by Elisha's Bones (2 Kings 13)
- **Miracle:** Dead man thrown in Elisha's tomb comes to life
- **Significance:** God's power remains even after prophet's death

### Hezekiah's Sundial (2 Kings 20; Isaiah 38)
- **Miracle:** Shadow goes backward ten steps
- **Significance:** God confirms His promise of healing and deliverance

### Hezekiah Healed (2 Kings 20; Isaiah 38)
- **Miracle:** 15 years added to life after terminal illness
- **Significance:** God answers prayer; extends life

### Sennacherib's Army Destroyed (2 Kings 19; Isaiah 37)
- **Miracle:** Angel kills 185,000 Assyrian soldiers overnight
- **Significance:** God defends His people; pride judged

### Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego in Fiery Furnace (Daniel 3)
- **Miracle:** Three men walk in blazing furnace unharmed
- **Significance:** God delivers those who refuse to compromise

### Daniel in Lions' Den (Daniel 6)
- **Miracle:** Lions don't harm Daniel all night
- **Significance:** God protects the faithful; shuts enemies' mouths

### Jonah and the Great Fish (Jonah 1-2)
- **Miracle:** Jonah survives three days in fish's belly
- **Significance:** God's mercy; type of Christ's burial and resurrection

#### Peter's Mother-in-Law Healed

- **Lesson:** Personal touch of Jesus; healing leads to service

#### Many Healed at Evening
- **References:** Matthew 8:16-17; Mark 1:32-34; Luke 4:40-41
- **Location:** Capernaum
- **Lesson:** Fulfills Isaiah 53; Jesus' compassion for all who come

#### Paralyzed Man Lowered Through Roof

#### Woman with Issue of Blood

#### Two Blind Men Healed

- **Lesson:** "Do you believe I can do this?"; faith required

#### Deaf and Mute Man Healed

- **Lesson:** Gradual healing (unique); may picture disciples' gradual understanding

#### Ten Lepers Cleansed

#### Blind Bartimaeus Healed
- **References:** Matthew 20:29-34; Mark 10:46-52; Luke 18:35-43

- **Lesson:** Persistent faith; "Jesus, Son of David, have mercy"; follows Jesus after healing

#### Woman Bent Over Healed
- **References:** Luke 13:10-17

- **Lesson:** Satan's bondage broken; religious leaders care more about rules than people

#### Man with Dropsy Healed

- **Location:** Pharisee's house on Sabbath
- **Lesson:** Compassion over legalism; Jesus challenges Sabbath hypocrisy

#### Canaanite Woman's Daughter Healed
- **References:** Matthew 15:21-28; Mark 7:24-30

- **Lesson:** Great faith from Gentile; persistence rewarded; gospel for all nations

#### Centurion's Servant Healed (Distance)
- **References:** Matthew 8:5-13; Luke 7:1-10

#### Official's Son Healed

#### Malchus' Ear Restored
- **References:** Luke 22:49-51; John 18:10

### Nature Miracles

#### Water Turned to Wine
- **References:** John 2:1-11
- **Location:** Cana of Galilee
- **First miracle:** Reveals Jesus' glory; disciples believe

#### Calming the Storm
- **References:** Matthew 8:23-27; Mark 4:35-41; Luke 8:22-25
- **Location:** Sea of Galilee
- **Lesson:** Jesus has authority over creation; "Why are you afraid?"; faith vs. fear

#### Feeding 5,000
- **References:** Matthew 14:13-21; Mark 6:30-44; Luke 9:10-17; John 6:1-15
- **Location:** Near Bethsaida
- **Only miracle in all 4 Gospels:** Shows its importance
- **Lesson:** Jesus provides abundantly; bread of life; compassion for the hungry

#### Walking on Water
- **References:** Matthew 14:22-33; Mark 6:45-52; John 6:16-21

- **Lesson:** "It is I; don't be afraid"; Peter's bold faith and doubt; Jesus saves

#### Feeding 4,000

- **Lesson:** Jesus' continued compassion; similar but distinct from feeding 5,000

#### Coin in Fish's Mouth
- **References:** Matthew 17:24-27
- **Location:** Capernaum
- **Lesson:** Jesus' provision; freedom yet avoiding offense

#### Fig Tree Withered
- **References:** Matthew 21:18-22; Mark 11:12-14, 20-25

- **Lesson:** Judgment on fruitless Israel; faith and prayer; forgiveness

#### Large Catch of Fish (First)
- **References:** Luke 5:1-11

## Statistics and Insights

### Jesus' Miracles

**Total Miracles:** 37+ specific miracles recorded (many more mentioned generally)

**By Type:**
- Healing: 23+ specific instances
- Nature: 8 miracles
- Exorcisms: 6+ specific instances
- Raising Dead: 3 instances

**By Gospel:**
- Matthew: ~20 specific miracles
- Mark: ~18 specific miracles
- Luke: ~20 specific miracles
- John: 7 specific "signs" (carefully selected)

**General Statements:**
- Matthew 4:23-24 - Healed every disease and sickness
- Matthew 9:35 - Healed every disease and sickness
- Matthew 14:14 - Healed their sick
- Mark 1:32-34 - Healed many who had various diseases
- Luke 6:17-19 - Power came from Him and healed them all
- John 20:30 - Many other signs Jesus performed

### Most Common Miracles
1. **Healing** (especially blindness, leprosy, paralysis, demon possession)
2. **Exorcisms** (showing authority over Satan's kingdom)
3. **Nature miracles** (demonstrating deity and control over creation)

### Geographic Distribution
- **Galilee:** Most miracles (Jesus' primary ministry area)
- **Jerusalem/Judea:** Fewer recorded miracles
- **Gentile regions:** Some notable miracles (Decapolis, Tyre/Sidon)

### Sabbath Miracles
**At least 7 miracles done on the Sabbath** - challenging Pharisaic tradition:
1. Man at Pool of Bethesda (John 5)
2. Man with withered hand (Matthew 12; Mark 3; Luke 6)
3. Woman bent over (Luke 13)
4. Man with dropsy (Luke 14)
5. Man born blind (John 9)
6. Demon-possessed man in synagogue (Mark 1; Luke 4)
7. Peter's mother-in-law (Mark 1; Luke 4)

## Why Fewer Miracles Today?

**Reasons miracles were concentrated in biblical times:**
1. **Authenticating God's messengers** - Prophets, Jesus, apostles needed credentials
2. **Establishing new revelation** - Miracles confirmed God's Word (Hebrews 2:3-4)
3. **Specific redemptive purposes** - Jesus' miracles proved He was Messiah

**But God still works miracles:**
- Answered prayer
- Changed lives (the greatest miracle)
- Providence and protection
- Healing (though not always instantaneous or guaranteed)
- The gospel itself is God's power (Romans 1:16)

## Lessons from Jesus' Miracles

1. **Jesus is God** - Only God has authority over nature, disease, demons, and death
2. **Jesus is compassionate** - Moved by suffering; heals out of mercy
3. **Faith matters** - Often Jesus says, "Your faith has healed you"
4. **Nothing is impossible** - No disease, demon, or death can resist His power
5. **Wholeness is the goal** - Physical healing points to spiritual healing
6. **Miracles reveal glory** - They point to Jesus' identity and mission
7. **The kingdom has come** - Miracles demonstrate God's kingdom breaking into this world

## How to Study Miracles

1. **Read the miracle in all Gospel accounts** to see different details emphasized
2. **Note the context** - Why did Jesus perform this miracle at this time?
3. **Identify faith or lack of it** - Was faith required? Whose faith?
4. **Observe Jesus' methods** - Touch, word, command, prayer, delay, etc.
5. **See the spiritual truth** - Physical miracles illustrate spiritual realities
6. **Watch reactions** - How did people respond? Witnesses? Religious leaders?
7. **Apply personally** - What does this reveal about Jesus? What should I believe/do?

---

## Study Suggestions

1. Read John's 7 "signs" and note what each reveals about Jesus
2. Study all healing miracles and categorize Jesus' methods
3. Compare OT miracles to Jesus' miracles - similarities and differences
4. Trace the theme of "faith" through the miracle accounts
5. Study the Sabbath controversies surrounding Jesus' miracles
6. Note every time Jesus said "Your faith has healed/saved you"
