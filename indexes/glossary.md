# Biblical Terms Glossary

A comprehensive guide to key theological terms, important Hebrew and Greek words, and biblical concepts. Use this resource to deepen your understanding of Scripture.

---

## How to Use This Glossary

This glossary provides:
- Clear definitions of theological terms
- Meanings of important Hebrew (H) and Greek (G) words
- Cross-references to where terms appear in Scripture
- Practical understanding of biblical concepts

**Note:** Hebrew words are marked with (H), Greek words with (G)

## A

### Abba (G - ἀββά)
**Meaning:** Aramaic word for "father," conveying intimacy and trust, like "daddy" or "papa"
**Usage:** Used by Jesus in prayer (Mark 14:36) and by believers through the Spirit (Romans 8:15; Galatians 4:6)
**Significance:** Demonstrates the intimate relationship believers have with God as adopted children

### Advocate
**Meaning:** One who pleads another's cause; a helper or intercessor
**Greek:** Parakletos (παράκλητος) - means "one called alongside to help"
**Usage:** Used of the Holy Spirit (John 14:16, 26; 15:26; 16:7) and of Jesus (1 John 2:1)
**Significance:** Both Christ and the Spirit actively help and intercede for believers

### Agape (G - ἀγάπη)
**Meaning:** Unconditional, self-sacrificial love; the highest form of love
**Usage:** God's love for humanity (John 3:16); the love Christians are to show (1 Corinthians 13; 1 John 4:7-8)
**Significance:** Distinguishes divine love from mere affection (phileo) or romantic love (eros)

### Amen
**Meaning:** Hebrew/Greek word meaning "truly," "so be it," or "let it be so"
**Usage:** Used to affirm prayers, statements of truth, and doxologies
**Significance:** Christ is called "the Amen" as the faithful and true witness (Revelation 3:14)

### Angel
**Meaning:** Spiritual beings created by God to serve Him and minister to believers
**Hebrew:** Mal'ak (מַלְאָךְ) - "messenger"
**Greek:** Angelos (ἄγγελος) - "messenger"
**Significance:** Both holy angels serve God (Hebrews 1:14) and fallen angels rebelled with Satan

### Anoint / Anointing
**Meaning:** To pour oil on someone or something as a sign of consecration, blessing, or empowerment
**Hebrew:** Mashach (מָשַׁח) - to smear or anoint
**Greek:** Chrio (χρίω) - to anoint
**Usage:** Priests, kings, and prophets were anointed; Christ means "Anointed One"
**Significance:** Represents being set apart for God's service and empowered by the Holy Spirit

### Apostle
**Meaning:** One who is sent with authority as a messenger or representative
**Greek:** Apostolos (ἀπόστολος) - "sent one"
**Usage:** The Twelve chosen by Jesus; Paul; also used more broadly of other messengers
**Significance:** Special authority to establish churches and write inspired Scripture

### Atonement
**Meaning:** The act of making amends for sin; reconciling God and humanity
**Hebrew:** Kaphar (כָּפַר) - to cover, purge, or make reconciliation
**Greek:** Hilasmos (ἱλασμός) - propitiation, atoning sacrifice
**Usage:** Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16); Christ's atoning sacrifice (Romans 3:25; 1 John 2:2)
**Significance:** Christ's death satisfies God's wrath and covers our sins

## B

### Baptism
**Meaning:** Ritual washing symbolizing purification, death to old life, and new life in Christ
**Greek:** Baptizo (βαπτίζω) - to immerse, dip, or wash
**Usage:** John's baptism of repentance; Christian baptism into Christ (Matthew 28:19; Romans 6:3-4)
**Significance:** Outward sign of inward spiritual reality; identification with Christ's death and resurrection

### Beatitudes
**Meaning:** The "blessed are" statements from Jesus' Sermon on the Mount
**Latin:** From "beatus" meaning "blessed" or "happy"
**Usage:** Matthew 5:3-12; Luke 6:20-23
**Significance:** Describe character traits and attitudes of kingdom citizens

### Believer
**Meaning:** One who has faith in and trusts Jesus Christ for salvation
**Usage:** Used throughout Acts and epistles for Christians
**Significance:** Emphasizes faith as the means of salvation and relationship with God

### Bless / Blessing
**Meaning:** To confer well-being, favor, or spiritual benefit; God's gracious benefits to humans
**Hebrew:** Barak (בָּרַךְ) - to kneel, bless, praise
**Greek:** Eulogeo (εὐλογέω) - to speak well of, to bless
**Significance:** God blesses His people with spiritual and material benefits; we bless (praise) God

### Blood
**Meaning:** Represents life; shed blood represents death; Christ's blood represents His atoning sacrifice
**Hebrew:** Dam (דָּם)
**Greek:** Haima (αἷμα)
**Usage:** "Without shedding of blood there is no forgiveness" (Hebrews 9:22)
**Significance:** Christ's blood cleanses from sin and establishes new covenant

### Born Again
**Meaning:** Spiritual regeneration; new birth through the Holy Spirit
**Greek:** Gennao anothen (γεννάω ἄνωθεν) - born from above
**Usage:** Jesus' teaching to Nicodemus (John 3:3-8); Peter's teaching (1 Peter 1:23)
**Significance:** Necessary for salvation; transformation from spiritual death to life

## C

### Calling
**Meaning:** God's invitation to salvation and His purpose for believers' lives
**Greek:** Klesis (κλῆσις) - calling, invitation
**Usage:** General call to all people; effectual call to believers (Romans 8:28-30; Ephesians 4:1)
**Significance:** God initiates salvation and assigns purpose to believers

### Canon
**Meaning:** The authoritative collection of books recognized as inspired Scripture
**Greek:** Kanon (κανών) - measuring rod, rule, standard
**Usage:** 39 Old Testament books; 27 New Testament books
**Significance:** Establishes what is God's Word with divine authority

### Cherubim
**Meaning:** High-ranking angelic beings associated with God's glory and holiness
**Hebrew:** Kerubim (כְּרוּבִים) - plural of cherub
**Usage:** Guarded Eden (Genesis 3:24); on ark of covenant (Exodus 25:18-22); in Ezekiel's visions
**Significance:** Represent God's majesty and holiness

### Christ
**Meaning:** The Anointed One; Greek equivalent of Hebrew "Messiah"
**Greek:** Christos (Χριστός)
**Hebrew:** Mashiach (מָשִׁיחַ)
**Significance:** Jesus is the promised Anointed One—Prophet, Priest, and King

### Church
**Meaning:** The assembly of believers; the body of Christ
**Greek:** Ekklesia (ἐκκλησία) - called-out ones, assembly
**Usage:** Universal church (all believers); local church (specific congregation)
**Significance:** Not a building but the people of God, bride of Christ, His body

### Circumcision
**Meaning:** Removal of male foreskin as sign of covenant; spiritual "circumcision of heart"
**Hebrew:** Mul (מוּל) - to circumcise
**Usage:** Sign of Abrahamic covenant (Genesis 17); spiritual symbol (Deuteronomy 10:16; Colossians 2:11)
**Significance:** OT sign of covenant; NT speaks of circumcision of heart by the Spirit

### Confession
**Meaning:** To acknowledge or agree with; admitting sin or affirming truth about God
**Greek:** Homologeo (ὁμολογέω) - to say the same thing, agree, confess
**Usage:** Confess sins (1 John 1:9); confess Christ (Romans 10:9)
**Significance:** Essential for forgiveness and salvation

### Covenant
**Meaning:** A binding agreement between God and His people
**Hebrew:** Berith (בְּרִית) - covenant, compact, agreement
**Greek:** Diatheke (διαθήκη) - covenant, will, testament
**Major Covenants:**
- Noahic (Genesis 9) - with all creation
- Abrahamic (Genesis 12, 15, 17) - land, descendants, blessing
- Mosaic (Exodus 19-24) - law given at Sinai
- Davidic (2 Samuel 7) - eternal kingdom
- New Covenant (Jeremiah 31; Luke 22) - through Christ's blood
**Significance:** Framework for understanding God's relationship with humanity

## D

### Deacon
**Meaning:** A servant or minister in the church
**Greek:** Diakonos (διάκονος) - servant, minister
**Usage:** Church office (1 Timothy 3:8-13); general service
**Significance:** Formal role of servant leadership in the church

### Disciple
**Meaning:** A learner or follower of a teacher; student
**Greek:** Mathetes (μαθητής) - learner, pupil
**Usage:** Followers of Jesus; Christians (Acts 11:26)
**Significance:** Emphasizes learning from and following Jesus

### Dispensation
**Meaning:** A period of time in which God deals with humanity in a particular way; stewardship
**Greek:** Oikonomia (οἰκονομία) - administration, stewardship
**Usage:** Different periods of biblical history with distinct administrations
**Significance:** Helps understand progressive revelation and God's unfolding plan

### Doctrine
**Meaning:** Teaching or instruction; systematic statement of beliefs
**Greek:** Didache (διδαχή) - teaching, instruction
**Usage:** Sound doctrine (1 Timothy 1:10; 2 Timothy 4:3); apostles' doctrine (Acts 2:42)
**Significance:** What the Bible teaches about God, salvation, and living

---

## E

### Elder
**Meaning:** Church leader; overseer of a local congregation
**Greek:** Presbuteros (πρεσβύτερος) - older, elder
**Usage:** Leadership role in church (Acts 14:23; 1 Timothy 3:1-7; Titus 1:5-9; 1 Peter 5:1-4)
**Significance:** Shepherds who lead, teach, and care for God's flock

### Election
**Meaning:** God's sovereign choice of individuals for salvation
**Greek:** Ekloge (ἐκλογή) - choice, selection
**Usage:** God chose Israel (Deuteronomy 7:6); God chose believers (Romans 9:11; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Peter 1:1-2)
**Significance:** Emphasizes God's sovereign grace in salvation

### Eternal Life
**Meaning:** Endless life with God; quality and quantity of life in Christ
**Greek:** Zoe aionios (ζωὴ αἰώνιος) - life everlasting
**Usage:** Gift through faith in Christ (John 3:16; 17:3; Romans 6:23; 1 John 5:11-13)
**Significance:** Begins now and continues forever; knowing God through Christ

### Evangelist
**Meaning:** One who proclaims the gospel; bearer of good news
**Greek:** Euangelistes (εὐαγγελιστής) - preacher of the gospel
**Usage:** Philip the evangelist (Acts 21:8); gift to the church (Ephesians 4:11); Timothy urged to do work of (2 Timothy 4:5)
**Significance:** Special calling to proclaim the gospel and make disciples

### Expiation
**Meaning:** The removal or cleansing of sin
**Related to propitiation but emphasizes sin removed rather than wrath appeased
**Usage:** Christ's sacrifice removes our sins (Hebrews 9:26; 1 John 1:7)
**Significance:** Christ dealt with our sins completely

## F

### Faith
**Meaning:** Trust in and reliance upon God and His promises; believing confidence
**Hebrew:** Emunah (אֱמוּנָה) - firmness, faithfulness, trust
**Greek:** Pistis (πίστις) - faith, belief, trust
**Usage:** Saving faith (Ephesians 2:8); living by faith (Habakkuk 2:4; Romans 1:17)
**Significance:** The means by which we receive salvation and please God (Hebrews 11:6)

### Fellowship
**Meaning:** Sharing in common; partnership; communion with God and believers
**Greek:** Koinonia (κοινωνία) - communion, fellowship, participation
**Usage:** Fellowship with God (1 John 1:3); fellowship with believers (Acts 2:42; Philippians 2:1)
**Significance:** Essential aspect of Christian life—shared life in Christ

### Flesh
**Meaning:** Physical body; human nature; sinful nature opposed to the Spirit
**Greek:** Sarx (σάρξ) - flesh, body, human nature
**Usage:** Physical body (Romans 1:3); sinful nature (Romans 7:18, 25; 8:3-9; Galatians 5:16-17)
**Significance:** Context determines if neutral (physical body) or negative (sinful nature)

### Forgiveness
**Meaning:** Pardoning of sin; removal of guilt and penalty
**Hebrew:** Salach (סָלַח) - to forgive, pardon
**Greek:** Aphiemi (ἀφίημι) - to send away, forgive, pardon
**Usage:** God forgives sins (Psalm 103:3; 1 John 1:9); we forgive others (Matthew 6:12; Ephesians 4:32)
**Significance:** Made possible through Christ's blood; required of believers toward others

## G

### Gentile
**Meaning:** Non-Jewish person; the nations
**Hebrew:** Goyim (גּוֹיִם) - nations, peoples
**Greek:** Ethne (ἔθνη) - nations, Gentiles
**Usage:** Distinction between Jew and Gentile throughout Scripture
**Significance:** Gospel is for all people; in Christ, Jew and Gentile are one (Galatians 3:28; Ephesians 2:14)

### Glory
**Meaning:** The visible manifestation of God's presence; honor, splendor, majesty
**Hebrew:** Kabod (כָּבוֹד) - weight, honor, glory
**Greek:** Doxa (δόξα) - glory, splendor, brightness
**Usage:** God's glory filled tabernacle/temple; Jesus revealed God's glory; believers reflect God's glory
**Significance:** Displays God's character, majesty, and presence

### Gospel
**Meaning:** Good news of salvation through Jesus Christ
**Greek:** Euangelion (εὐαγγέλιον) - good news, glad tidings
**Content:** Christ died for sins, was buried, rose on third day (1 Corinthians 15:1-4)
**Significance:** Power of God for salvation (Romans 1:16); message we proclaim

### Grace
**Meaning:** Unmerited favor; God's kindness toward undeserving sinners
**Hebrew:** Chen (חֵן) - grace, favor
**Greek:** Charis (χάρις) - grace, favor, kindness
**Usage:** Saved by grace (Ephesians 2:8-9); grace abounds over sin (Romans 5:20)
**Significance:** Basis of salvation and Christian life; we don't earn or deserve it

## H

### Hallelujah
**Meaning:** "Praise the LORD"; expression of worship
**Hebrew:** Hallelu-Yah (הַלְלוּ-יָהּ) - praise Yahweh
**Usage:** Frequently in Psalms; heavenly worship (Revelation 19:1, 3, 4, 6)
**Significance:** Universal call to praise God

### Heart
**Meaning:** The center of one's being; mind, will, and emotions
**Hebrew:** Leb (לֵב) - heart, mind, inner man
**Greek:** Kardia (καρδία) - heart
**Usage:** God looks at the heart (1 Samuel 16:7); out of heart come issues of life (Proverbs 4:23)
**Significance:** Represents the true self, not just emotions

### Heaven
**Meaning:** The sky/atmosphere; God's dwelling place; eternal home of believers
**Hebrew:** Shamayim (שָׁמַיִם) - heavens, sky
**Greek:** Ouranos (οὐρανός) - heaven, sky
**Three Heavens:** Atmosphere (birds fly); space (stars); God's dwelling (2 Corinthians 12:2)
**Significance:** Where God dwells; believers' eternal home

### Hell
**Meaning:** Place of eternal punishment for the wicked
**Greek Terms:**
- Hades (ᾅδης) - realm of the dead
- Gehenna (γέεννα) - place of eternal punishment (Matthew 5:22, 29; 10:28)
**Usage:** Lake of fire (Revelation 20:14-15)
**Significance:** Eternal separation from God; final judgment for unbelievers

### Hesed (H - חֶסֶד)
**Meaning:** Covenant love, steadfast love, lovingkindness, loyal love
**Usage:** Describes God's faithful covenant love (Exodus 34:6; Psalm 136; Lamentations 3:22)
**Significance:** God's committed, enduring love toward His covenant people

### Holiness
**Meaning:** Set apart for God; moral purity; separation from sin
**Hebrew:** Qodesh (קֹדֶשׁ) - holiness, sacredness
**Greek:** Hagios (ἅγιος) - holy, sacred, set apart
**Usage:** God is holy (Isaiah 6:3); believers called to be holy (Leviticus 11:44; 1 Peter 1:15-16)
**Significance:** God's essential nature; required of His people

### Holy Spirit
**Meaning:** Third person of the Trinity; God's presence and power in believers
**Hebrew:** Ruach HaKodesh (רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ) - Spirit of holiness
**Greek:** Pneuma Hagion (Πνεῦμα Ἅγιον) - Holy Spirit
**Roles:** Convicts of sin, regenerates, indwells, empowers, teaches, intercedes
**Significance:** God dwelling in believers; source of spiritual life and power

### Hope
**Meaning:** Confident expectation of future good based on God's promises
**Greek:** Elpis (ἐλπίς) - hope, expectation
**Usage:** Living hope (1 Peter 1:3); hope of glory (Colossians 1:27); blessed hope (Titus 2:13)
**Significance:** Not wishful thinking but confident assurance based on God's faithfulness

## I

### Idol / Idolatry
**Meaning:** False god; image worshiped in place of true God; anything that takes God's rightful place
**Hebrew:** Pesel (פֶּסֶל) - carved image
**Greek:** Eidolon (εἴδωλον) - image, idol
**Usage:** Forbidden (Exodus 20:3-5); includes covetousness (Colossians 3:5)
**Significance:** Spiritual adultery; giving worship due only to God to created things

### Immanuel
**Meaning:** "God with us"
**Hebrew:** Immanu'el (עִמָּנוּאֵל)
**Usage:** Prophecy of Christ (Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23)
**Significance:** Jesus is God incarnate, dwelling among humanity

### Impute
**Meaning:** To credit, reckon, or attribute to someone's account
**Greek:** Logizomai (λογίζομαι) - to reckon, count, credit
**Usage:** Sin imputed to Christ (Isaiah 53:6); righteousness imputed to believers (Romans 4:3-8; 2 Corinthians 5:19-21)
**Significance:** Legal transaction in justification; Christ's righteousness credited to us

### Incarnation
**Meaning:** God becoming flesh; the Word made flesh in Jesus Christ
**Latin:** From "in" (in) + "caro" (flesh)
**Usage:** John 1:14; Philippians 2:5-8; 1 Timothy 3:16
**Significance:** God became fully human while remaining fully divine

### Inherit / Inheritance
**Meaning:** What is received from another; believers' promised blessings in Christ
**Greek:** Kleronomia (κληρονομία) - inheritance, possession
**Usage:** Inherit eternal life (Matthew 19:29); inheritance in Christ (Ephesians 1:11, 14; Colossians 1:12; 1 Peter 1:4)
**Significance:** Everything God has promised and provided for His children

### Intercession
**Meaning:** Prayer on behalf of others; pleading another's cause
**Greek:** Entugchano (ἐντυγχάνω) - to intercede, make petition
**Usage:** Holy Spirit intercedes (Romans 8:26-27); Christ intercedes (Romans 8:34; Hebrews 7:25)
**Significance:** Both Christ and the Spirit actively pray for believers

## J

### Jerusalem
**Meaning:** "City of peace" or "foundation of peace"; holy city
**Hebrew:** Yerushalayim (יְרוּשָׁלַיִם)
**Significance:** City of David; location of temple; where Christ died and rose; New Jerusalem in Revelation 21

### Jehovah
**Meaning:** English rendering of God's personal covenant name (YHWH)
**Hebrew:** YHWH (יהוה) - usually rendered LORD in all capitals
**Meaning:** "I AM WHO I AM" - the self-existent, eternal God
**Significance:** God's personal, covenant name revealed to Moses (Exodus 3:14-15)

### Judgment
**Meaning:** God's evaluation and verdict; divine justice executed
**Greek:** Krisis (κρίσις) - judgment, decision, justice
**Usage:** Present judgment on sin; future final judgment (John 5:22-29; Romans 2:5; Hebrews 9:27; Revelation 20:11-15)
**Significance:** God is just judge; all will give account

### Justification
**Meaning:** God's declaration that a sinner is righteous through faith in Christ
**Greek:** Dikaiosis (δικαίωσις) - justification, vindication
**Usage:** Justified by faith (Romans 3:28; 5:1; Galatians 2:16)
**Significance:** Legal declaration, not moral transformation (that's sanctification); based on Christ's righteousness

---

## K

### Kingdom of God/Heaven
**Meaning:** God's sovereign rule and reign; realm where God's will is done
**Usage:** "Kingdom of heaven" (Matthew); "Kingdom of God" (Mark, Luke, John)
**Significance:** Already present in Christ; fully realized in future; central theme of Jesus' teaching

### Koinonia (G - κοινωνία)
**Meaning:** Fellowship, communion, partnership, sharing in common
**Usage:** Fellowship with God and believers (Acts 2:42; 1 John 1:3, 6-7); sharing resources (Romans 15:26; 2 Corinthians 8:4)
**Significance:** Essential aspect of Christian community and shared life

---

## L

### Lamb of God
**Meaning:** Title of Jesus; the perfect sacrifice for sin
**Usage:** John 1:29, 36; throughout Revelation
**Significance:** Jesus fulfills Passover lamb (Exodus 12) and Isaiah's suffering servant (Isaiah 53)

### Law
**Meaning:** God's revealed will; specifically the Torah (first five books); commandments
**Hebrew:** Torah (תּוֹרָה) - instruction, teaching, law
**Greek:** Nomos (νόμος) - law
**Usage:** Moral, ceremonial, and civil law of Moses; principle or rule
**Significance:** Reveals God's character and our sin; cannot save but points to Christ (Galatians 3:24)

### Lord
**Meaning:** Master, owner, one with authority
**Hebrew:** Adonai (אֲדֹנָי) - Lord, master (used for God)
**Greek:** Kurios (κύριος) - lord, master, sir
**Usage:** Title for God (LORD = YHWH); title for Jesus as God and Master
**Significance:** Confessing Jesus as Lord is essential to salvation (Romans 10:9)

### Love
See also **Agape**
**Greek Words:**
- Agape (ἀγάπη) - unconditional, sacrificial love
- Phileo (φιλέω) - brotherly love, affection, friendship
- Storge - family love (not used in NT)
- Eros - romantic love (not used in NT)
**Significance:** Agape is God's kind of love; what believers are to show

---

## M

### Mediator
**Meaning:** One who goes between two parties to reconcile them
**Greek:** Mesites (μεσίτης) - mediator, go-between
**Usage:** Moses mediated old covenant; Christ mediates new covenant (1 Timothy 2:5; Hebrews 8:6; 9:15; 12:24)
**Significance:** Jesus is the only mediator between God and humanity

### Mercy
**Meaning:** Compassion toward those in need; withholding deserved punishment
**Hebrew:** Racham (רָחַם) - to have compassion, show mercy
**Greek:** Eleos (ἔλεος) - mercy, compassion, pity
**Usage:** God is rich in mercy (Ephesians 2:4); we receive mercy (Hebrews 4:16); show mercy (Matthew 5:7)
**Significance:** God's compassion toward undeserving sinners

### Messiah
**Meaning:** Anointed One; the promised deliverer
**Hebrew:** Mashiach (מָשִׁיחַ) - anointed
**Greek:** Christos (Χριστός) - Christ, Anointed One
**Usage:** OT promised Messiah; Jesus is the Messiah
**Significance:** Jesus is the fulfillment of all messianic prophecies

### Minister / Ministry
**Meaning:** To serve; service rendered to God or others
**Greek:** Diakoneo (διακονέω) - to serve, minister
**Usage:** All believers have ministry (2 Corinthians 5:18; Ephesians 4:12); specific offices
**Significance:** Christian life is service to God and others

---

## N

### New Covenant
**Meaning:** The covenant established by Christ's blood; replaces old covenant
**Prophecy:** Jeremiah 31:31-34
**Inauguration:** Luke 22:20; Hebrews 8:6-13
**Significance:** Written on hearts, not stone; offers complete forgiveness; better promises

### New Testament
**Meaning:** The second part of the Bible; "testament" means covenant
**Greek:** Kaine Diatheke (Καινὴ Διαθήκη) - New Covenant
**Content:** 27 books; Gospels, Acts, Epistles, Revelation
**Significance:** Records Christ's life, early church, and Christian doctrine

---

## O

### Old Testament
**Meaning:** The first part of the Bible; the old covenant
**Hebrew:** Tanakh (תַּנַ״ךְ) - Torah, Nevi'im (Prophets), Ketuvim (Writings)
**Content:** 39 books; Law, Historical, Wisdom, Prophets
**Significance:** Prepares for and points to Christ; God's covenant with Israel

### Omnipotent
**Meaning:** All-powerful; having unlimited power
**Usage:** Applied to God (Genesis 17:1; Revelation 19:6)
**Significance:** God can do all things; nothing is impossible for Him

### Omnipresent
**Meaning:** Present everywhere at once
**Usage:** God is everywhere (Psalm 139:7-10; Jeremiah 23:24)
**Significance:** Cannot escape or hide from God

### Omniscient
**Meaning:** All-knowing; having complete knowledge
**Usage:** God knows all things (Psalm 139:1-6; 1 John 3:20)
**Significance:** Nothing hidden from God; He knows past, present, future

---

## P

### Parable
**Meaning:** Short story with spiritual lesson; comparison
**Greek:** Parabole (παραβολή) - comparison, illustration
**Usage:** Jesus taught in parables (Matthew 13; Luke 15)
**Significance:** Reveals truth to receptive hearts; conceals from hard hearts

### Pentecost
**Meaning:** Feast of Weeks; day Holy Spirit came upon the church
**Greek:** Pentekoste (πεντηκοστή) - fiftieth day
**OT:** Harvest festival 50 days after Passover (Leviticus 23:15-21)
**NT:** Holy Spirit given (Acts 2)
**Significance:** Birthday of the church; Spirit empowers for witness

### Pharisee
**Meaning:** Jewish religious party emphasizing strict observance of the law and traditions
**Hebrew:** Perushim (פְּרוּשִׁים) - separated ones
**Significance:** Often opposed Jesus; Jesus condemned their hypocrisy and self-righteousness

### Predestination
**Meaning:** God's predetermined plan; foreordained
**Greek:** Proorizo (προορίζω) - to predetermine, foreordain
**Usage:** Romans 8:29-30; Ephesians 1:5, 11
**Significance:** God sovereignly determined His plan before creation

### Priest
**Meaning:** One who represents people before God; offers sacrifices
**Hebrew:** Kohen (כֹּהֵן) - priest
**Greek:** Hiereus (ἱερεύς) - priest
**Usage:** OT priesthood; Christ our High Priest (Hebrews 4:14-16); believers are priests (1 Peter 2:9)
**Significance:** Christ is perfect High Priest; believers have direct access to God

### Prophet
**Meaning:** One who speaks God's message; God's spokesperson
**Hebrew:** Navi (נָבִיא) - prophet
**Greek:** Prophetes (προφήτης) - prophet, one who speaks forth
**Usage:** OT prophets; Christ as prophet; NT gift of prophecy
**Significance:** Spoke God's Word; foretold future; called people to faithfulness

### Propitiation
**Meaning:** Sacrifice that appeases God's wrath and satisfies His justice
**Greek:** Hilasmos (ἱλασμός) - atoning sacrifice, propitiation
**Usage:** Romans 3:25; 1 John 2:2; 4:10
**Significance:** Christ's death satisfied God's just wrath against sin

---

## R

### Ransom
**Meaning:** Price paid to free a captive; redemption price
**Greek:** Lutron (λύτρον) - ransom, price of release
**Usage:** Christ gave His life as ransom (Matthew 20:28; Mark 10:45; 1 Timothy 2:6)
**Significance:** We were slaves to sin; Christ paid the price to free us

### Reconciliation
**Meaning:** Restoration of friendly relationship; peace between enemies
**Greek:** Katallage (καταλλαγή) - reconciliation, restoration
**Usage:** 2 Corinthians 5:18-20; Romans 5:10-11; Ephesians 2:16; Colossians 1:20-21
**Significance:** Christ's death reconciles us to God; we become ambassadors of reconciliation

### Redemption
**Meaning:** Deliverance by payment of a price; being bought back
**Hebrew:** Ga'al (גָּאַל) - to redeem, act as kinsman-redeemer
**Greek:** Apolutrosis (ἀπολύτρωσις) - redemption, release
**Usage:** Ephesians 1:7; Colossians 1:14; Hebrews 9:12
**Significance:** Christ redeemed us from slavery to sin by His blood

### Regeneration
**Meaning:** New birth; spiritual rebirth by the Holy Spirit
**Greek:** Palingenesia (παλιγγενεσία) - rebirth, renewal
**Usage:** Matthew 19:28 (cosmic renewal); Titus 3:5 (individual new birth)
**Significance:** The Spirit gives new spiritual life; born again

### Repentance
**Meaning:** Change of mind leading to change of action; turning from sin to God
**Hebrew:** Shub (שׁוּב) - to turn back, return
**Greek:** Metanoia (μετάνοια) - change of mind, repentance
**Usage:** Required for salvation (Acts 2:38; 3:19; 17:30; 2 Corinthians 7:10)
**Significance:** Essential element of conversion; turning from sin to God

### Resurrection
**Meaning:** Rising from the dead; being raised to life
**Greek:** Anastasis (ἀνάστασις) - resurrection, rising up
**Usage:** Christ's resurrection (1 Corinthians 15:3-4); believers' resurrection (1 Thessalonians 4:16; 1 Corinthians 15:51-52)
**Significance:** Central to Christian faith; proves Christ's victory; guarantees our resurrection

### Revelation
**Meaning:** God's disclosure of Himself and His will to humanity
**Greek:** Apokalupsis (ἀποκάλυψις) - unveiling, revelation
**Types:** General (creation) and special (Scripture, Christ)
**Significance:** We know God because He revealed Himself

### Righteousness
**Meaning:** Right standing before God; moral perfection; uprightness
**Hebrew:** Tsedaqah (צְדָקָה) - righteousness, justice
**Greek:** Dikaiosune (δικαιοσύνη) - righteousness, justice
**Usage:** God's righteousness; righteousness imputed to believers (Romans 3:21-22; 2 Corinthians 5:21)
**Significance:** We receive Christ's righteousness by faith

---

## S

### Sabbath
**Meaning:** Seventh day of rest; cessation from work
**Hebrew:** Shabbat (שַׁבָּת) - rest, cessation
**Usage:** Creation pattern (Genesis 2:2-3); fourth commandment (Exodus 20:8-11); fulfilled in Christ (Colossians 2:16-17)
**Significance:** Points to rest in Christ; principle of rest

### Sacrifice
**Meaning:** Offering made to God; something given up for a greater purpose
**Hebrew:** Zebach (זֶבַח) - sacrifice, offering
**Usage:** OT animal sacrifices; Christ's perfect sacrifice (Hebrews 10:10-12); living sacrifice (Romans 12:1)
**Significance:** Foreshadowed Christ; His death was final sacrifice

### Salvation
**Meaning:** Deliverance from sin and its consequences; being saved
**Hebrew:** Yesha (יֶשַׁע) / Yeshuah (יְשׁוּעָה) - salvation, deliverance
**Greek:** Soteria (σωτηρία) - salvation, deliverance, preservation
**Aspects:** Justification (saved from penalty); sanctification (saved from power); glorification (saved from presence)
**Significance:** Complete deliverance by God's grace through faith in Christ

### Sanctification
**Meaning:** Process of being made holy; set apart for God
**Greek:** Hagiasmos (ἁγιασμός) - sanctification, holiness
**Usage:** 1 Thessalonians 4:3; Hebrews 12:14; 1 Peter 1:2
**Types:** Positional (set apart at salvation); progressive (ongoing growth); complete (glorification)
**Significance:** God's work of making us holy; involves our cooperation

### Satan
**Meaning:** The adversary; the devil
**Hebrew:** Satan (שָׂטָן) - adversary, accuser
**Greek:** Satanas (Σατανᾶς) / Diabolos (Διάβολος - devil, slanderer)
**Significance:** Fallen angel; enemy of God and humanity; defeated by Christ

### Savior
**Meaning:** One who saves; deliverer
**Hebrew:** Moshia (מוֹשִׁיעַ) - savior, deliverer
**Greek:** Soter (σωτήρ) - savior, deliverer
**Usage:** Applied to God; applied to Jesus Christ
**Significance:** Jesus is the Savior who delivers from sin

### Scripture
**Meaning:** Sacred writings; the Bible
**Greek:** Graphe (γραφή) - writing, Scripture
**Usage:** Old Testament writings; all inspired writings (2 Timothy 3:16)
**Significance:** God's inspired, authoritative Word

### Seal
**Meaning:** Mark of ownership, authenticity, or security
**Greek:** Sphragis (σφραγίς) - seal, signet
**Usage:** Holy Spirit is seal (2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 1:13; 4:30)
**Significance:** Believers are marked as God's possession; guaranteed inheritance

### Sin
**Meaning:** Missing the mark; transgression of God's law; lawlessness
**Hebrew:** Chata (חָטָא) - to miss, sin
**Greek:** Hamartia (ἁμαρτία) - missing the mark, sin
**Usage:** Universal problem (Romans 3:23); what Christ died for (1 Corinthians 15:3)
**Significance:** Separates from God; brings death; requires atonement

### Son of God
**Meaning:** Title emphasizing Jesus' divine nature and relationship to the Father
**Usage:** Throughout Gospels and epistles
**Significance:** Declares Jesus' deity; second person of Trinity

### Son of Man
**Meaning:** Jesus' favorite self-designation; emphasizes humanity and messianic role
**Hebrew:** Ben Adam (בֶּן־אָדָם)
**Greek:** Huios tou Anthropou (Υἱὸς τοῦ Ἀνθρώπου)
**Background:** Daniel 7:13-14
**Significance:** Emphasizes Jesus' humanity and His role as Messiah

### Soul
**Meaning:** The immaterial part of a person; life; inner being
**Hebrew:** Nephesh (נֶפֶשׁ) - soul, life, person
**Greek:** Psuche (ψυχή) - soul, life
**Usage:** Living being; seat of emotions and will
**Significance:** Eternal; can be saved or lost

### Spirit
**Meaning:** Immaterial being; breath; wind; the Holy Spirit
**Hebrew:** Ruach (רוּחַ) - spirit, breath, wind
**Greek:** Pneuma (πνεῦμα) - spirit, breath, wind
**Usage:** Holy Spirit; human spirit; spiritual beings
**Significance:** The immaterial aspect of reality; God is Spirit

---

## T

### Tabernacle
**Meaning:** Tent of meeting; portable sanctuary where God dwelt with Israel
**Hebrew:** Mishkan (מִשְׁכָּן) - dwelling place
**Usage:** Constructed in wilderness (Exodus 25-40); foreshadowed Christ (John 1:14 - "tabernacled" among us)
**Significance:** God's presence with His people; type of Christ

### Temple
**Meaning:** Permanent structure for worship; house of God
**Hebrew:** Hekal (הֵיכָל) / Bayit (בַּיִת - house)
**Greek:** Naos (ναός) - temple, sanctuary
**Usage:** Solomon's temple; second temple; believers are temple (1 Corinthians 3:16; 6:19)
**Significance:** God's dwelling place; fulfilled in Christ and believers

### Temptation
**Meaning:** Enticement to sin; testing
**Greek:** Peirasmos (πειρασμός) - trial, temptation, testing
**Usage:** Testing from God (strengthens); temptation from Satan/flesh (entices to sin)
**Significance:** God provides way of escape (1 Corinthians 10:13); Christ was tempted (Hebrews 4:15)

### Testament
**Meaning:** Covenant; will
**Greek:** Diatheke (διαθήκη) - covenant, will, testament
**Usage:** Old and New Testaments; Christ's will (Hebrews 9:16-17)
**Significance:** Covenant relationship between God and His people

### Testimony
**Meaning:** Witness; declaration of truth
**Greek:** Marturia (μαρτυρία) - testimony, witness
**Usage:** Witness about Christ; our testimony
**Significance:** Believers testify to what God has done

### Transgression
**Meaning:** Crossing a boundary; violation of law; trespass
**Hebrew:** Pesha (פֶּשַׁע) - transgression, rebellion
**Greek:** Parabasis (παράβασις) - transgression, violation
**Usage:** Sin as willful violation of known law
**Significance:** Active rebellion against God's commands

### Tribulation
**Meaning:** Affliction; distress; suffering
**Greek:** Thlipsis (θλίψις) - tribulation, affliction, distress
**Usage:** General suffering (John 16:33); great tribulation (Matthew 24:21; Revelation 7:14)
**Significance:** Expected in Christian life; God gives grace to endure

### Trinity
**Meaning:** The doctrine that God exists as three persons in one essence
**Latin:** From "tri" (three) + "unity"
**Biblical basis:** Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14; multiple passages showing Father, Son, Spirit as God
**Significance:** One God in three persons: Father, Son, Holy Spirit

### Truth
**Meaning:** That which corresponds to reality; God's revelation
**Hebrew:** Emet (אֱמֶת) - truth, faithfulness
**Greek:** Aletheia (ἀλήθεια) - truth, reality
**Usage:** God is truth; Christ is truth (John 14:6); Word is truth (John 17:17)
**Significance:** God's Word is absolute truth; Jesus embodied truth

---

## V

### Virgin Birth
**Meaning:** Jesus was born of a virgin (Mary) by the power of the Holy Spirit
**Prophecy:** Isaiah 7:14
**Fulfillment:** Matthew 1:18-25; Luke 1:26-38
**Significance:** Miraculous conception; Jesus is fully human and fully divine; born without sin

---

## W

### Will of God
**Meaning:** God's sovereign plan; what God desires
**Greek:** Thelema (θέλημα) - will, desire
**Types:** Sovereign will (always happens); moral will (commands in Scripture); individual will (guidance for decisions)
**Significance:** Seek and do God's will (Romans 12:2; Ephesians 5:17)

### Wisdom
**Meaning:** Skill in living; knowledge applied correctly
**Hebrew:** Chokmah (חָכְמָה) - wisdom, skill
**Greek:** Sophia (σοφία) - wisdom
**Usage:** Fear of LORD is beginning (Proverbs 9:10); Christ our wisdom (1 Corinthians 1:30); ask God (James 1:5)
**Significance:** Practical application of knowledge; gift from God

### Witness
**Meaning:** One who testifies; testimony about what was seen or heard
**Greek:** Martus (μάρτυς) - witness (root of "martyr")
**Usage:** Witness of Christ's resurrection; believers are witnesses (Acts 1:8)
**Significance:** Christians testify to Christ; word became "martyr" because early witnesses died for faith

### Word
**Meaning:** God's revelation; Scripture; Jesus Christ
**Hebrew:** Dabar (דָּבָר) - word, matter, thing
**Greek:** Logos (λόγος) - word, reason, message
**Usage:** Scripture is Word of God; Christ is the Word (John 1:1, 14)
**Significance:** God reveals Himself through His Word—Scripture and Christ

### Works
**Meaning:** Actions; deeds; things done
**Greek:** Ergon (ἔργον) - work, deed, action
**Usage:** Works cannot save (Ephesians 2:9); faith produces works (James 2:17); judged by works (Revelation 20:12-13)
**Significance:** Not the basis of salvation but evidence and fruit of faith

### Worship
**Meaning:** Honor and adoration given to God; ascribing worth
**Hebrew:** Shachah (שָׁחָה) - to bow down, worship
**Greek:** Proskuneo (προσκυνέω) - to worship, bow down
**Usage:** Due to God alone; in spirit and truth (John 4:23-24)
**Significance:** Central purpose of humanity; giving God His due honor

### Wrath
**Meaning:** God's righteous anger against sin
**Hebrew:** Aph (אַף) - anger, wrath (lit. "nose")
**Greek:** Orge (ὀργή) - wrath, anger
**Usage:** God's wrath revealed (Romans 1:18); saved from wrath (1 Thessalonians 1:10; 5:9)
**Significance:** God's holy reaction to sin; Christ bore God's wrath for us

---

## Y

### Yahweh (H - יהוה)
**Meaning:** God's personal covenant name; "I AM WHO I AM"
**Usually rendered:** LORD (all capitals) in English Bibles
**Revealed:** Exodus 3:14-15
**Significance:** The self-existent, eternal, covenant-keeping God

---

## Z

### Zion
**Meaning:** Jerusalem; City of David; God's dwelling place; His people
**Hebrew:** Tsiyon (צִיּוֹן)
**Usage:** Physical Jerusalem; spiritual community of believers; heavenly Jerusalem
**Significance:** Represents God's people and His reign

## Study Tips for Using This Glossary

1. **Look up unfamiliar terms** as you read Scripture
2. **Cross-reference** - see how terms relate to each other
3. **Study original languages** - understand nuances of Hebrew and Greek
4. **Read verses in context** - don't just memorize definitions
5. **Compare usages** - see how terms are used in different passages
6. **Build theological understanding** - connect doctrines together
7. **Apply to life** - let understanding lead to transformation
