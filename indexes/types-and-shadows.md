# Types and Shadows of Christ

Christ in the Old Testament through typology

---

## What Are Types and Shadows?

**"These are a shadow of the things that were to come; the reality, however, is found in Christ." - Colossians 2:17**

### Definitions

**Type:** A person, event, or institution in the Old Testament that foreshadows or prefigures something greater in the New Testament, especially Christ.

**Antitype:** The fulfillment or greater reality that the type points to (usually Christ).

**Shadow:** An Old Testament reality that points forward to the full revelation in Christ.

### How Typology Works

- **Designed by God:** Types were intentionally woven into Israel's history by God
- **Historical:** The type is a real historical person/event, not allegory
- **Prophetic:** Points forward to something greater
- **Fulfilled in Christ:** Jesus is the ultimate fulfillment

**The Old Testament is full of Christ!**

**Luke 24:27** - "And beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, he explained to them what was said in all the Scriptures concerning himself."

**John 5:39** - "These are the very Scriptures that testify about me."

## Biblical Persons as Types

### Adam - The First Man

**Type:** Romans 5:14 - "Adam... is a pattern of the one to come"

**Comparisons:**

| Adam (First Adam) | Christ (Last Adam) |
|------------------|-------------------|
| First man | Last Adam (1 Cor 15:45) |
| Brought sin and death | Brought righteousness and life |
| By one man's disobedience, many died | By one man's obedience, many live (Rom 5:19) |
| Federal head of fallen humanity | Federal head of redeemed humanity |
| Earthly | Heavenly (1 Cor 15:47) |

**Key Verses:**
- **1 Corinthians 15:22** - "For as in Adam all die, so in Christ all will be made alive"
- **1 Corinthians 15:45** - "The first man Adam became a living being; the last Adam, a life-giving spirit"

**Application:** Everyone is either "in Adam" (condemned) or "in Christ" (saved).

### Melchizedek - Priest and King

**Type:** Hebrews 7:3 - "Like the Son of God, he remains a priest forever"

**Who was Melchizedek?**
- King of Salem (Jerusalem)
- Priest of God Most High
- Blessed Abraham (Genesis 14:18-20)
- Received tithes from Abraham

**How He Points to Christ:**

| Melchizedek | Christ |
|-------------|--------|
| King of Salem (Peace) | Prince of Peace (Isa 9:6) |
| King of Righteousness (meaning of name) | Our Righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) |
| Both king and priest | King and High Priest |
| No recorded genealogy | Eternal Son |
| Greater than Abraham | Greater than all |
| Without recorded beginning or end | Eternal (Heb 7:3) |

**Key Verses:**
- **Psalm 110:4** - "You are a priest forever, in the order of Melchizedek"
- **Hebrews 7:17** - "You are a priest forever, in the order of Melchizedek"

**Significance:** Christ's priesthood is superior to the Levitical priesthood. He's both King and Priest, like Melchizedek.

### Moses - Prophet and Deliverer

**Type:** Deuteronomy 18:15 - "The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me"

**Parallels:**

| Moses | Christ |
|-------|--------|
| Escaped death as infant (Exodus 2) | Escaped Herod's massacre (Matt 2) |
| Delivered Israel from Egypt | Delivers from sin |
| Mediator of old covenant | Mediator of new covenant (Heb 9:15) |
| Gave the Law | Fulfilled the Law |
| Fed with manna | Bread of Life (John 6) |
| Brought water from rock | Living Water |
| Lifted up serpent | Lifted up on cross (John 3:14) |
| Prophet to Israel | Prophet to the world |
| Interceded for sinful Israel | Intercedes for us (Heb 7:25) |
| Led people to Promised Land | Leads us to heaven |

**Key Verses:**
- **Acts 3:22** - "Moses said, 'The Lord your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your own people'"
- **John 1:17** - "For the law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ"

**Christ is Greater:**
- **Hebrews 3:3** - "Jesus has been found worthy of greater honor than Moses"
- Moses was faithful servant in God's house; Christ is the Son over God's house

### David - The King

**Type:** Jesus is the "Son of David," the ultimate King

**Parallels:**

| David | Christ |
|-------|--------|
| Shepherd | Good Shepherd (John 10:11) |
| Chosen and anointed | The Anointed One (Messiah/Christ) |
| Defeated Goliath | Defeated Satan |
| King over Israel | King of kings |
| From Bethlehem | Born in Bethlehem (Micah 5:2; Matt 2) |
| Man after God's heart | Beloved Son |
| Suffered before reigning | Suffered before glory |
| Psalms point to Messiah | Many Psalms are Messianic |
| Established kingdom | Establishes eternal kingdom |

**Messianic Promises:**
- **2 Samuel 7:12-13** - "I will raise up your offspring... and I will establish his kingdom forever"
- **Psalm 89:3-4** - "I will establish your line forever"

**Key Verse:**
- **Matthew 1:1** - "Jesus the Messiah the son of David"
- **Revelation 22:16** - "I am the Root and the Offspring of David"

### Joseph - The Rejected Savior

**Type:** Joseph's life remarkably foreshadows Christ

**Parallels:**

| Joseph | Christ |
|-------|--------|
| Father's beloved son (Gen 37:3) | Father's beloved Son (Matt 3:17) |
| Hated by brothers (37:4) | Rejected by His own (John 1:11) |
| Plotted against (37:18) | Plotted against (Matt 26:3-4) |
| Sold for silver (37:28) | Sold for 30 pieces of silver (Matt 26:15) |
| Tempted but stayed pure (39:7-12) | Tempted but without sin (Heb 4:15) |
| Falsely accused (39:14-18) | Falsely accused (Matt 26:59-61) |
| Suffered unjustly | Suffered for our sins |
| Placed with two criminals (40:1-3) | Crucified with two criminals (Luke 23:32) |
| One saved, one condemned (40:20-22) | One thief saved, one condemned (Luke 23:39-43) |
| Exalted after suffering (41:40-41) | Exalted after resurrection (Phil 2:9) |
| Saved his family and nations | Saves Jews and Gentiles |
| Revealed himself to brothers (45:1-3) | Will reveal Himself to Israel (Zech 12:10) |
| Forgave those who betrayed him (50:20) | "Father, forgive them" (Luke 23:34) |
| Provided bread in famine | Bread of Life |

**Key Verse:**
- **Genesis 50:20** - "You intended to harm me, but God intended it for good to accomplish what is now being done, the saving of many lives"

**Remarkable:** No other biblical character so thoroughly mirrors Christ's life!

### Jonah - Death, Burial, Resurrection

**Type:** Jesus explicitly used Jonah as a sign

**Parallels:**

| Jonah | Christ |
|-------|--------|
| Prophet to Gentiles | Light to Gentiles |
| Three days and nights in fish | Three days and nights in tomb |
| "Resurrection" from fish | Resurrection from dead |
| Preached to Nineveh | Preached to all nations |

**Key Verse:**
- **Matthew 12:40** - "For as Jonah was three days and three nights in the belly of a huge fish, so the Son of Man will be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth"

**Significance:** Jesus Himself validated Jonah as a type, pointing to His death and resurrection.

### Isaac - The Promised Son

**Type:** The sacrifice of Isaac foreshadows Christ's sacrifice

**Parallels:**

| Isaac | Christ |
|-------|--------|
| Miraculous birth to aged parents | Virgin birth |
| Only son, beloved (Gen 22:2) | Only Son, beloved (John 3:16) |
| Son of promise | Fulfillment of promise |
| Carried wood up mountain (22:6) | Carried cross |
| Offered as sacrifice | Sacrificed for us |
| Submitted to father's will (22:9) | "Not my will, but yours" (Luke 22:42) |
| Received back from death (figuratively) | Literally resurrected (Heb 11:19) |
| Father provided a substitute ram | God provided the Lamb (John 1:29) |

**Key Verse:**
- **Genesis 22:8** - "God himself will provide the lamb for the burnt offering"
- **John 1:29** - "Look, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!"

**The Mountain:** Mount Moriah (Gen 22:2) is in the region where Jerusalem and the temple were later built—where Christ was crucified!

## Events as Types

**Type:** 1 Corinthians 5:7 - "Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed"

| Exodus Event | Christ/Salvation |
|-------------|------------------|
| Slavery in Egypt | Slavery to sin |
| Pharaoh | Satan |
| Passover lamb's blood | Christ's blood |
| Death angel passed over | Judgment passes over believers |
| Deliverance through Red Sea | Deliverance through baptism (1 Cor 10:1-2) |
| Manna in wilderness | Bread of Life (John 6:31-35) |
| Water from the rock | Christ, the Rock (1 Cor 10:4) |
| Journey to Promised Land | Journey to heaven |

**Key Verses:**
- **1 Corinthians 10:11** - "These things happened to them as examples and were written down as warnings for us"
- **Exodus 12:13** - "When I see the blood, I will pass over you"

### The Bronze Serpent

**Type:** Jesus explicitly identified this

**Event:** Numbers 21:4-9 - People bitten by snakes; look at bronze serpent on pole to live

**Fulfillment:**
- **John 3:14-15** - "Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up, that everyone who believes may have eternal life in him"

**Parallels:**
- Deadly snake bite = deadly sin
- Bronze serpent lifted up = Christ lifted up on cross
- Look and live = Believe and live
- Simple faith required = Simple faith in Christ

**Key Truth:** Just as Israelites were saved by looking in faith at the serpent, we're saved by looking in faith to Christ crucified.

## Institutions and Rituals

### The Sabbath

**Type:** Colossians 2:16-17 - "These are a shadow of the things to come; the reality, however, is found in Christ"

**Meaning:**
- Seventh-day rest = Our rest in Christ
- **Matthew 11:28** - "Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest"
- **Hebrews 4:9-10** - "There remains, then, a Sabbath-rest for the people of God"

**Fulfillment:** We rest from our works (trying to earn salvation) and rest in Christ's finished work.

### The Feasts

**Quick Summary:**

1. **Passover** → Christ our Passover Lamb (1 Cor 5:7)

## The Tabernacle

**Type:** Hebrews 8:5 - "They serve at a sanctuary that is a copy and shadow of what is in heaven"

### The Structure

#### 1. The Outer Court

**Bronze Altar (Sacrifice)**
- **Type:** Christ's sacrifice on the cross
- Only way into God's presence
- Blood shed here
- **Hebrews 13:10** - "We have an altar from which those who minister at the tabernacle have no right to eat"

**Bronze Laver (Washing)**
- **Type:** Cleansing through God's Word
- Priests washed before serving
- **Ephesians 5:26** - "Cleansing her by the washing with water through the word"
- **John 13:10** - Jesus washing disciples' feet

#### 2. The Holy Place

**Table of Showbread**
- **Type:** Christ, the Bread of Life (John 6:35)
- 12 loaves (12 tribes) always before God
- We are always before God in Christ

**Golden Lampstand (Menorah)**
- **Type:** Christ, the Light of the World (John 8:12)
- Seven branches (perfection)
- Only light in the Holy Place
- Believers are lights (Matt 5:14) only because of Christ

**Altar of Incense**
- **Type:** Prayers of the saints (Rev 8:3-4)
- Christ's intercession (Heb 7:25)
- Sweet-smelling aroma before God
- **Revelation 5:8** - "Golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of God's people"

#### 3. The Most Holy Place (Holy of Holies)

**The Veil**
- **Type:** Christ's flesh (Heb 10:20)
- Separated man from God's presence
- **Matthew 27:51** - Veil torn in two when Jesus died
- Access to God now open!

**The Ark of the Covenant**
- **Type:** Christ's presence
- God's throne/mercy seat
- Inside ark: Law (Christ fulfilled), manna (Bread of Life), Aaron's rod (Resurrection)
- **Hebrews 9:4** lists contents

**The Mercy Seat**
- **Type:** Where God meets with man
- Blood sprinkled here on Day of Atonement
- Atonement = "at-one-ment"
- Christ is our mercy seat (propitiation - Rom 3:25)

**The Cherubim**
- Guarding God's holiness
- Covering the mercy seat
- Looking down at the blood

### The Materials

**Gold:** Deity (divine nature)
**Silver:** Redemption (atonement money - Ex 30:11-16)
**Bronze:** Judgment (bronze altar, laver)
**Blue:** Heavenly
**Purple:** Royalty
**Scarlet:** Blood/sacrifice
**Fine linen:** Righteousness

### The Way In

**Progression:**
1. Through the gate (Christ - John 10:9)
2. To the altar (the cross)
3. Washed (cleansed by Word)
4. Into God's presence (through the veil)
5. Before the throne (bold access - Heb 4:16)

**Key Verse:**
- **Hebrews 10:19-20** - "We have confidence to enter the Most Holy Place by the blood of Jesus, by a new and living way opened for us through the curtain, that is, his body"

## The Sacrificial System

**Type:** Hebrews 10:1 - "The law is only a shadow of the good things that are coming—not the realities themselves"

### The Five Main Offerings (Leviticus 1-7)

| Offering | Reference | Christ Fulfillment |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Burnt Offering** | Lev 1 | Total dedication; Christ's complete obedience |
| **Grain Offering** | Lev 2 | Christ's sinless life offered to God |
| **Peace Offering** | Lev 3 | Fellowship restored; Christ our peace (Eph 2:14) |
| **Sin Offering** | Lev 4 | Christ bore our sin (2 Cor 5:21) |
| **Trespass Offering** | Lev 5-6 | Restitution; Christ paid our debt |

### The Day of Atonement (Yom Kippur)

**Type:** Leviticus 16 - Annual covering of sin

**Two Goats:**

1. **Goat Sacrificed**
   - Blood taken into Most Holy Place
   - Atonement made
   - **Type:** Christ's death

2. **Scapegoat**
   - High priest confessed sins over it
   - Led into wilderness
   - Sins removed
   - **Type:** Christ carrying our sins away (John 1:29)

**Fulfillment:**
- **Hebrews 9:12** - "He entered the Most Holy Place once for all by his own blood, thus obtaining eternal redemption"
- **Hebrews 9:26** - "He has appeared once for all at the culmination of the ages to do away with sin by the sacrifice of himself"
- **Hebrews 10:10** - "We have been made holy through the sacrifice of the body of Jesus Christ once for all"

### Requirements for Sacrifices

**Without blemish (Lev 1:3)**
- **Type:** Christ, the sinless Lamb (1 Pet 1:19)

**Blood must be shed (Lev 17:11)**
- **Type:** Christ's blood (Heb 9:22)

**Life for life**
- **Type:** Christ died in our place (substitutionary atonement)

**Key Truth:** All sacrifices pointed to the one final sacrifice—Christ on the cross.

**Exodus 17:6** - Moses struck the rock, water flowed

**Parallels:**
- Rock struck = Christ struck (crucified)
- Water flowed = Living water (Holy Spirit)
- Quenched thirst = Satisfies spiritual thirst

**Fulfillment:**
- **John 1:51** - "You will see 'heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending on' the Son of Man"

### The Veil

**Type:** Hebrews 10:20 - "The curtain, that is, his body"

**The Temple Veil:**
- Separated Holy Place from Most Holy Place
- No access to God's presence
- Only high priest once a year

**When Christ Died:**
- **Matthew 27:51** - "The curtain of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom"
- Torn from top = God tore it
- Access now open to all believers

**Meaning:** Christ's body was torn so we could enter God's presence.

- **Rahab's scarlet cord** (Josh 2:18) - Salvation

- **Scarlet robe on Jesus** (Matt 27:28) - Mockery turned to truth

**Meaning:** Blood of Christ runs through all of Scripture.

## How to Study Typology

### Rules for Identifying Types

1. **Start with Scripture**
   - If the NT explicitly says something is a type, it is (Rom 5:14; 1 Cor 10:6; Heb 8:5)
   - Don't force types where Scripture doesn't indicate

2. **Look for Correspondence**
   - Real historical person/event
   - Points forward to Christ or salvation
   - Has clear parallels

3. **Christ-Centered**
   - Valid types ultimately point to Christ
   - If it doesn't lead to Jesus, be cautious

4. **Don't Allegorize Everything**
   - Not every detail has meaning
   - Major features, not minute details

5. **Check with Solid Commentaries**
   - Don't create private interpretations
   - Study what Scripture-faithful scholars say

### Questions to Ask

When studying a potential type:
1. Is this explicitly identified as a type in the NT?
2. Does it point to Christ or salvation?
3. What are the clear parallels?
4. How does Christ fulfill this?
5. What does this teach me about Jesus?

### Devotional Use

**Weekly Practice:**
1. Choose one type from this index
2. Read the OT passage
3. Read the NT fulfillment
4. List the parallels
5. Worship Christ for being the reality

**Example: Study the Bronze Serpent**
- Read Numbers 21:4-9
- Read John 3:14-16
- Note: lifted up, look and live, faith required
- Praise Jesus for being lifted up for our salvation

## The Big Picture

### Christ in All Scripture

**Luke 24:44** - "Everything must be fulfilled that is written about me in the Law of Moses, the Prophets and the Psalms."

**Progressive Revelation:**
- Genesis 3:15 - Seed of woman will crush serpent's head
- Throughout OT - Types and shadows multiply
- The Gospels - The Reality appears
- The Epistles - The meaning explained
- Revelation - The Victory consummated

**Every page points to Jesus:**
- Law: Shows our need for Christ
- History: God's plan of redemption unfolding
- Poetry: Worship and wisdom centered on God's Messiah
- Prophets: Future hope in the coming King
- Gospels: Christ revealed
- Acts: Christ proclaimed
- Epistles: Christ explained
- Revelation: Christ reigning

### Why Typology Matters

1. **Proves Divine Authorship**
   - Only God could weave such intricate connections through 1,500 years of writing

2. **Shows Unity of Scripture**
   - One book, one plan, one Savior

3. **Deepens Understanding of Christ**
   - See His glory from multiple angles

4. **Increases Faith**
   - Everything pointed to Him; God's plan is sure

5. **Fuels Worship**
   - When you see Christ everywhere, you worship Him more deeply
