# Miracles of the Bible

A comprehensive catalog of supernatural acts of God throughout Scripture, organized by type and testament.

---

## What Are Miracles?

**Definition:** A miracle is a supernatural act of God that interrupts or supersedes natural laws to accomplish His purposes.

**Purpose of Miracles:**
- Reveal God's character and power
- Authenticate God's messengers (prophets, apostles, Jesus)
- Demonstrate God's compassion and care
- Confirm God's Word
- Bring glory to God
- Point to Jesus as Messiah
- Build faith in God

**Jesus' Miracles Specifically:**
- Proved He is the Messiah (John 20:30-31)
- Fulfilled prophecy (Isaiah 35:5-6; 61:1)
- Demonstrated the kingdom of God breaking into the world
- Revealed God's compassion for suffering humanity
- Showed His authority over creation, disease, demons, and death

# OLD TESTAMENT MIRACLES

## Creation & Judgment Miracles

### Creation of the Universe
**Reference:** Genesis 1:1-2:3
**Description:** God spoke the universe into existence in six days
**Purpose:** Demonstrates God's unlimited power; establishes Him as Creator
**Significance:** Foundation for all biblical theology; God creates ex nihilo (from nothing)

---

### The Flood
**Reference:** Genesis 6-8
**Description:** Worldwide flood destroys all life except Noah, his family, and animals in the ark
**Purpose:** Judgment on sin; preservation of humanity through Noah
**Significance:** Shows God's justice and mercy; foreshadows baptism (1 Peter 3:20-21)

---

### Confusion of Languages at Babel
**Reference:** Genesis 11:1-9
**Description:** God confuses human language, scattering people across the earth
**Purpose:** Judgment on pride; ensures fulfillment of command to fill the earth
**Significance:** Origin of different languages and nations; reversed at Pentecost (Acts 2)

---

### Destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah
**Reference:** Genesis 19:1-29
**Description:** Fire and brimstone rain from heaven, destroying cities
**Purpose:** Judgment on wickedness; demonstration of God's holiness
**Significance:** Example of eternal fire (Jude 7); illustration of final judgment

---

## Exodus & Wilderness Miracles

### The Burning Bush
**Reference:** Exodus 3:1-6
**Description:** Bush burns but isn't consumed; God speaks to Moses from it
**Purpose:** Call Moses to deliver Israel; reveal God's holy presence
**Significance:** God makes Himself known; holy ground

---

### The Ten Plagues on Egypt
**Reference:** Exodus 7-12

1. **Water to Blood** (7:14-24) - Nile turns to blood; fish die
2. **Frogs** (8:1-15) - Frogs cover the land
3. **Gnats/Lice** (8:16-19) - Dust becomes gnats; magicians can't replicate
4. **Flies** (8:20-32) - Swarms of flies; Goshen (Israelites) spared
5. **Plague on Livestock** (9:1-7) - Egyptian livestock die; Israelite livestock spared
6. **Boils** (9:8-12) - Painful boils on Egyptians and animals
7. **Hail** (9:13-35) - Devastating hail; worst in Egypt's history
8. **Locusts** (10:1-20) - Locusts devour everything green
9. **Darkness** (10:21-29) - Thick darkness for three days; light in Goshen
10. **Death of Firstborn** (11:1-12:30) - All firstborn in Egypt die; Passover protects Israel

**Purpose:** Force Pharaoh to release Israel; demonstrate God's power over Egyptian gods
**Significance:** Greatest OT redemptive event; basis for Passover; type of salvation through blood

---

### Parting of the Red Sea
**Reference:** Exodus 14:21-31
**Description:** Waters divide; Israel crosses on dry ground; waters return and drown Egyptian army
**Purpose:** Deliver Israel from Egypt; destroy pursuers
**Significance:** Ultimate demonstration of God's power; baptism imagery (1 Corinthians 10:1-2)

---

### Water from the Rock
**References:** Exodus 17:1-7; Numbers 20:7-11
**Description:** Moses strikes rock; water gushes out for the people and animals
**Purpose:** Provide water in the desert; demonstrate God's provision
**Significance:** Rock represents Christ (1 Corinthians 10:4); source of living water

---

### Manna from Heaven
**Reference:** Exodus 16
**Description:** Bread from heaven appears each morning for 40 years
**Purpose:** Provide daily food in wilderness; teach dependence on God
**Significance:** Type of Christ, the Bread of Life (John 6:31-35)

---

### Quail Provided
**References:** Exodus 16:13; Numbers 11:31-34
**Description:** Quail cover the camp; people gather meat
**Purpose:** Provide meat in wilderness (also with judgment in second instance)
**Significance:** God provides abundantly but judges grumbling

---

### Fire Consumes Nadab and Abihu
**Reference:** Leviticus 10:1-3
**Description:** Aaron's sons offer unauthorized fire; fire from LORD consumes them
**Purpose:** Judgment on irreverent worship; establish holiness standards
**Significance:** God must be approached His way; holiness required in worship

---

### Ground Swallows Korah and Rebels
**Reference:** Numbers 16
**Description:** Earth opens and swallows Korah, Dathan, Abiram, families, and possessions; fire consumes 250 rebels
**Purpose:** Judgment on rebellion against God's appointed leadership
**Significance:** Severe consequences for challenging God's authority

---

### Aaron's Rod Buds
**Reference:** Numbers 17
**Description:** Aaron's staff sprouts, buds, blossoms, and produces almonds overnight
**Purpose:** Confirm God's choice of Aaron and Levites for priesthood; end grumbling
**Significance:** Life from dead wood; God's chosen leadership validated

---

### Bronze Serpent
**Reference:** Numbers 21:4-9
**Description:** Fiery serpents bite people; those who look at bronze serpent on pole live
**Purpose:** Heal snake bites after judgment on grumbling
**Significance:** Type of Christ lifted up (John 3:14-15); healing through looking in faith

---

### Balaam's Donkey Speaks
**Reference:** Numbers 22:21-35
**Description:** Donkey sees angel and speaks to Balaam; Balaam's eyes opened to see angel
**Purpose:** Prevent Balaam from cursing Israel; rebuke the prophet
**Significance:** God can use anything to accomplish His purposes; even animals obey better than people

## Conquest of Canaan

### Jordan River Divides
**Reference:** Joshua 3-4
**Description:** Waters of Jordan pile up; Israel crosses on dry ground while carrying ark
**Purpose:** Enter Promised Land; demonstrate God's presence with Joshua as with Moses
**Significance:** Crossing from wilderness to promise; baptism imagery

---

### Walls of Jericho Fall
**Reference:** Joshua 6
**Description:** After marching around city 7 days, walls collapse at trumpet blast and shout
**Purpose:** Give Israel victory; demonstrate God fights for His people
**Significance:** Victory through obedience and faith, not military might

---

### Sun and Moon Stand Still
**Reference:** Joshua 10:12-14
**Description:** Sun stops in the sky for about a full day during battle
**Purpose:** Give Israel time to defeat enemies completely
**Significance:** Most unique miracle in Scripture; God controls all creation

## Miracles Through Elijah

### Widow's Flour and Oil Multiplied
**Reference:** 1 Kings 17:8-16
**Description:** Flour and oil don't run out during famine
**Purpose:** Provide for widow who showed hospitality to prophet
**Significance:** God provides for those who trust Him; little becomes much

---

### Widow's Son Raised from Dead
**Reference:** 1 Kings 17:17-24
**Description:** Elijah prays; dead boy's life returns
**Purpose:** Demonstrate God's power over death; validate Elijah as prophet
**Significance:** First recorded resurrection in Scripture

---

### Fire from Heaven on Mount Carmel
**Reference:** 1 Kings 18:16-40
**Description:** Fire consumes sacrifice, wood, stones, soil, and water after Elijah's prayer
**Purpose:** Prove YHWH is the true God; defeat Baal prophets
**Significance:** God answers by fire; demonstrates His supremacy

---

### Rain After Drought
**Reference:** 1 Kings 18:41-46
**Description:** Rain comes after 3½ years of drought following Elijah's prayer
**Purpose:** End famine; show God controls weather
**Significance:** God hears and answers fervent prayer (James 5:17-18)

---

### Food Provided by Ravens and Angel
**References:** 1 Kings 17:2-6; 19:4-8
**Description:** Ravens bring bread and meat daily; angel provides food for 40-day journey
**Purpose:** Sustain Elijah during hiding and depression
**Significance:** God provides in unexpected ways; sustains His servants

---

### Fire from Heaven Consumes Soldiers
**Reference:** 2 Kings 1:9-15
**Description:** Fire consumes two groups of 50 soldiers sent to arrest Elijah
**Purpose:** Protect the prophet; judge wicked king Ahaziah
**Significance:** God protects His messengers

---

### Jordan Divided by Elijah
**Reference:** 2 Kings 2:7-8
**Description:** Elijah strikes water with cloak; Jordan divides; both cross on dry ground
**Purpose:** Demonstrate God's power through Elijah before his departure
**Significance:** Similar to Moses' Red Sea miracle; passing the torch to Elisha

---

### Elijah Taken Up to Heaven
**Reference:** 2 Kings 2:11-12
**Description:** Chariot and horses of fire separate Elijah and Elisha; Elijah taken up in whirlwind
**Purpose:** Remove Elijah from earth without death
**Significance:** One of two people who didn't die (Enoch and Elijah); foreshadows resurrection/rapture

---

**Significance:** God judges those who mock His servants

---

**Purpose:** Provide water for armies; bring victory
**Significance:** God provides in impossible circumstances

---

### Widow's Oil Multiplied
**Reference:** 2 Kings 4:1-7
**Description:** Small jar of oil fills many containers; pays widow's debt
**Purpose:** Save widow's sons from slavery; provide for her needs
**Significance:** God provides abundantly from small amount; meets needs

---

### Shunammite Woman's Son Born and Raised
**References:** 2 Kings 4:8-37
**Description:** Barren woman has son as promised; years later, son dies and is raised
**Purpose:** Reward hospitality; demonstrate power over death
**Significance:** God gives life and restores it

---

### Poisonous Stew Made Edible
**Reference:** 2 Kings 4:38-41
**Description:** Elisha puts flour in poisonous stew; it becomes harmless
**Purpose:** Save prophets from poisoned food
**Significance:** God can neutralize danger

---

### 100 Men Fed with 20 Loaves
**Reference:** 2 Kings 4:42-44
**Description:** 100 men eat 20 loaves with some left over
**Purpose:** Feed prophets during famine
**Significance:** Foreshadows Jesus feeding 5,000

---

### Naaman Healed of Leprosy
**Reference:** 2 Kings 5:1-19
**Description:** Syrian commander dips in Jordan 7 times; flesh becomes like child's
**Purpose:** Heal Gentile; show God's power extends beyond Israel
**Significance:** Healing through humble obedience; gospel goes to Gentiles

---

### Gehazi Struck with Leprosy
**Reference:** 2 Kings 5:20-27
**Description:** Elisha's servant greedily takes gifts; struck with Naaman's leprosy
**Purpose:** Judgment on greed and deception
**Significance:** Consequences of sin; can't serve God and money

---

### Axe Head Floats
**Reference:** 2 Kings 6:1-7
**Description:** Borrowed axe head falls in water; floats when Elisha throws stick in
**Purpose:** Help poor prophet recover borrowed tool
**Significance:** God cares about small matters; nothing too trivial for Him

---

### Aramean Army Blinded and Captured
**Reference:** 2 Kings 6:8-23
**Description:** Army sent to capture Elisha struck with blindness; led into Samaria
**Purpose:** Protect Elisha; show God's power to enemy
**Significance:** God protects His servants; spiritual eyes opened to God's protection

---

### Dead Man Raised by Touching Elisha's Bones
**Reference:** 2 Kings 13:20-21
**Description:** Dead man thrown in Elisha's grave; touches bones and comes back to life
**Purpose:** Final miracle through Elisha even after death
**Significance:** God's power not limited by death of His servant

## Other Old Testament Miracles

### Samson's Strength
**References:** Judges 14-16
**Description:** Supernatural strength to kill lion, Philistines; pull down temple pillars
**Purpose:** Deliver Israel from Philistines
**Significance:** God empowers for His purposes; strength from the Spirit

---

### Three Hebrew Men in Fiery Furnace
**Reference:** Daniel 3
**Description:** Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego thrown in furnace; unharmed; fourth man appears
**Purpose:** Vindicate faith; show God's power over fire; convert king
**Significance:** God is with His faithful in persecution; deliverance from fire

---

### Daniel in Lions' Den
**Reference:** Daniel 6
**Description:** Daniel survives night in lions' den; mouth of lions shut
**Purpose:** Protect faithful servant; convert Darius
**Significance:** God protects those who refuse to compromise; shuts lions' mouths (Hebrews 11:33)

---

### Jonah and the Great Fish
**Reference:** Jonah 1-2
**Description:** Great fish swallows Jonah; he survives 3 days and is vomited out
**Purpose:** Preserve Jonah; bring him to repentance; get him to Nineveh
**Significance:** Sign of Christ's death and resurrection (Matthew 12:39-41)

---

### Shadow Moves Backward
**Reference:** 2 Kings 20:8-11; Isaiah 38:7-8
**Description:** Shadow on sundial moves backward 10 steps as sign to Hezekiah
**Purpose:** Confirm God's promise of healing and 15 more years
**Significance:** God controls time and nature; validates His word

---

### Uzzah Struck Dead
**Reference:** 2 Samuel 6:6-7
**Description:** Uzzah touches ark to steady it; God strikes him dead
**Purpose:** Judgment on irreverent handling of holy things
**Significance:** God's holiness requires reverent obedience

# NEW TESTAMENT MIRACLES

## Miracles of Jesus: Healing

### Peter's Mother-in-Law
**References:** Matthew 8:14-15; Mark 1:29-31; Luke 4:38-39
**Description:** Jesus rebukes fever; she's instantly healed and serves them
**Purpose:** Demonstrate authority over disease
**Significance:** Immediate, complete healing; serves after healing

---

### Leper Cleansed
**References:** Matthew 8:1-4; Mark 1:40-45; Luke 5:12-16
**Description:** Jesus touches leper; says "Be clean"; instantly cleansed
**Purpose:** Show compassion; demonstrate power over uncleanness
**Significance:** Jesus touches the "untouchable"; cleansing from defilement

---

### Paralytic Lowered Through Roof
**References:** Matthew 9:1-8; Mark 2:1-12; Luke 5:17-26
**Description:** Friends lower paralyzed man through roof; Jesus forgives and heals him
**Purpose:** Demonstrate authority to forgive sins; heal body and soul
**Significance:** Forgiveness precedes healing; faith of friends

---

### Man at Pool of Bethesda
**Reference:** John 5:1-15
**Description:** Jesus heals man sick 38 years on the Sabbath
**Purpose:** Show compassion; demonstrate He is Lord of Sabbath
**Significance:** "Do you want to be made well?"; stop sinning

---

### Man with Withered Hand
**References:** Matthew 12:9-14; Mark 3:1-6; Luke 6:6-11
**Description:** Jesus heals withered hand on Sabbath in synagogue
**Purpose:** Show it's lawful to do good on Sabbath
**Significance:** Religious leaders plot to kill Him after this

---

### Centurion's Servant
**References:** Matthew 8:5-13; Luke 7:1-10
**Description:** Jesus heals servant from a distance at centurion's request
**Purpose:** Demonstrate healing power; commend Gentile's great faith
**Significance:** Healing without being present; faith greater than in Israel

---

### Widow's Son at Nain Raised
**Reference:** Luke 7:11-17
**Description:** Jesus raises dead son of widow during funeral procession
**Purpose:** Show compassion; demonstrate power over death
**Significance:** Jesus takes initiative without being asked

---

### Two Blind Men
**Reference:** Matthew 9:27-31
**Description:** Two blind men follow Jesus asking for mercy; He touches eyes and heals
**Purpose:** Respond to faith; demonstrate messiahship
**Significance:** "Do you believe I can do this?"

---

### Mute Demoniac
**Reference:** Matthew 9:32-34
**Description:** Jesus casts out demon; mute man speaks
**Purpose:** Demonstrate power over demons
**Significance:** Pharisees attribute power to Beelzebul

---

### Man Blind, Mute, and Demon-Possessed
**References:** Matthew 12:22-23; Luke 11:14
**Description:** Jesus heals blind and mute demoniac
**Purpose:** Demonstrate kingdom power
**Significance:** Leads to blasphemy of Holy Spirit teaching

---

### Syrophoenician Woman's Daughter
**References:** Matthew 15:21-28; Mark 7:24-30
**Description:** Jesus casts demon from Gentile woman's daughter from a distance
**Purpose:** Show gospel extends to Gentiles; commend great faith
**Significance:** Persistent faith; "even dogs eat crumbs"

---

### Deaf and Mute Man
**Reference:** Mark 7:31-37
**Description:** Jesus puts fingers in ears, spits, touches tongue; says "Ephphatha" (be opened)

**Significance:** Fulfilled prophecy: "Ears of deaf unstopped, tongue of mute sing"

---

### Blind Man at Bethsaida
**Reference:** Mark 8:22-26
**Description:** Jesus spits on eyes; lays hands twice; sight restored gradually
**Purpose:** Show power over blindness
**Significance:** Only gradual miracle; second touch completes healing

---

### Man Born Blind
**Reference:** John 9:1-41
**Description:** Jesus spits, makes mud, anoints eyes; man washes in Siloam; sees
**Purpose:** Display works of God; show He is light of the world
**Significance:** Born blind, healed on Sabbath; leads to deep teaching; man believes

---

### Woman with Flow of Blood
**References:** Matthew 9:20-22; Mark 5:25-34; Luke 8:43-48
**Description:** Woman touches Jesus' garment after 12 years of bleeding; instantly healed
**Purpose:** Show power flows from Jesus; respond to faith
**Significance:** "Your faith has made you well"; healed in crowd

### Two Blind Men (Including Bartimaeus)

**Purpose:** Respond to persistent faith; demonstrate compassion

### Blind and Mute Demoniac

### Official's Son at Capernaum

## Miracles of Jesus: Nature

### Water Turned to Wine
**Reference:** John 2:1-11
**Description:** Jesus turns six stone jars of water (120-180 gallons) into wine
**Purpose:** First sign; reveal glory; meet need
**Significance:** Shows authority over creation; abundant provision; better than what came before

---

### First Catch of Fish
**Reference:** Luke 5:1-11
**Description:** After fishing all night with no catch, boats filled to breaking
**Purpose:** Call disciples to follow; teach them about fishing for people
**Significance:** "Leave everything and follow"; partners in gospel

---

### Calming the Storm
**References:** Matthew 8:23-27; Mark 4:35-41; Luke 8:22-25
**Description:** Jesus rebukes wind and waves; immediate calm
**Purpose:** Demonstrate authority over nature; build disciples' faith
**Significance:** "Who is this? Even wind and waves obey Him!"

---

### Feeding 5,000
**References:** Matthew 14:13-21; Mark 6:30-44; Luke 9:10-17; John 6:1-15
**Description:** Five loaves and two fish feed 5,000 men (plus women and children); 12 baskets left over
**Purpose:** Show compassion; demonstrate creative power; point to Bread of Life
**Significance:** Only miracle in all four Gospels; leads to Bread of Life discourse

---

### Walking on Water
**References:** Matthew 14:22-33; Mark 6:45-52; John 6:16-21
**Description:** Jesus walks on water in storm; Peter walks briefly; Jesus calms storm
**Purpose:** Reveal deity; strengthen faith; rescue disciples
**Significance:** "It is I" (ego eimi—I AM); worship: "Truly you are Son of God"

---

### Feeding 4,000
**References:** Matthew 15:32-39; Mark 8:1-10
**Description:** Seven loaves and few fish feed 4,000 men (plus women and children); 7 baskets left
**Purpose:** Show compassion; demonstrate provision
**Significance:** Second mass feeding; to Gentile region

---

### Coin in Fish's Mouth
**Reference:** Matthew 17:24-27
**Description:** Peter catches fish with coin in mouth to pay temple tax
**Purpose:** Teach about freedom; avoid offense; demonstrate knowledge and power
**Significance:** Supernatural provision; Jesus knew what would happen

---

### Fig Tree Withered
**References:** Matthew 21:18-22; Mark 11:12-14, 20-26
**Description:** Jesus curses fruitless fig tree; it withers immediately
**Purpose:** Illustrate judgment on fruitless Israel; teach about faith and prayer
**Significance:** Only destructive miracle; symbolic of judgment

---

### Second Catch of Fish
**Reference:** John 21:1-14
**Description:** After resurrection, disciples catch nothing; Jesus tells them where to cast; net fills (153 fish)
**Purpose:** Restore Peter; demonstrate resurrection body; commission disciples
**Significance:** Bookends ministry (first and last catches); "feed my sheep"

---

**Purpose:** Demonstrate power over death; respond to faith

### Widow's Son at Nain
**Reference:** Luke 7:11-17
(See above in healing section)

### Lazarus
**Reference:** John 11:1-46
**Description:** Lazarus dead 4 days; Jesus weeps; calls him out; he comes out wrapped in grave clothes

## Miracles in Acts

### Lame Beggar Healed at Temple Gate
**Reference:** Acts 3:1-10
**By:** Peter and John
**Description:** Man lame from birth healed in Jesus' name; leaps and walks
**Purpose:** Authenticate apostles; provide platform for preaching; demonstrate Jesus' continued power

---

### Ananias and Sapphira Struck Dead
**Reference:** Acts 5:1-11
**By:** Judgment through Peter's word
**Description:** Couple lies about donation; both struck dead
**Purpose:** Purify church; demonstrate seriousness of sin; warn against lying to Holy Spirit
**Significance:** Great fear comes on church; purity maintained

---

### Shadow Healings
**Reference:** Acts 5:12-16
**By:** Apostles, especially Peter
**Description:** Multitudes healed; even Peter's shadow brought healing
**Purpose:** Authenticate gospel; show God's power through apostles
**Significance:** Extraordinary measures; multitudes added to church

---

### Philip's Miracles in Samaria
**Reference:** Acts 8:6-7
**By:** Philip the evangelist
**Description:** Unclean spirits cast out; paralyzed and lame healed
**Purpose:** Authenticate gospel to Samaritans; bring great joy
**Significance:** Gospel spreads beyond Jews; whole city pays attention

### Saul's Sight Restored
**Reference:** Acts 9:17-19

## Significance of Miracles

### They Reveal God's Character
- **Power:** God is omnipotent; nothing is impossible
- **Compassion:** God cares about human suffering
- **Holiness:** God judges sin; requires purity
- **Faithfulness:** God keeps His promises
- **Sovereignty:** God controls all creation

### They Authenticate God's Messengers
- Moses validated by signs before Pharaoh and Israel
- Elijah and Elisha confirmed as true prophets
- Jesus proved to be Messiah (John 20:30-31; Acts 2:22)
- Apostles confirmed as Christ's representatives (2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:3-4)

### They Point to Greater Spiritual Realities
- Physical healing → spiritual healing/salvation
- Deliverance from bondage → freedom from sin
- Provision of food → Jesus as Bread of Life
- Raising the dead → resurrection and eternal life
- Calming storms → peace with God

### They Build Faith
- Demonstrate God's power and trustworthiness
- Encourage believers in trials
- Call unbelievers to faith
- Strengthen commitment to God

## Questions About Miracles

### Does God Still Do Miracles Today?
- God is unchanging; He can and does work miracles
- Salvation itself is a miracle (2 Corinthians 5:17)
- God heals in answer to prayer (James 5:14-16)
- The greater work is spiritual transformation
- Founding miracles (authenticating apostles and Scripture) were unique
- Our ultimate hope is resurrection and new creation

### Why Doesn't God Always Heal?
- His ways are higher than ours (Isaiah 55:8-9)
- Suffering has purposes in refining faith (James 1:2-4; 1 Peter 1:6-7)
- Not all sickness is due to specific sin
- Paul had a "thorn in flesh" God didn't remove (2 Corinthians 12:7-10)
- Ultimate healing comes in resurrection
- God's power is perfected in weakness

### How Should We Respond to Miracles?
- Glorify God, not the instrument (Acts 3:12-13)
- Let them strengthen faith in Christ
- Share testimony of God's work
- Obey and serve God faithfully
- Don't seek signs and wonders over the Sign-Giver
- Remain humble—it's God's power, not ours
