# Names and Titles of God

A comprehensive guide to how God reveals Himself through His names in Scripture. Each name unveils a facet of His character and invites us to know Him more deeply.

---

## Why Names Matter

In biblical times, names weren't mere labels but revealed character, nature, and purpose. God's names are:
- **Revelations of His character** - Who He is
- **Promises to His people** - What He will do
- **Invitations to relationship** - How we can know Him

**"Those who know your name trust in you, for you, LORD, have never forsaken those who seek you."** - Psalm 9:10

## Names of God the Father

### YHWH / Yahweh (LORD)

**Hebrew:** יהוה (YHWH) - The Tetragrammaton

**Meaning:** "I AM WHO I AM" - The self-existent, eternal God

**First Use:** Exodus 3:14-15 - God reveals His name to Moses at the burning bush

**Significance:** God's personal, covenant name. Most sacred name. Shows He is:
- Self-existent (doesn't depend on anything)
- Eternal (no beginning or end)
- Unchanging (always the same)
- Covenant-keeping (faithful to His promises)

**English Bibles:** Usually rendered as "LORD" (all capitals)

**Key Verses:**
- Exodus 3:14 - "I AM WHO I AM"
- Exodus 6:3 - Revealed specifically to Israel
- Psalm 83:18 - "You alone are the Most High"

**Application:** The God who revealed Himself to Moses is the same yesterday, today, and forever. He is present with you, unchanging, faithful.

---

### Elohim (God)

**Hebrew:** אֱלֹהִים

**Meaning:** "God" or "gods" (plural form, but refers to one God - plural of majesty)

**First Use:** Genesis 1:1 - "In the beginning Elohim created..."

**Significance:**
- Emphasizes God's power and might
- Creator God
- Plural form hints at Trinity
- Used ~2,600 times in OT

**English Bibles:** Translated as "God"

**Key Verses:**
- Genesis 1:1 - Creator
- Genesis 1:26 - "Let us make mankind" (plural)
- Psalm 19:1 - "The heavens declare the glory of Elohim"

**Application:** The almighty Creator of the universe cares about you personally.

---

### Adonai (Lord, Master)

**Hebrew:** אֲדֹנָי

**Meaning:** "Lord," "Master," "Owner"

**Significance:**
- Emphasizes God's sovereignty and lordship
- You belong to Him
- He has authority over you
- Often paired with YHWH as "Adonai YHWH" (Lord GOD)

**English Bibles:** Translated as "Lord" (only first letter capital) or "my Lord"

**Key Verses:**
- Genesis 15:2 - "Adonai GOD, what can you give me...?" (Abraham)
- Psalm 8:1 - "LORD, our Lord, how majestic is your name"
- Isaiah 6:8 - "I heard the voice of the Lord" (Isaiah's calling)

**Application:** Jesus is your Lord and Master. Do you live in submission to His authority?

---

### El (God, Mighty One)

**Hebrew:** אֵל

**Meaning:** "God," "mighty," "strong"

**Significance:**
- Emphasizes God's power and might
- Often combined with other words to form compound names
- Short form, emphasizes strength

**Key Verses:**
- Genesis 14:18 - "El Elyon" (God Most High)
- Deuteronomy 10:17 - "God of gods and Lord of lords"

## Compound Names of God (El/Yahweh + Description)

### El Shaddai (God Almighty)

**Hebrew:** אֵל שַׁדַּי

**Meaning:** "God Almighty" or "God of the Mountains" or "All-Sufficient One"

**First Use:** Genesis 17:1 - God appears to Abraham

**Significance:**
- God is all-powerful
- God is sufficient for all needs
- Nothing is too hard for Him
- Associated with blessing and fruitfulness

**Key Verses:**
- Genesis 17:1 - God's covenant with Abraham
- Genesis 28:3 - Isaac blesses Jacob
- Exodus 6:3 - God known to patriarchs as El Shaddai
- Ruth 1:20-21 - Naomi says "Call me Mara, for Shaddai has dealt bitterly"

**Application:** Whatever you face, God is sufficient. His power is unlimited.

---

### El Elyon (God Most High)

**Hebrew:** אֵל עֶלְיוֹן

**Meaning:** "God Most High," "Exalted One"

**First Use:** Genesis 14:18-20 - Melchizedek blesses Abraham

**Significance:**
- God is sovereign over all
- Above all other gods
- Supreme authority
- Owner of heaven and earth

**Key Verses:**
- Genesis 14:19-20 - "Blessed be Abram by God Most High, Creator of heaven and earth"
- Psalm 91:1 - "He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High"
- Daniel 4:34 - Nebuchadnezzar acknowledges God Most High

**Application:** God is above all earthly powers, authorities, and problems. Nothing is over Him.

---

### El Roi (The God Who Sees)

**Hebrew:** אֵל רֳאִי

**Meaning:** "The God Who Sees Me"

**First Use:** Genesis 16:13 - Hagar names God after He sees her distress

**Significance:**
- God sees your situation
- No one is invisible to God
- He sees the afflicted and oppressed
- He cares about the marginalized

**Key Verse:**
- Genesis 16:13 - "You are the God who sees me, for she said, 'I have now seen the One who sees me'"

**Application:** When you feel unseen, forgotten, or alone, God sees you. He knows your pain.

---

### El Olam (The Everlasting God)

**Hebrew:** אֵל עוֹלָם

**Meaning:** "The Eternal God," "The Everlasting God"

**First Use:** Genesis 21:33 - Abraham plants a tree and calls on El Olam

**Significance:**
- God has no beginning or end
- He transcends time
- His promises last forever
- His love endures forever

**Key Verses:**
- Genesis 21:33 - Abraham worships El Olam
- Isaiah 40:28 - "The LORD is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth. He will not grow tired or weary"

**Application:** In a world of change, God never changes. His promises are eternal.

---

### Yahweh Jireh (The LORD Will Provide)

**Hebrew:** יְהוָה יִרְאֶה

**Meaning:** "The LORD Will Provide" or "The LORD Sees"

**First Use:** Genesis 22:14 - Abraham names the place after God provided the ram

**Significance:**
- God provides for your needs
- Just in time provision
- God sees your need before you ask
- He provided the ultimate sacrifice (Jesus)

**Key Verse:**
- Genesis 22:14 - "Abraham called that place The LORD Will Provide. And to this day it is said, 'On the mountain of the LORD it will be provided'"

**Application:** Trust God to provide. He sees your needs and provides at the right time, often in unexpected ways.

---

### Yahweh Rapha (The LORD Who Heals)

**Hebrew:** יְהוָה רָפָא

**Meaning:** "The LORD Who Heals You"

**First Use:** Exodus 15:26 - After healing the bitter waters of Marah

**Significance:**
- God heals physically, emotionally, spiritually
- He is the Great Physician
- All healing ultimately comes from Him
- He heals the broken hearted

**Key Verse:**
- Exodus 15:26 - "If you listen carefully to the LORD your God... I will not bring on you any of the diseases I brought on the Egyptians, for I am the LORD, who heals you"

**Application:** Bring your brokenness to the Great Physician. He heals all kinds of wounds.

---

### Yahweh Nissi (The LORD is My Banner)

**Hebrew:** יְהוָה נִסִּי

**Meaning:** "The LORD is My Banner"

**First Use:** Exodus 17:15 - After victory over the Amalekites

**Significance:**
- God is your rallying point
- He gives victory in battle
- Fight under His banner
- He leads His people to victory

**Key Verse:**
- Exodus 17:15 - "Moses built an altar and called it The LORD is my Banner"

**Application:** Rally around God. He leads you to victory over your spiritual enemies.

---

### Yahweh M'Kaddesh (The LORD Who Sanctifies)

**Hebrew:** יְהוָה מְקַדֵּשׁ

**Meaning:** "The LORD Who Sanctifies You" or "Makes You Holy"

**First Use:** Leviticus 20:8 - In context of holiness laws

**Significance:**
- God makes you holy
- Sanctification is His work, not yours
- He sets you apart for Himself
- Holiness is a gift and a process

**Key Verses:**
- Leviticus 20:8 - "I am the LORD, who makes you holy"
- Leviticus 21:8 - "I the LORD am holy—I who make you holy"

**Application:** You can't make yourself holy, but God does. Cooperate with His sanctifying work.

---

### Yahweh Shalom (The LORD is Peace)

**Hebrew:** יְהוָה שָׁלוֹם

**Meaning:** "The LORD is Peace"

**First Use:** Judges 6:24 - Gideon builds an altar after encounter with the Angel of the LORD

**Significance:**
- God is your peace
- True peace comes from Him
- Peace despite circumstances
- Wholeness and well-being

**Key Verse:**
- Judges 6:24 - "So Gideon built an altar to the LORD there and called it The LORD Is Peace"

**Application:** In anxiety and chaos, God is your peace. He calms the storm within.

---

### Yahweh Sabaoth (The LORD of Hosts)

**Hebrew:** יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת

**Meaning:** "The LORD of Armies" or "The LORD of Hosts"

**First Use:** 1 Samuel 1:3 - At Shiloh, where Hannah prayed

**Significance:**
- God commands heavenly armies
- Warrior God who fights for His people
- Supreme military commander
- Angels at His command

**Key Verses:**
- 1 Samuel 1:3, 11 - Hannah's prayer
- 1 Samuel 17:45 - David to Goliath: "I come against you in the name of the LORD of Hosts"
- Psalm 46:7, 11 - "The LORD of Hosts is with us"
- Isaiah 6:3 - Seraphim cry "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Hosts"

**Application:** God fights for you. Angelic armies are at His command. You don't fight alone.

---

### Yahweh Roi (The LORD is My Shepherd)

**Hebrew:** יְהוָה רֹעִי

**Meaning:** "The LORD is My Shepherd"

**First Use:** Psalm 23:1

**Significance:**
- God cares for you like a shepherd
- Provides, protects, guides
- Personal care and intimacy
- Knows each sheep by name

**Key Verse:**
- Psalm 23:1 - "The LORD is my shepherd, I lack nothing"

**Application:** Jesus is the Good Shepherd. Follow His voice, trust His care, rest in His provision.

---

### Yahweh Tsidkenu (The LORD Our Righteousness)

**Hebrew:** יְהוָה צִדְקֵנוּ

**Meaning:** "The LORD is Our Righteousness"

**First Use:** Jeremiah 23:6; 33:16 - Messianic prophecy

**Significance:**
- God is righteous
- He provides righteousness (we can't earn it)
- Points to Christ, our righteousness
- Imputed righteousness

**Key Verse:**
- Jeremiah 23:6 - "This is the name by which he will be called: The LORD Our Righteousness"

**Application:** You have no righteousness of your own. Christ is your righteousness. Rest in His, not yours.

---

### Yahweh Shammah (The LORD is There)

**Hebrew:** יְהוָה שָׁמָּה

**Meaning:** "The LORD is There" or "The LORD is Present"

**First Use:** Ezekiel 48:35 - Last words of Ezekiel, describing the restored city

**Significance:**
- God's presence with His people
- Immanuel - God with us
- He never leaves or forsakes
- Ultimate fulfillment in new creation

**Key Verse:**
- Ezekiel 48:35 - "And the name of the city from that time on will be: THE LORD IS THERE"

**Application:** Wherever you are, God is there. You are never alone. His presence is your greatest blessing.

---

### Yahweh Raah (The LORD My Shepherd)

**Hebrew:** יְהוָה רָעָה

**Meaning:** "The LORD Who Shepherds" or "The LORD Who Tends"

**Note:** Similar to Yahweh Roi above

**Key Verse:**
- Psalm 23:1 - "The LORD is my shepherd"

## Other Important Titles

### Abba (Father)

**Aramaic:** אַבָּא

**Meaning:** "Father" (intimate, like "Papa" or "Daddy")

**Significance:**
- Jesus' preferred name for God
- Intimate relationship
- Adoption into God's family
- Access to the Father

**Key Verses:**
- Mark 14:36 - Jesus in Gethsemane: "Abba, Father, everything is possible for you"
- Romans 8:15 - "The Spirit you received brought about your adoption to sonship. And by him we cry, 'Abba, Father'"
- Galatians 4:6 - "God sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, the Spirit who calls out, 'Abba, Father'"

**Application:** You can approach God as your loving Father, not a distant deity.

---

### Ancient of Days

**Reference:** Daniel 7:9, 13, 22

**Meaning:** Eternal, timeless God; wise with age

**Significance:** God predates all creation; He is the eternal judge

**Application:** God has always been and always will be. His wisdom is infinite.

---

### Rock

**Hebrew:** צוּר (Tsur)

**References:** Deuteronomy 32:4; Psalm 18:2; 62:2; Isaiah 26:4

**Meaning:** Solid, unmovable, reliable, stable foundation

**Significance:**
- God is unchanging and dependable
- Safe refuge
- Foundation to build life on

**Application:** Build your life on the solid Rock. He will not shift or fail.

---

### Fortress / Refuge / Shield

**References:** Psalm 18:2; 46:1; 91:2

**Meaning:** Protection, safety, defense

**Significance:** God protects His people from spiritual enemies

**Application:** Run to God for protection. He is your safe place in trouble.

---

### King of Glory

**Reference:** Psalm 24:7-10

**Meaning:** Glorious, majestic King

**Significance:** God is the supreme ruler, worthy of worship

**Application:** Give God the honor and worship He deserves.

---

### Holy One of Israel

**References:** Isaiah 1:4; 5:19, 24; 41:14 (and ~30 times in Isaiah)

**Meaning:** Set apart, pure, morally perfect

**Significance:** God's holiness defines Him; He is separate from sin

**Application:** Approach God with reverence. His holiness should lead you to holiness.

## Names of Jesus Christ

### Jesus (Yeshua/Joshua)

**Hebrew:** יֵשׁוּעַ (Yeshua)

**Meaning:** "The LORD Saves" or "Salvation"

**First Use:** Matthew 1:21 - "You are to give him the name Jesus, because he will save his people from their sins"

**Significance:** His mission stated in His name - to save people from sin

**Application:** Jesus is the Savior. There is no salvation apart from Him.

---

### Christ (Messiah)

**Greek:** Χριστός (Christos)
**Hebrew:** מָשִׁיחַ (Mashiach)

**Meaning:** "Anointed One"

**Significance:**
- The promised Messiah
- Anointed as Prophet, Priest, and King
- Fulfillment of OT prophecies

**Key Verses:**
- Matthew 16:16 - Peter's confession: "You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God"
- John 1:41 - "We have found the Messiah" (that is, the Christ)

**Application:** Jesus is the fulfillment of all God's promises. He is the One we've been waiting for.

---

### Immanuel (God With Us)

**Hebrew:** עִמָּנוּאֵל

**Meaning:** "God With Us"

**Reference:** Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23

**Significance:**
- God became flesh
- God dwells with humanity
- Incarnation

**Application:** God is not distant - He came near in Jesus. He is with you.

---

### Son of God

**References:** Throughout the Gospels

**Meaning:** Divine nature; unique relationship with the Father

**Significance:** Jesus is fully God; equal with the Father

**Key Verses:**
- Matthew 3:17 - At baptism: "This is my Son, whom I love"
- John 1:1, 14 - "The Word was God... became flesh"
- Hebrews 1:3 - "The Son is the radiance of God's glory"

**Application:** Jesus is not just a good teacher - He is God in flesh.

---

### Son of Man

**References:** Used ~80 times by Jesus in the Gospels

**Meaning:** Representative of humanity; messianic title from Daniel 7:13

**Significance:**
- Jesus is fully human
- Identifies with humanity
- Authority to judge

**Key Verses:**
- Mark 10:45 - "The Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many"
- Daniel 7:13-14 - Coming on clouds with authority

**Application:** Jesus understands your humanity. He became like you to save you.

---

### Lamb of God

**Reference:** John 1:29, 36

**Meaning:** Sacrificial lamb; Passover lamb; atoning sacrifice

**Significance:**
- Jesus is the perfect sacrifice for sin
- Fulfills the sacrificial system
- Takes away sin

**Key Verse:**
- John 1:29 - "Look, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!"
- Revelation 5:6-14 - The Lamb is worthy; worshiped in heaven

**Application:** Jesus died in your place. His blood covers your sin.

---

### Good Shepherd

**Reference:** John 10:11, 14

**Meaning:** One who cares for, protects, and guides the sheep

**Significance:**
- Jesus cares for His people personally
- Lays down life for sheep
- Knows each sheep by name

**Key Verse:**
- John 10:11 - "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep"

**Application:** Follow Jesus' voice. He leads you to green pastures and still waters.

---

### Bread of Life

**Reference:** John 6:35, 48

**Meaning:** Essential spiritual food; satisfies deepest hunger

**Significance:** Jesus alone satisfies spiritual hunger

**Key Verse:**
- John 6:35 - "I am the bread of life. Whoever comes to me will never go hungry, and whoever believes in me will never be thirsty"

**Application:** Look to Jesus alone to satisfy your deepest needs.

---

### Light of the World

**Reference:** John 8:12; 9:5

**Meaning:** Illuminates truth; dispels darkness; gives guidance

**Significance:** Jesus reveals truth and gives direction

**Key Verse:**
- John 8:12 - "I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will never walk in darkness, but will have the light of life"

**Application:** Follow Jesus and you'll never walk in darkness. He lights your path.

---

### The Door / The Gate

**Reference:** John 10:7, 9

**Meaning:** Only entrance to salvation; access to God

**Significance:** Jesus is the only way to the Father

**Key Verse:**
- John 10:9 - "I am the gate; whoever enters through me will be saved"

**Application:** There is no other way to God except through Jesus.

---

### The Way, the Truth, and the Life

**Reference:** John 14:6

**Meaning:** The only path to God; ultimate reality; source of life

**Significance:** Exclusive claim - no one comes to Father except through Him

**Application:** Don't seek truth apart from Jesus. He IS truth.

---

### The Resurrection and the Life

**Reference:** John 11:25

**Meaning:** Source of resurrection power and eternal life

**Significance:** Jesus conquered death; gives eternal life

**Key Verse:**
- John 11:25 - "I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me will live, even though they die"

**Application:** Death is not the end for believers. Jesus gives eternal life.

---

### The True Vine

**Reference:** John 15:1, 5

**Meaning:** Source of spiritual life; believers are branches

**Significance:** Abide in Him to bear fruit

**Key Verse:**
- John 15:5 - "I am the vine; you are the branches. If you remain in me and I in you, you will bear much fruit; apart from me you can do nothing"

**Application:** Stay connected to Jesus. All spiritual life flows from Him.

---

### Alpha and Omega

**Reference:** Revelation 1:8; 21:6; 22:13

**Meaning:** First and last letters of Greek alphabet - beginning and end

**Significance:** Jesus is eternal; controls all history

**Application:** Jesus was there at creation and will be there at the end. Trust His plan.

---

### King of Kings and Lord of Lords

**Reference:** Revelation 19:16

**Meaning:** Supreme ruler over all earthly authorities

**Significance:** Jesus has ultimate authority

**Application:** Jesus reigns supreme. Submit to His lordship.

---

### The Word (Logos)

**Reference:** John 1:1, 14

**Meaning:** God's ultimate self-expression and communication

**Significance:** Jesus reveals God perfectly

**Key Verse:**
- John 1:1, 14 - "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God... The Word became flesh"

**Application:** Want to know God? Look at Jesus. He is God's perfect revelation.

---

## Names of the Holy Spirit

### Holy Spirit

**References:** Throughout NT; also OT (ruach elohim)

**Meaning:** The third person of the Trinity; God's presence and power

**Significance:** Indwells believers; empowers for ministry; sanctifies

---

### Spirit of Truth

**Reference:** John 14:17; 15:26; 16:13

**Meaning:** Reveals and teaches truth

**Significance:** Guides believers into all truth

---

### Comforter / Counselor / Advocate (Parakletos)

**Reference:** John 14:16, 26; 15:26; 16:7

**Meaning:** One called alongside to help; advocate; counselor

**Significance:** The Spirit comes alongside to help, teach, comfort

---

### Spirit of God / Spirit of the LORD

**References:** Genesis 1:2; throughout OT and NT

**Meaning:** God's presence and power at work

**Significance:** The Spirit carries out God's will and work

## How to Use God's Names in Prayer and Worship

1. **Call upon His name that matches your need:**
   - Need provision? Pray to Yahweh Jireh
   - Need healing? Pray to Yahweh Rapha
   - Need peace? Pray to Yahweh Shalom
   - Need guidance? Pray to Yahweh Roi (The Shepherd)

2. **Worship Him through His names:**
   - Meditate on what each name reveals
   - Praise Him for who He is
   - Declare His names in worship

3. **Build your faith:**
   - Remember what His names promise
   - Trust His character revealed in His names
   - Stand on His names when you pray

4. **Deepen your relationship:**
   - Each name reveals more of who He is
   - Get to know Him through His names
   - Experience Him personally as each name describes

---

## Study Suggestions

1. **Take one name per week** - Meditate on it, find all references, pray using that name
2. **Create a "Names of God" journal** - Write what each name means to you personally
3. **Memorize key names** - Know who God is and what He promises
4. **Pray Scripture** using God's names - "You are Yahweh Jireh, my provider..."
5. **Search the Bible** for each name and see the context
