# Spiritual Disciplines

Practices for growing in Christlikeness

---

## Introduction

**"Train yourself to be godly. For physical training is of some value, but godliness has value for all things, holding promise for both the present life and the life to come." - 1 Timothy 4:7-8**

Spiritual disciplines are practices that help us grow in our relationship with God and become more like Christ. They're not ways to earn God's favor—we're saved by grace alone. Rather, they're **means of grace**: ways we position ourselves to receive God's transforming work.

**Think of them as spiritual exercises.** Just as physical exercise builds muscle, spiritual disciplines build spiritual strength.

## What Are Spiritual Disciplines?

### Definition

**Spiritual disciplines are intentional practices that create space for God to work in our lives.**

They are:
- **Voluntary** - We choose to do them
- **God-ordained** - Rooted in Scripture
- **Transformative** - Shape us into Christ's image
- **Means, not ends** - The goal is knowing God, not checking boxes

### Why Practice Them?

**1. We Can't Transform Ourselves**
- Only God changes hearts
- But we can put ourselves in position to be changed
- Like planting seeds: we create conditions; God gives growth

**2. Jesus Practiced Them**
- Jesus prayed (Mark 1:35)
- Jesus fasted (Matthew 4:2)
- Jesus sought solitude (Luke 5:16)
- We follow His example

**3. Scripture Commands Some**
- "Pray continually" (1 Thessalonians 5:17)
- "Do not give up meeting together" (Hebrews 10:25)
- "Offer your bodies as a living sacrifice" (Romans 12:1)

**4. They Combat Spiritual Drift**
- We naturally drift from God
- Disciplines keep us anchored
- They're guardrails on the path

### Two Categories

**Disciplines of Abstinence** - Saying "no" to normal activities
- Fasting, solitude, silence, simplicity
- Create space by removing things

**Disciplines of Engagement** - Saying "yes" to spiritual activities
- Prayer, Bible study, worship, fellowship, service
- Fill space with God-honoring practices

Both are necessary.

## The Disciplines

### 1. Prayer

**"Devote yourselves to prayer, being watchful and thankful." - Colossians 4:2**

#### What Is Prayer?

Conversation with God. Talking to Him and listening for His voice.

#### Why Practice It?

- Deepens relationship with God
- Aligns our will with His
- Releases anxiety (Philippians 4:6-7)
- Accesses God's power (James 5:16)
- Jesus modeled it constantly

#### Biblical Examples

- **Jesus:** Early morning prayer (Mark 1:35), all-night prayer (Luke 6:12), Gethsemane (Matthew 26:36-44)
- **Daniel:** Three times daily (Daniel 6:10)
- **Hannah:** Desperate prayer (1 Samuel 1:10-20)
- **Paul:** Constant prayer for churches (Ephesians 1:16; Philippians 1:4)

See [Prayers of the Bible](../indexes/prayers.md) for more examples.

#### How to Practice

**Daily Prayer Time**
- Set specific time (morning recommended)
- Find quiet place
- Start with 10-15 minutes; build from there

**Structure Your Prayer (ACTS Model)**
- **A**doration - Praise God for who He is
- **C**onfession - Admit your sins
- **T**hanksgiving - Thank Him for what He's done
- **S**upplication - Make your requests

**Pray Throughout the Day**
- "Arrow prayers" - quick prayers throughout day (Nehemiah 2:4)
- Pray while driving, walking, working
- "Pray continually" (1 Thessalonians 5:17)

**Prayer List**
- Write down prayer requests
- Track answers
- Pray for: family, church, missionaries, government, lost friends

**Pray Scripture**
- Use Psalms as prayers
- Pray Paul's prayers (Ephesians 1:17-19; 3:16-19; Philippians 1:9-11; Colossians 1:9-12)

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I don't know what to say"** → Use the Lord's Prayer as a template (Matthew 6:9-13)
- **"I get distracted"** → Normal! Gently return focus. Pray aloud to help concentration.
- **"God doesn't answer"** → He always hears. Answer may be yes, no, wait, or different. See [Prayers of the Bible](../indexes/prayers.md) on unanswered prayer.

### 2. Bible Study & Meditation

**"Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path." - Psalm 119:105**

#### What Is It?

- **Bible Study:** Carefully reading and examining Scripture to understand its meaning
- **Meditation:** Thoughtfully reflecting on Scripture, letting it sink deep

#### Why Practice It?

- God speaks through His Word (2 Timothy 3:16)
- Necessary for spiritual growth (1 Peter 2:2)
- Renews the mind (Romans 12:2)
- Equips for every good work (2 Timothy 3:17)
- Protects from error (Acts 17:11)

#### Biblical Examples

- **Ezra:** "devoted himself to the study and observance of the Law" (Ezra 7:10)
- **Bereans:** "examined the Scriptures every day" (Acts 17:11)
- **Blessed man:** Meditates on God's law day and night (Psalm 1:2-3)
- **Joshua:** Told to meditate on the Law (Joshua 1:8)

#### How to Practice

**Daily Bible Reading**
- Set consistent time and place
- Start with 15 minutes
- Use a reading plan (see [Bible Reading Plans](reading-plans.md))
- Begin with Gospel of John if new

**Inductive Bible Study**
1. **Observation** - What does it say?
2. **Interpretation** - What does it mean?
3. **Application** - What should I do?

See [Bible Study Methods](bible-study-methods.md) for detailed guidance.

**Scripture Meditation**
- Choose a short passage (1-5 verses)
- Read slowly multiple times
- Ask:
  - What does this reveal about God?
  - What does it say about me?
  - What should I do?
- Think about it throughout the day
- Memorize key verses

**Scripture Memorization**
- Choose verses that speak to you
- Write them on cards
- Review daily
- Recite aloud
- Hide God's Word in your heart (Psalm 119:11)

**Recommended Tools**
- Study Bible (ESV, NIV, CSB)
- Bible app (YouVersion)
- Blue Letter Bible (online)
- This study guide!

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I don't understand it"** → Start with easier books (John, Philippians). Use study notes. It's okay not to understand everything.
- **"I don't have time"** → Start small (10 min/day). Cut something less important. You find time for what you value.
- **"I get bored"** → Try different translation. Read aloud. Use daily devotional. Vary what you're reading.

### 3. Fasting

**"When you fast, put oil on your head and wash your face, so that it will not be obvious to others that you are fasting, but only to your Father, who is unseen." - Matthew 6:17-18**

#### What Is Fasting?

Voluntarily abstaining from food (or other things) for spiritual purposes. Going without to feast on God.

#### Why Practice It?

- Expresses dependence on God ("Man shall not live on bread alone" - Matthew 4:4)
- Intensifies prayer
- Humbles the soul (Psalm 35:13)
- Seeks God's guidance (Acts 14:23)
- Repentance and mourning over sin (Joel 2:12)
- Jesus expected it: "**When** you fast..." not "if" (Matthew 6:16)

#### Biblical Examples

- **Moses:** 40 days receiving the Law (Exodus 34:28)
- **David:** Fasted over his dying child (2 Samuel 12:16)
- **Esther:** Called fast before approaching king (Esther 4:16)
- **Daniel:** Partial fast, 21 days (Daniel 10:3)
- **Jesus:** 40 days in wilderness (Matthew 4:2)
- **Early church:** Before sending missionaries (Acts 13:2-3)

#### Types of Fasts

**1. Normal Fast**
- No food, water only
- Most common type

**2. Partial Fast**
- Restrict certain foods (like Daniel)
- One meal per day
- Specific food categories

**3. Absolute Fast**
- No food or water
- Short duration only (Esther's 3-day fast)
- **Use caution**—medically risky

**4. Non-Food Fasts**
- Social media
- Television/entertainment
- Technology
- Shopping
- Not biblical fasts but can help focus on God

#### How to Practice

**Start Small**
- Skip one meal, spend that time in prayer
- Try 24-hour fast (dinner to dinner)
- Build up to longer fasts

**Prepare Physically**
- Check with doctor if health concerns
- Ease in (light meals before)
- Drink plenty of water
- Ease out (don't gorge after!)

**Prepare Spiritually**
- Set purpose (prayer focus, seek guidance, repentance, etc.)
- Plan what you'll do with meal times (pray, read Scripture)
- Expect hunger—that's the point! Let it turn you to God

**During the Fast**
- Pray when hungry
- Read Scripture
- Journal
- Seek God's face
- Don't draw attention to it (Matthew 6:16-18)

**Break the Fast**
- Light food first
- Thank God
- Journal what you learned

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I get too hungry"** → Start with skipping one meal. Drink water. Remember: hunger is the point—it reminds you to pray.
- **"I have medical issues"** → Fast from something besides food. God cares about your heart, not legalism.
- **"Does it manipulate God?"** → No. Fasting doesn't twist God's arm. It changes us, not Him. It positions our hearts to hear.

### 4. Worship

**"Worship the LORD in the splendor of his holiness." - Psalm 29:2**

#### What Is Worship?

Ascribing worth to God; honoring and adoring Him for who He is and what He's done. Involves both personal devotion and corporate gatherings.

#### Why Practice It?

- We were created to worship (Revelation 4:11)
- God seeks worshipers (John 4:23-24)
- Worship reorients our hearts toward God
- Connects us to body of Christ
- Combats idolatry (we worship something—better be God!)

#### Biblical Examples

- **David:** Danced before the Lord (2 Samuel 6:14); wrote worship songs (Psalms)
- **Isaiah:** Encountered God's holiness, cried "Holy, holy, holy!" (Isaiah 6:1-8)
- **Mary:** Magnificat (Luke 1:46-55)
- **Early church:** "Devoted themselves to... breaking of bread and to prayer" (Acts 2:42)
- **Heaven:** Continuous worship (Revelation 4-5)

#### How to Practice

**Personal Worship**
- Sing to God (Ephesians 5:19; Colossians 3:16)
  - Worship music at home
  - Sing in car
  - Sing Psalms
- Speak praise to God
  - "God, you are..."
  - Thank Him for who He is
- Meditate on God's attributes
  - Holy, loving, just, merciful, powerful, wise, etc.
- Read Psalms aloud as worship

**Corporate Worship**
- Gather with believers (Hebrews 10:24-25)
- Participate in singing
- Listen to preaching
- Take communion
- Pray together
- Give offerings
- Don't just attend—engage!

**Lifestyle Worship**
- "Offer your bodies as living sacrifices" (Romans 12:1)
- Work as unto the Lord (Colossians 3:23)
- Every action can be worship when done for God's glory (1 Corinthians 10:31)

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I'm not musical"** → Worship isn't about talent. Sing joyfully, even if off-key. God loves it!
- **"I don't feel it"** → Worship isn't based on feelings. It's obedience. Feelings often follow.
- **"Church is boring"** → Pray for your heart. Serve in church. Remember: you're not the audience—God is.

### 5. Solitude & Silence

**"Be still, and know that I am God." - Psalm 46:10**

#### What Is It?

- **Solitude:** Being alone with God, away from people
- **Silence:** Quieting external noise and internal chatter to hear God

#### Why Practice It?

- Jesus regularly withdrew to solitary places (Luke 5:16)
- Modern life is noisy and rushed
- Silence slows us down
- Solitude removes distractions
- Allows us to hear God's "still small voice" (1 Kings 19:12)
- Combats busyness and anxiety

#### Biblical Examples

- **Jesus:** Alone in wilderness 40 days (Luke 4:1-2); withdrew to pray (Mark 1:35; Luke 5:16)
- **Elijah:** Heard God in silence, not earthquake/fire/wind (1 Kings 19:11-13)
- **Moses:** Alone with God on mountain (Exodus 24:18)
- **Paul:** Arabia for 3 years after conversion (Galatians 1:17-18)

#### How to Practice

**Mini Retreats**
- 15 minutes: Turn off phone, sit quietly, pray
- 1 hour: Go to park or quiet room, Bible and journal, pray and reflect
- Half day: Saturday morning, extended time with God
- Full day: Personal retreat once or twice a year

**Silence**
- Start with 5 minutes of silence
- No phone, music, talking, reading
- Sit. Breathe. Be still.
- Let thoughts come and go
- Rest in God's presence
- Build up to longer periods

**Solitude**
- Go somewhere alone
- No agenda (no checklist to complete)
- Bring Bible if helpful
- Walk and pray
- Listen

**Listening Prayer**
- After praying, sit quietly
- "Speak, Lord, your servant is listening" (1 Samuel 3:9)
- Don't expect audible voice
- Notice impressions, Scripture that comes to mind, peace
- Test against Scripture (1 John 4:1)

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I can't sit still"** → Start with 5 minutes. It's hard! That's why it's a discipline. You're training yourself.
- **"My mind wanders"** → Normal. Gently return focus. It gets easier with practice.
- **"I don't hear anything"** → God speaks in many ways. Sometimes silence itself is the gift—rest in His presence.

### 6. Confession & Repentance

**"If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness." - 1 John 1:9**

#### What Is It?

- **Confession:** Agreeing with God about our sin; admitting it honestly
- **Repentance:** Turning from sin; change of mind leading to change of behavior

#### Why Practice It?

- Sin breaks fellowship with God
- Confession restores it
- Unconfessed sin hardens the heart
- Keeping short accounts with God maintains sensitivity to Holy Spirit
- Leads to freedom and joy (Psalm 32:1-5)

#### Biblical Examples

- **David:** Psalm 51 after adultery with Bathsheba
- **Tax collector:** "God, have mercy on me, a sinner" (Luke 18:13)
- **Prodigal son:** "Father, I have sinned..." (Luke 15:21)
- **Peter:** Wept bitterly after denying Jesus (Luke 22:62)
- **Israelites:** National confession led by Ezra and Nehemiah (Nehemiah 9)

#### How to Practice

**Daily Examination**
- Before bed, review your day
- Ask God to show you where you sinned
- Confess specifically (not "forgive my sins in general")
- Receive forgiveness
- Plan how to change

**Immediate Confession**
- When Holy Spirit convicts, confess immediately
- Don't let sin pile up
- Quick repentance keeps heart tender

**Confession to Others**
- "Confess your sins to each other" (James 5:16)
- Find trusted Christian friend/accountability partner
- Confess struggles and temptations
- Not all sin needs public confession—but secret sin needs to be confessed to someone

**Steps for Repentance**
1. **Acknowledge** - Admit the sin, no excuses
2. **Confess** - Tell God specifically
3. **Forsake** - Turn away from it
4. **Replace** - Put off sin, put on righteousness (Ephesians 4:22-24)
5. **Make restitution** if needed - Make things right with others

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I keep sinning the same way"** → Normal battle. Keep confessing. Don't give up. Seek help if needed. Sanctification is a process.
- **"I don't feel forgiven"** → Forgiveness is a fact, not a feeling. God promises it (1 John 1:9). Trust His Word.
- **"My sin is too big"** → No sin is too big for God's grace. He forgave murderers, adulterers, deniers. Come to Him.

### 7. Fellowship

**"Let us not give up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing, but let us encourage one another." - Hebrews 10:25**

#### What Is It?

Authentic community with other believers. Sharing life together, not just attending church.

#### Why Practice It?

- We need each other (1 Corinthians 12:12-27)
- Iron sharpens iron (Proverbs 27:17)
- Accountability and encouragement
- Bearing one another's burdens (Galatians 6:2)
- God's design from the beginning (Genesis 2:18 - "not good to be alone")
- Early church devoted to fellowship (Acts 2:42)

#### Biblical Examples

- **Early church:** Daily fellowship, shared meals, prayed together (Acts 2:42-47)
- **David and Jonathan:** Deep friendship (1 Samuel 18:1-4)
- **Paul and his companions:** Traveled together, supported each other
- **Jesus and disciples:** Lived life together for 3 years

#### How to Practice

**Be Committed to a Local Church**
- Join a church (don't just attend)
- Participate actively
- Serve in ministry
- Give financially
- Submit to leadership

**Join a Small Group**
- Bible study group
- Accountability group
- Life group/home group
- Meet regularly (weekly if possible)
- Share struggles, pray together

**Develop Deep Friendships**
- Move beyond surface level
- Be vulnerable
- Meet one-on-one regularly
- Serve together
- Do life together

**Hospitality**
- Invite people to your home
- Share meals
- "Practice hospitality" (Romans 12:13)
- Doesn't need to be fancy—just genuine

**Be Intentional**
- Fellowship doesn't happen by accident
- Schedule time with believers
- Prioritize community

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I don't have time"** → Make time. We find time for what we value. Cut something less important.
- **"I've been hurt by Christians"** → Understandable. But don't isolate. Find a healthy church. Give it another try. We need the body.
- **"I'm introverted"** → Fellowship doesn't require being the life of the party. Even introverts need a few close friendships.

### 8. Service

**"Serve one another humbly in love." - Galatians 5:13**

#### What Is It?

Using your time, talents, and resources to meet others' needs and advance God's kingdom. Serving God by serving others.

#### Why Practice It?

- Jesus came to serve (Mark 10:45)
- We're saved to serve (Ephesians 2:10)
- Love is demonstrated through action (1 John 3:18)
- Combats selfishness
- Builds Christ's body (Ephesians 4:12)
- We'll be judged by how we served (Matthew 25:31-46)

#### Biblical Examples

- **Jesus:** Washed disciples' feet (John 13:1-17)
- **Dorcas:** "Always doing good and helping the poor" (Acts 9:36)
- **Phoebe:** Servant of the church, helped many (Romans 16:1-2)
- **Paul:** Worked to support ministry, served churches tirelessly

#### How to Practice

**Serve in Your Church**
- Teach children's class
- Greet visitors
- Serve on tech team
- Clean the building
- Lead worship
- Whatever's needed!

**Serve Your Family**
- Husbands: Love and lead
- Wives: Support and encourage
- Parents: Raise children in the Lord
- Children: Obey and honor parents
- Do household chores joyfully

**Serve Your Community**
- Volunteer at food bank
- Visit nursing homes
- Tutor kids
- Help single moms with home repairs
- Be a good neighbor

**Serve in Your Workplace**
- Work as unto the Lord (Colossians 3:23-24)
- Be excellent
- Show integrity
- Serve your coworkers and customers

**Use Your Spiritual Gifts**
- Every believer has gifts (1 Corinthians 12; Romans 12; Ephesians 4)
- Discover yours (try different ministries, ask others)
- Use them to serve the body

#### Attitudes in Service

- **Humility** - Jesus washed feet
- **Joy** - Serve cheerfully (2 Corinthians 9:7)
- **Faithfulness** - Do what you commit to
- **Excellence** - Do your best
- **Invisibly** - Don't seek recognition (Matthew 6:1-4)

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I'm too busy"** → You have time for what you prioritize. Start small. Serve where you already are.
- **"I don't have any skills"** → You have skills! Everyone can encourage, pray, show up, help. Don't underestimate simple service.
- **"I burn out"** → Serve from overflow, not emptiness. Can't give what you don't have. Balance is key.

### 9. Generosity

**"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." - 2 Corinthians 9:7**

#### What Is It?

Giving freely of your financial resources, time, and possessions to God's work and others' needs.

#### Why Practice It?

- Everything we have is God's (Psalm 24:1)
- "Where your treasure is, there your heart will be" (Matthew 6:21)
- Combats materialism and greed
- Demonstrates trust in God's provision
- Supports God's work in the world
- Jesus was generous (2 Corinthians 8:9)

#### Biblical Examples

- **Widow's mite:** Gave all she had (Mark 12:41-44)
- **Macedonian churches:** Gave generously despite poverty (2 Corinthians 8:1-5)
- **Zacchaeus:** Gave half his possessions, repaid those he cheated (Luke 19:8)
- **Early church:** Shared possessions to meet needs (Acts 2:44-45; 4:32-37)

#### How to Practice

**Tithe**
- Give first 10% to your local church
- OT principle; NT principle is generous, sacrificial giving
- "Honor the LORD with your wealth, with the firstfruits" (Proverbs 3:9)

**Give Beyond the Tithe**
- Missions
- Charities
- Help individuals in need
- Build God's kingdom

**Give Strategically**
- Support gospel-preaching churches and ministries
- Help the poor (Proverbs 19:17)
- Invest in eternal things

**Give Sacrificially**
- Widow gave sacrificially (Mark 12:42-44)
- Macedonians gave "beyond their ability" (2 Corinthians 8:3)
- Give until it affects your lifestyle

**Give Cheerfully**
- Not grudgingly
- "God loves a cheerful giver" (2 Corinthians 9:7)
- Joy in giving reflects trust in God

**Be Generous in Other Ways**
- Time
- Hospitality
- Encouragement
- Resources
- Skills

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I can't afford it"** → Can't afford NOT to. God provides for givers (2 Corinthians 9:8-11). Trust Him.
- **"What about my savings?"** → Save wisely, but don't hoard. Balance saving with giving.
- **"The church wastes money"** → Find a faithful church. But don't use this as excuse not to give.

### 10. Sabbath Rest

**"Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest." - Matthew 11:28**

#### What Is It?

Setting aside regular time (traditionally one day per week) to rest from work and focus on God. Not legalistic Sabbath-keeping but gospel-centered rest.

#### Why Practice It?

- God rested on seventh day, established pattern (Genesis 2:2-3)
- Fourth Commandment (Exodus 20:8-11)
- Reminds us God is in control, not us
- Combats drivenness and workaholism
- Physical and spiritual refreshment
- Jesus is our Sabbath rest (Hebrews 4:9-10)

#### Biblical Examples

- **God:** Rested on seventh day (Genesis 2:2-3)
- **Israel:** Commanded to rest (Exodus 20:8-11)
- **Jesus:** Attended synagogue on Sabbath (Luke 4:16), but wasn't legalistic about it (Mark 2:27)

#### How to Practice

**Choose a Day**
- Traditionally Sunday (Lord's Day)
- Or another day that works for your schedule
- Be consistent

**Rest from Work**
- No job-related work
- No household projects
- Catch up later
- Trust God to provide

**Focus on God**
- Church worship
- Extended prayer and Bible reading
- Family devotions
- Christian fellowship

**Enjoy God's Creation**
- Take a walk
- Spend time in nature
- Play with family
- Enjoy hobbies
- Delight in God's gifts

**Prepare Ahead**
- Get work done other days
- Prepare meals in advance
- Clear schedule
- Protect your Sabbath

**Don't Be Legalistic**
- Jesus said Sabbath was made for man, not man for Sabbath (Mark 2:27)
- Works of mercy and necessity are fine
- Focus on rest, worship, delight—not rules

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I'm too busy"** → That's exactly why you NEED Sabbath. God will provide the time. Trust Him.
- **"I have to work Sundays"** → Choose another day. Principle matters more than specific day.
- **"I get bored"** → Rediscover delight in simple things. We're so overstimulated we forgot how to rest.

### 11. Simplicity

**"But godliness with contentment is great gain." - 1 Timothy 6:6**

#### What Is It?

Living with less; focusing on what truly matters; freedom from materialism and excess.

#### Why Practice It?

- Combats materialism (1 Timothy 6:9-10)
- Frees resources for kingdom work
- Reduces stress and distraction
- Increases contentment (Philippians 4:11-12)
- Focuses on eternal, not temporal (Matthew 6:19-21)

#### Biblical Examples

- **Jesus:** "Foxes have dens... but the Son of Man has no place to lay his head" (Matthew 8:20)
- **Paul:** Content in all circumstances (Philippians 4:11-13)
- **Rich young ruler (negative):** Couldn't give up possessions (Mark 10:17-22)
- **Early church:** Sold possessions to meet needs (Acts 2:45)

#### How to Practice

**Declutter**
- Go through possessions
- Give away what you don't need/use
- "If in doubt, throw it out" (or give it away)
- Less stuff = less stress

**Resist Consumerism**
- Don't buy impulsively
- Wait 30 days before major purchases
- Ask: "Do I need this or just want it?"
- Unsubscribe from marketing emails

**Live Below Your Means**
- Don't spend everything you make
- Avoid debt (Romans 13:8)
- Save, give, live simply

**Contentment**
- "I have learned the secret of being content" (Philippians 4:12)
- Practice gratitude
- Stop comparing to others
- Find satisfaction in God, not things

**Focus Time and Energy**
- Say no to good things to say yes to best things
- Simplify schedule
- Protect margin

#### Common Obstacles

- **"But I worked hard for this"** → Yes. But do you own your possessions or do they own you?
- **"I might need it someday"** → Trust God to provide. You don't need to hoard.
- **"Everyone else has..."** → So what? You're not called to keep up with anyone.

### 12. Journaling

**"Write down the revelation." - Habakkuk 2:2**

#### What Is It?

Writing down prayers, insights from Scripture, spiritual experiences, and reflections on your walk with God.

#### Why Practice It?

- Helps process thoughts and emotions
- Records God's faithfulness (for remembering and testimony)
- Tracks spiritual growth
- Clarifies thinking
- Deepens prayer and Bible study
- Biblical precedent (Psalms are David's journal!)

#### Biblical Examples

- **David:** Psalms record his prayers and experiences
- **Prophets:** Wrote down God's messages
- **Paul:** Letters to churches (his "journal" to them)

#### How to Practice

**Get a Journal**
- Notebook, digital doc, app—whatever you'll use
- Don't overthink it

**Write Regularly**
- Daily or several times per week
- Make it a habit
- Don't pressure yourself to write a lot

**What to Write**
- Prayers to God
- Bible verses that spoke to you
- Insights from study
- Struggles and victories
- Answered prayers (important!)
- Questions you have
- Spiritual experiences

**Review Periodically**
- Monthly or yearly
- See how God has worked
- Recognize patterns
- Build faith by remembering

#### Common Obstacles

- **"I'm not a writer"** → It's not about quality. It's for you (and God). No one else needs to read it.
- **"I don't have time"** → 5 minutes is enough. Just a few sentences.
- **"I don't know what to write"** → Start with what you learned in Bible reading, or a simple prayer.

## Building a Rule of Life

### What Is a "Rule of Life"?

A **"Rule of Life"** is a personal plan for practicing spiritual disciplines. Not legalistic rules, but rhythms and practices that create space for God.

### Create Your Own

**1. Assess Current Reality**
- Which disciplines do you already practice?
- Which do you neglect?
- What's one small change you could make?

**2. Start Small**
- Don't try to do everything at once
- Pick 1-3 disciplines to focus on
- Build gradually

**3. Be Specific**
- "I will pray more" is too vague
- "I will pray 15 minutes every morning at 6am" is specific

**4. Build Rhythms**

**Daily:**
- Morning prayer (15 min)
- Bible reading (15 min)
- Evening gratitude (5 min)

**Weekly:**
- Church worship (Sunday)
- Sabbath rest (Sunday)
- Small group (Wednesday)

**Monthly:**
- Half-day solitude retreat (first Saturday)
- Review journal

**Yearly:**
- Full-day retreat (once or twice)
- Fast for specific purpose

**5. Get Accountability**
- Share your plan with a friend
- Ask them to check in
- Adjust as needed

**6. Give Yourself Grace**
- You'll miss days—that's okay
- Don't give up
- Disciplines are means of grace, not laws

### Sample Rule of Life

**Morning (30 min):**
- Prayer (10 min) - ACTS model
- Bible reading & journaling (20 min)

**Throughout Day:**
- Arrow prayers when I think of it
- "Practice the presence of God"

**Evening (10 min):**
- Examine the day
- Confession if needed
- Gratitude list (3 things)

**Sunday:**
- Church worship
- Sabbath rest (no work)
- Family time

**Monthly:**
- Fast one day
- Solitude retreat (3 hours)

**Ongoing:**
- Serve in church (children's ministry)
- Small group Wednesday nights
- Generosity (tithe + offerings)

## Common Obstacles

### 1. Legalism

**Problem:** Treating disciplines as a way to earn God's favor

**Solution:**
- Remember: Saved by grace ALONE (Ephesians 2:8-9)
- Disciplines don't save you; they position you to receive grace
- It's relationship, not religion

### 2. Guilt When You Miss

**Problem:** Feeling like a failure when you skip a day

**Solution:**
- Grace for yourself
- Start again tomorrow
- God doesn't love you less

### 3. Inconsistency

**Problem:** Starting strong but not maintaining

**Solution:**
- Start smaller
- Build one habit at a time
- Accountability partner

### 4. Busyness

**Problem:** "I don't have time"

**Solution:**
- You find time for what you value
- Start with 10-15 minutes
- Cut something less important

### 5. Expecting Immediate Results

**Problem:** "I don't feel different"

**Solution:**
- Spiritual growth is gradual
- Keep practicing
- Look back over months/years to see change

### 6. Comparison

**Problem:** "Everyone else is more spiritual"

**Solution:**
- Run your own race (Hebrews 12:1)
- Don't compare
- Focus on YOUR growth

## Final Encouragement

**Spiritual disciplines are training, not earning.**

Just as an athlete trains to compete, we train to grow in godliness. The goal is knowing God, becoming like Christ, and glorifying Him in all we do.

**Start where you are.** Pick one or two disciplines. Practice them for a month. Add more gradually.

**God will meet you.** As you create space for Him, He will fill it. He promises to draw near to those who draw near to Him (James 4:8).

**You can do this.** Not through willpower alone, but through the Holy Spirit's power working in you.
