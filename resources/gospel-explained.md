# The Gospel Explained

**The most important message in all of Scripture**

---

## What Is the Gospel?

The word "gospel" means **"good news."** It is the central message of the entire Bible: God's plan to save humanity through Jesus Christ.

**In one sentence:** *God sent His Son Jesus Christ to die for our sins and rise from the dead, so that everyone who believes in Him can be forgiven and have eternal life.*

**"For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life." - John 3:16**

---

## The Gospel in Four Points

### 1. GOD (His Design)

**God created us to be with Him and for His glory.**

- **Genesis 1:27** - "So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them."
- **Revelation 4:11** - "You are worthy, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power, for you created all things, and by your will they were created and have their being."

**Purpose:** We were made by God, for God, to know God and enjoy Him forever.

**The problem:** Something went terribly wrong.

---

### 2. MAN (Our Problem)

**We sinned and separated ourselves from God.**

- **Romans 3:23** - "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."
- **Romans 6:23a** - "For the wages of sin is death..."
- **Isaiah 59:2** - "But your iniquities have separated you from your God; your sins have hidden his face from you."

**What is sin?**
- Missing the mark of God's perfect standard
- Rebellion against God
- Going our own way instead of God's way (Isaiah 53:6)
- Loving anything more than God

**The result of sin:**
- Spiritual death (separation from God)
- Physical death
- Eternal separation from God (hell)
- Broken relationship with our Creator

**The reality:** We cannot save ourselves. Our good works cannot erase our sin debt.

- **Ephesians 2:8-9** - "For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith—and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God—not by works, so that no one can boast."
- **Isaiah 64:6** - "All our righteous acts are like filthy rags."

---

### 3. CHRIST (God's Solution)

**Jesus Christ died for our sins and rose from the dead.**

**Who is Jesus?**
- Fully God and fully man (John 1:1, 14)
- Born of a virgin (Isaiah 7:14, Matthew 1:23)
- Lived a sinless life (Hebrews 4:15)
- The only mediator between God and man (1 Timothy 2:5)

**What did Jesus do?**

**A. He died in our place**
- **Romans 5:8** - "But God demonstrates his own love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us."
- **2 Corinthians 5:21** - "God made him who had no sin to be sin for us, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God."
- **1 Peter 3:18** - "For Christ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, to bring you to God."

Jesus took the punishment we deserved. He paid the penalty for our sin on the cross.

**B. He rose from the dead**
- **1 Corinthians 15:3-4** - "For what I received I passed on to you as of first importance: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures."
- **Romans 1:4** - He "was declared to be the Son of God in power according to the Spirit of holiness by his resurrection from the dead."

The resurrection proves:
- Jesus is who He said He is (God)
- Death is defeated
- His sacrifice was accepted
- We too will be raised

**C. He offers forgiveness and eternal life**
- **Romans 6:23** - "For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord."
- **John 14:6** - "I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me."
- **Acts 4:12** - "Salvation is found in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to mankind by which we must be saved."

---

### 4. RESPONSE (Our Decision)

**We must repent and believe to be saved.**

**A. REPENT (Turn from sin)**
- **Acts 3:19** - "Repent, then, and turn to God, so that your sins may be wiped out."
- **Luke 13:3** - "Unless you repent, you too will all perish."
- **2 Corinthians 7:10** - "Godly sorrow brings repentance that leads to salvation."

Repentance means:
- Acknowledging your sin before God
- Feeling genuine sorrow over your sin
- Turning away from sin
- Turning toward God

**B. BELIEVE (Trust in Christ alone)**
- **John 3:16** - "Whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."
- **Acts 16:31** - "Believe in the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved."
- **Romans 10:9** - "If you declare with your mouth, 'Jesus is Lord,' and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved."

Believing means:
- Trusting that Jesus died for YOUR sins
- Relying completely on Jesus, not yourself
- Surrendering your life to Jesus as Lord
- Receiving Him as your Savior

**C. RECEIVE (Accept God's gift)**
- **John 1:12** - "Yet to all who did receive him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God."
- **Revelation 3:20** - "Here I am! I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in."

## The Results of Salvation

When you trust Christ, you receive:

### 1. Forgiveness of Sins
- **Ephesians 1:7** - "In him we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins."
- **1 John 1:9** - "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness."
- **Psalm 103:12** - "As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us."

### 2. Eternal Life
- **John 3:36** - "Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life."
- **John 10:28** - "I give them eternal life, and they shall never perish."
- **1 John 5:11-12** - "God has given us eternal life, and this life is in his Son. Whoever has the Son has life."

### 3. A New Identity
- **2 Corinthians 5:17** - "Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!"
- **John 1:12** - You become a child of God
- **Ephesians 1:5** - You are adopted into God's family

### 4. The Holy Spirit
- **Ephesians 1:13-14** - "You were marked in him with a seal, the promised Holy Spirit, who is a deposit guaranteeing our inheritance."
- **Romans 8:9** - "You, however, are not in the realm of the flesh but are in the realm of the Spirit, if indeed the Spirit of God lives in you."

### 5. Peace with God
- **Romans 5:1** - "Therefore, since we have been justified through faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ."
- **Colossians 1:20** - Reconciliation with God

### 6. Eternal Security
- **John 10:28-29** - "I give them eternal life, and they shall never perish; no one will snatch them out of my hand."
- **Romans 8:38-39** - Nothing can separate us from God's love
- **Philippians 1:6** - "He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion."

## How to Respond to the Gospel

### A Prayer of Salvation

If you understand the gospel and want to trust Christ, you can pray something like this:

> *"Dear God,*
>
> *I know that I am a sinner and that I deserve separation from You. I cannot save myself. Thank You for sending Jesus to die on the cross for my sins. I believe that Jesus died for me and rose from the dead. I turn away from my sin and trust in Jesus alone as my Savior and Lord.*
>
> *Please forgive me and give me eternal life. Make me Your child. Help me to live for You from this day forward.*
>
> *In Jesus' name I pray, Amen."*

**Remember:** It's not the exact words that save you—it's genuine faith in Jesus Christ. The prayer is simply a way to express that faith to God.

## What Happens Next?

If you have trusted Christ, here's what to do:

### 1. Tell Someone
- **Romans 10:9** - "If you declare with your mouth, 'Jesus is Lord,' and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved."
- Tell a Christian friend, family member, or pastor

### 2. Get Baptized
- **Matthew 28:19-20** - Jesus commands believers to be baptized
- Baptism publicly identifies you with Christ
- It's an outward sign of an inward change

### 3. Read Your Bible
- **1 Peter 2:2** - "Like newborn babies, crave pure spiritual milk, so that by it you may grow up in your salvation."
- Start with the Gospel of John
- See our [Bible Reading Plans](reading-plans.md)

### 4. Pray Daily
- **1 Thessalonians 5:17** - "Pray continually."
- Talk to God about everything
- See our [Prayers of the Bible](../indexes/prayers.md) for examples

### 5. Find a Church
- **Hebrews 10:24-25** - "Let us not give up meeting together."
- You need other believers
- Look for a church that preaches the Bible

### 6. Tell Others About Jesus
- **Matthew 28:19-20** - "Go and make disciples."
- Share what Jesus has done for you
- Use this guide to explain the gospel to others

## The Romans Road to Salvation

A simple way to explain the gospel using the book of Romans:

1. **Romans 3:23** - "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."
   - **We are all sinners**

2. **Romans 6:23** - "For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord."
   - **Sin's penalty is death, but God offers eternal life**

3. **Romans 5:8** - "But God demonstrates his own love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us."
   - **Jesus died for us**

4. **Romans 10:9** - "If you declare with your mouth, 'Jesus is Lord,' and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved."
   - **We must believe and confess**

5. **Romans 10:13** - "Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved."
   - **Salvation is available to all**

6. **Romans 8:1** - "Therefore, there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus."
   - **Believers are free from condemnation**

7. **Romans 8:38-39** - Nothing "will be able to separate us from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord."
   - **We are secure in God's love**

## Common Questions About Salvation

### Q: Is baptism required for salvation?

**A:** No. Salvation is by faith alone in Christ alone (Ephesians 2:8-9). However, Jesus commands believers to be baptized (Matthew 28:19), and it's an important step of obedience. The thief on the cross was saved without baptism (Luke 23:43).

### Q: Can I lose my salvation?

**A:** No. True believers are eternally secure (John 10:28-29, Romans 8:38-39). God finishes what He starts (Philippians 1:6). However, genuine faith produces a changed life (James 2:17).

### Q: What if I sin after I'm saved?

**A:** Christians still struggle with sin, but we have an Advocate—Jesus (1 John 2:1). When we sin, we confess it (1 John 1:9), and God forgives us. Salvation is not lost through sin, but fellowship with God is affected.

### Q: How can I know I'm saved?

**A:**
- You have assurance through God's promises (1 John 5:11-13)
- The Holy Spirit testifies with your spirit (Romans 8:16)
- Your life shows the fruit of salvation (changed desires, love for God and others, growth)
- You have peace with God (Romans 5:1)

### Q: What about people who never hear the gospel?

**A:** God is both just and loving (Romans 3:26). He reveals Himself through creation (Romans 1:20) and conscience (Romans 2:14-15). Those who truly seek God will find Him (Jeremiah 29:13). Our responsibility is to share the gospel so people have the opportunity to believe (Romans 10:14-15).

### Q: Is Jesus the only way to God?

**A:** Yes. Jesus Himself said, "I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me" (John 14:6). Salvation is found in no other name (Acts 4:12). Other religions offer human attempts to reach God; Christianity is God reaching down to save us.

### Q: Can I just be a good person instead?

**A:** No one is good enough (Romans 3:10-12). All have sinned (Romans 3:23). Our best efforts are insufficient (Isaiah 64:6). We need a Savior, not self-improvement. Good works are the result of salvation, not the cause (Ephesians 2:8-10).

### Q: What if I've committed terrible sins?

**A:** God's grace is greater than any sin. Jesus died for ALL sins—past, present, and future. Consider:
- **David** - Committed adultery and murder; God forgave him (Psalm 51)
- **Paul** - Persecuted Christians; became the greatest apostle (1 Timothy 1:15-16)
- **The thief on the cross** - Saved at the last moment (Luke 23:43)

"Though your sins are like scarlet, they shall be as white as snow" (Isaiah 1:18).

## Sharing the Gospel with Others

### Quick Presentation (1-2 minutes)

**The bridge illustration:**
1. God created us for relationship with Him (Genesis 1-2)
2. Sin separates us from God (Romans 3:23, 6:23)
3. Jesus is the bridge—He died to reconcile us to God (1 Timothy 2:5)
4. We cross the bridge by faith—repenting and believing (John 3:16)

### Conversational Approach

Ask questions:
- "Have you ever thought about spiritual things?"
- "What do you think happens after we die?"
- "Can I share what changed my life?"

Share your story:
- What your life was like before Christ
- How you came to faith
- What Jesus has done in your life

Point to Scripture:
- Use the Romans Road or John 3:16
- Let God's Word do the work

Invite response:
- "Does this make sense?"
- "Is there anything keeping you from trusting Christ today?"
- "Would you like to receive Jesus as your Savior?"
