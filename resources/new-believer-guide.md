# New Believer's Guide: Your First 90 Days as a Christian

## Welcome to the Family!

Congratulations! If you've recently given your life to Jesus Christ, you've made the most important decision of your entire life. You've been:

- **Forgiven** of all your sins (1 John 1:9)
- **Adopted** into God's family (John 1:12)
- **Sealed** with the Holy Spirit (Ephesians 1:13)
- **Given** eternal life (John 3:16)
- **Made** a new creation (2 Corinthians 5:17)

You're not the same person you were. Old things have passed away; everything has become new!

This guide will help you navigate your first 90 days as a follower of Jesus. Think of it as a roadmap for your new life in Christ.

## What Just Happened? Understanding Your Salvation

### The Moment of Salvation

When you placed your faith in Jesus Christ, several incredible things happened instantaneously:

**1. You Were Forgiven**
*"If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness."* - 1 John 1:9

- Every sin—past, present, and future—was forgiven
- God removed your guilt completely
- Your slate is wiped clean

**2. You Were Justified**
*"Therefore, since we have been justified through faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ."* - Romans 5:1

- You've been declared righteous before God
- It's as if you never sinned
- You now have peace with God

**3. You Were Adopted**
*"Yet to all who did receive him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God."* - John 1:12

- You're now a child of God
- God is your Father
- You're part of His family forever

**4. You Received the Holy Spirit**
*"And you also were included in Christ when you heard the message of truth, the gospel of your salvation. When you believed, you were marked in him with a seal, the promised Holy Spirit."* - Ephesians 1:13

- The Holy Spirit now lives inside you
- He will guide, teach, and transform you
- You're never alone

**5. You Received Eternal Life**
*"For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."* - John 3:16

- You will live forever with God
- Death is not the end
- Your future is secure

**6. You Became a New Creation**
*"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!"* - 2 Corinthians 5:17

- You're not just an improved version of yourself
- You're fundamentally new
- God is making you more like Jesus

### Can You Lose Your Salvation?

**No.** Your salvation is secure because it's based on what Jesus did, not what you do.

*"I give them eternal life, and they shall never perish; no one will snatch them out of my hand."* - John 10:28

- You're held by God's power, not your own
- Jesus' death was sufficient for all your sins
- The Holy Spirit seals you until redemption (Ephesians 4:30)

**But doesn't that mean I can sin all I want?**

No! If you truly know Jesus, you won't want to keep sinning. The Holy Spirit will convict you and change your desires. Real faith produces real change (James 2:17).

## Your First 24 Hours

### Things to Do Right Away

**1. Thank God**
Take a moment to pray and thank God for saving you. It doesn't have to be fancy:

*"God, thank You for forgiving me. Thank You for Jesus. Thank You for giving me eternal life. I don't fully understand it all yet, but I'm grateful. Help me to follow You. Amen."*

**2. Tell Someone**
Jesus said, *"Whoever acknowledges me before others, I will also acknowledge before my Father in heaven"* (Matthew 10:32).

- Tell the person who led you to Christ
- Call a Christian friend or family member
- Text someone: "I just gave my life to Jesus!"

Don't keep it a secret. Telling others reinforces your decision and gives them an opportunity to encourage you.

**3. Write It Down**
Document this day. You'll want to remember it.

- Write the date in your Bible
- Journal about what happened
- Note what led you to this decision

**4. Read the Gospel of John**
Start reading the Bible. The Gospel of John is a great place to begin.

- Read John 1 today
- Try to read a chapter each day
- Underline or highlight verses that stand out

**5. Pray Before Bed**
End your day in prayer. Talk to God like you'd talk to a loving Father.

## Your First Week

### Goals for Week 1

**1. Establish a Daily Quiet Time**
Set aside time each day to read your Bible and pray.

- **When**: Morning is ideal, but choose what works for you
- **Where**: Find a quiet place without distractions
- **How Long**: Start with 10-15 minutes
- **What to Read**: Continue in the Gospel of John (chapters 1-7)

**Sample Morning Routine**:
1. Pray: "God, open my eyes to see wonderful things in Your Word" (Psalm 119:18)
2. Read: One chapter of John
3. Reflect: What did I learn about God? About myself?
4. Pray: Talk to God about what you read and about your day

**2. Connect with the Person Who Led You to Christ**
- Meet for coffee or lunch
- Ask questions you have
- Let them know how you're doing

**3. Find a Church (If You Don't Have One)**
You need a church family. Don't try to follow Jesus alone.

- Ask your Christian friend for recommendations
- Visit this Sunday
- Look for a church that teaches the Bible clearly

**4. Remove Obvious Sin from Your Life**
The Holy Spirit may already be convicting you about certain things.

- Delete apps or unsubscribe from content that leads you to sin
- Distance yourself from relationships that pull you away from God
- Make changes that align with your new identity

**5. Tell Your Story**
Practice sharing your testimony in three parts:
- Before Jesus (what your life was like)
- Meeting Jesus (how you came to faith)
- After Jesus (what's changed)

See: [Personal Testimony Guide](personal-testimony-guide.md)

## Your First 30 Days

### Goals for Month 1

**1. Complete the Gospel of John**
- Read one chapter per day
- John has 21 chapters, so you'll finish in 3 weeks
- Write down questions as you read

**2. Attend Church Every Sunday**
Make church attendance a non-negotiable priority.

- Sit near the front to minimize distractions
- Take notes during the sermon
- Introduce yourself to people

**3. Join a Small Group or Bible Study**
You need close Christian community.

- Ask church staff about groups for new believers
- Look for a group that meets weekly
- Be honest that you're new to faith

**4. Memorize Your First Scripture Verse**
Start building God's Word into your heart.

**Suggested First Verse**: John 3:16
*"For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."*

**How to Memorize**:
- Write it on a notecard
- Say it out loud 10 times
- Review it daily for a week
- Recite it without looking

See: [Scripture Memory System](memory-verse-system.md)

**5. Pray Daily**
Talk to God every day. Prayer is conversation with God.

**Simple Prayer Framework (ACTS)**:
- **A**doration: Praise God for who He is
- **C**onfession: Admit your sins
- **T**hanksgiving: Thank God for what He's done
- **S**upplication: Ask God for what you and others need

**Example**:
- "God, You are holy and loving..." (Adoration)
- "I'm sorry for being impatient today..." (Confession)
- "Thank You for saving me and giving me Your Spirit..." (Thanksgiving)
- "Please help my friend who's struggling..." (Supplication)

**6. Be Baptized**
Baptism is your public declaration of faith.

- Talk to your pastor about baptism
- Understand what it symbolizes (see section below)
- Schedule your baptism

**7. Start Giving**
God calls us to give financially to support His work.

- Start with whatever you can afford
- Work toward giving 10% (a tithe) of your income
- Give to your local church first

*"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."* - 2 Corinthians 9:7

## Your First 60 Days

### Goals for Month 2

**1. Read a Second Book of the Bible**
After finishing John, choose one of these:

**Option A: Gospel of Mark**
- Shortest Gospel
- Fast-paced account of Jesus' life
- 16 chapters

**Option B: Philippians**
- Letter about joy
- Practical and encouraging
- 4 chapters

**Option C: 1 John**
- Written by same author as Gospel of John
- Short letter about assurance and love
- 5 chapters

**2. Begin Serving**
Find a way to serve at your church.

- Volunteer in children's ministry
- Join the welcome team
- Help with setup or cleanup
- Serve at a community outreach

**Why Serve?**
- Discover your spiritual gifts
- Build relationships
- Grow in humility
- Use your new life to bless others

**3. Share Your Faith**
Look for opportunities to tell others about Jesus.

- Share your testimony with a friend or family member
- Invite someone to church
- Post about your faith on social media

See: [How to Share Your Faith](how-to-share-your-faith.md)

**4. Address Relationships**
Evaluate your relationships in light of your new faith.

**Toxic Relationships**:
- You may need to distance yourself from people who pull you toward sin
- Pray for them, but protect your faith
- *"Bad company corrupts good character"* (1 Corinthians 15:33)

**Family and Friends Who Don't Believe**:
- Be patient and loving
- Let your changed life speak
- Share when opportunities arise, but don't be pushy

**Dating Relationships**:
- If you're dating a non-Christian, this is a serious issue
- God calls believers not to be "unequally yoked" (2 Corinthians 6:14)
- Seek counsel from mature Christians

**5. Memorize Three More Verses**
Build on your Scripture memory.

**Suggested Verses**:
- Romans 8:1 - "Therefore, there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus."
- Ephesians 2:8-9 - "For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith..."
- Philippians 4:13 - "I can do all this through him who gives me strength."

**6. Learn About Spiritual Disciplines**
Spiritual growth requires intentional practices.

**Key Disciplines**:
- **Bible Reading**: Daily intake of God's Word
- **Prayer**: Ongoing conversation with God
- **Worship**: Singing, praising, adoring God
- **Fasting**: Going without food to focus on God
- **Solitude**: Quiet time alone with God
- **Service**: Using your gifts to serve others
- **Giving**: Generous financial stewardship
- **Evangelism**: Sharing your faith

Start with Bible reading and prayer, then add others gradually.

## Your First 90 Days

### Goals for Month 3

**1. Read Through a Longer Book**
Challenge yourself with a meatier book.

**Option A: Romans**
- Paul's theological masterpiece
- Explanation of the gospel
- 16 chapters

**Option B: Acts**
- History of the early church
- Exciting narrative
- 28 chapters (read 1 chapter per day)

**Option C: Genesis**
- Beginning of everything
- Foundational stories (Creation, Abraham, Joseph)
- 50 chapters

**2. Get Baptized (If You Haven't Yet)**
Don't delay obedience.

- Baptism doesn't save you, but Jesus commands it
- It's your public testimony
- It's a celebration of your new life

**3. Pursue Church Membership**
Formally commit to a local church.

- Attend membership class
- Meet with church leaders
- Sign the membership covenant

**Why Membership Matters**:
- Biblical model of commitment (Acts 2:41-47)
- Provides accountability
- Gives you a voice in church decisions
- Shows you're all-in

**4. Find a Mentor**
Ask a mature Christian to meet with you regularly.

- Look for someone of the same gender
- Meet weekly or bi-weekly
- Read a book together
- Be honest about your struggles

**5. Develop a Consistent Quiet Time**
By now, daily Bible reading and prayer should be a habit.

**If You're Struggling**:
- Set a specific time and place
- Use a devotional or reading plan
- Ask for accountability
- Remember: it's about relationship, not ritual

**6. Learn Basic Doctrine**
Understand what you believe and why.

**Essential Doctrines**:
- The Trinity (God is three persons: Father, Son, Holy Spirit)
- The deity of Christ (Jesus is fully God and fully man)
- Salvation by grace through faith (not by works)
- The authority of Scripture (the Bible is God's Word)
- The resurrection of Jesus (He literally rose from the dead)
- The return of Christ (Jesus is coming back)

**Resources**:
- Ask your pastor for a book recommendation
- Take a new believers class
- Read basic theology book like "Basic Christianity" by John Stott

**7. Identify Your Spiritual Gifts**
God has given you abilities to serve His church.

**Common Spiritual Gifts**:
- Teaching, leadership, mercy, giving, hospitality, evangelism, encouragement, service, faith, wisdom, discernment

**How to Discover Yours**:
- Serve in different areas
- Ask mature Christians what they see in you
- Notice what you're passionate about
- Take a spiritual gifts assessment

## Building Essential Habits

### The Big Three Daily Habits

**1. Bible Reading**
*"Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path."* - Psalm 119:105

- Set a specific time (morning is best)
- Have a plan (don't just open randomly)
- Use a journal to write down insights
- Ask: What does this teach me about God? About myself?

**Bible Reading Plans**:
- **Chronological**: Read the Bible in the order events happened
- **Canonical**: Genesis to Revelation in order
- **Thematic**: Study specific topics or themes
- **Book-by-Book**: Read one book at a time, all the way through

**2. Prayer**
*"Pray continually."* - 1 Thessalonians 5:17

- Morning: Start your day with God
- Throughout the day: Brief prayers as needs arise
- Evening: Review your day with God

**Prayer Tips**:
- Be honest (God knows your thoughts anyway)
- Be specific (not just "bless everyone")
- Pray Scripture back to God
- Keep a prayer journal

**3. Church Attendance**
*"And let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together."* - Hebrews 10:24-25

- Commit to every Sunday unless providentially hindered
- Don't let convenience dictate your attendance
- Participate (sing, take notes, engage)
- Serve, don't just attend

### The Weekly Rhythm

**Sunday**:
- Worship service
- Small group or Sunday school
- Review sermon notes later in the week

**Midweek**:
- Attend a Bible study or small group meeting
- Meet with your mentor or accountability partner

**Throughout the Week**:
- Daily Bible reading and prayer
- Look for opportunities to serve
- Share your faith naturally

## Common Questions and Struggles

### "I don't feel any different. Is my salvation real?"

**Answer**: Salvation is not based on feelings; it's based on God's promise.

*"If you declare with your mouth, 'Jesus is Lord,' and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved."* - Romans 10:9

Did you believe and confess? Then you are saved, whether you "feel" saved or not.

**Feelings**:
- Can fluctuate based on circumstances, sleep, diet, etc.
- Are not reliable indicators of spiritual reality
- Will grow as you mature in faith

**Focus on**: God's promises, not your feelings.

### "I keep sinning. Have I lost my salvation?"

**Answer**: No. Christians still struggle with sin, but they don't stay in it comfortably.

*"If we claim to be without sin, we deceive ourselves and the truth is not in us."* - 1 John 1:8

**The Difference**:
- **Before salvation**: You were a slave to sin
- **After salvation**: Sin is no longer your master, but you still battle it

**What to Do When You Sin**:
1. Confess it to God immediately (1 John 1:9)
2. Turn away from it (repent)
3. Accept God's forgiveness
4. Get back up and keep following Jesus

**Growth is a Process**: You won't be perfect overnight. Sanctification (becoming more like Jesus) takes a lifetime.

### "The Bible is confusing. I don't understand what I'm reading."

**Answer**: That's normal! The Bible was written thousands of years ago in different cultures.

**Tips for Understanding**:
- **Start with easier books** (Gospels, Psalms, Proverbs)
- **Use a study Bible** with notes that explain difficult passages
- **Ask questions** in your small group or to mature Christians
- **Pray for understanding** (Psalm 119:18)
- **Don't get stuck** - if you don't understand something, keep reading

**Remember**: You don't need to understand everything to benefit from the Bible. Even what you do understand will transform you.

### "I don't know how to pray. What should I say?"

**Answer**: Prayer is just talking to God. There's no magic formula.

**Be Yourself**:
- God wants honesty, not fancy words
- Tell Him what's on your heart
- Ask Him for what you need

**If You're Stuck, Use Scripture**:
- Pray the Lord's Prayer (Matthew 6:9-13)
- Pray through the Psalms
- Pray Paul's prayers from his letters

### "My friends and family think I'm crazy."

**Answer**: Jesus warned this would happen.

*"If the world hates you, keep in mind that it hated me first."* - John 15:18

**How to Respond**:
- Love them anyway
- Pray for them
- Let your changed life be your witness
- Don't be defensive or argumentative
- Trust God with the results

**Remember**: Their rejection isn't about you—it's about Jesus.

### "I'm being tempted to go back to my old life."

**Answer**: Temptation is normal, but you don't have to give in.

*"No temptation has overtaken you except what is common to mankind. And God is faithful; he will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear. But when you are tempted, he will also provide a way out so that you can endure it."* - 1 Corinthians 10:13

**Strategies for Fighting Temptation**:
- **Flee**: Remove yourself from tempting situations (2 Timothy 2:22)
- **Scripture**: Use God's Word as your weapon (Matthew 4:1-11)
- **Accountability**: Tell someone you're struggling
- **Prayer**: Ask God for strength in the moment
- **Replace**: Fill your life with good things, not just empty space

### "I'm not growing as fast as I thought I would."

**Answer**: Spiritual growth is more like a marathon than a sprint.

**Be Patient with Yourself**:
- You won't change overnight
- Growth happens gradually
- Some seasons are faster than others

**Keep Doing the Basics**:
- Bible reading
- Prayer
- Church involvement
- Serving others

**Trust the Process**: God isn't finished with you yet (Philippians 1:6).

## Next Steps: Baptism and Church Membership

### Baptism: Your Public Declaration

**What is Baptism?**
Baptism is an outward symbol of an inward reality. It represents:
- Death to your old life
- Burial with Christ
- Resurrection to new life

**Why Get Baptized?**
- **Jesus commands it**: "Therefore go and make disciples... baptizing them" (Matthew 28:19)
- **Jesus modeled it**: He was baptized Himself (Matthew 3:13-17)
- **Early Christians did it**: Every convert in Acts was baptized

**When Should You Be Baptized?**
- As soon as possible after salvation
- In the Bible, people were baptized immediately (Acts 8:36-38; Acts 16:33)

**Does Baptism Save You?**
No. You're saved by faith alone. Baptism is an act of obedience that follows salvation.

**What If I Was Baptized as a Baby?**
Many Christians believe baptism should follow a personal decision to follow Jesus. If you were baptized as an infant before you could believe, many churches would encourage "believer's baptism."

Talk to your pastor about your church's beliefs on this.

### Church Membership: Your Formal Commitment

**What is Church Membership?**
A formal commitment to a local body of believers, where you:
- Submit to church leadership
- Commit to regular attendance
- Agree to the church's statement of faith
- Use your gifts to serve

**Why Join a Church?**
- **Biblical pattern**: Early Christians were identified with local churches (Acts 2:41-47)
- **Accountability**: Membership provides spiritual oversight (Hebrews 13:17)
- **Responsibility**: You commit to care for others (1 Corinthians 12:12-27)
- **Protection**: The church guards your faith and doctrine

**How Do You Join?**
Each church has its own process, but typically:
1. Attend a membership class
2. Meet with church leaders
3. Give your testimony
4. Sign a membership covenant
5. Be publicly welcomed by the congregation

## Red Flags: What to Avoid

### False Teaching

Be careful about what you listen to and who you follow.

**Warning Signs of False Teachers**:
- Deny Jesus is God
- Say you can earn salvation by works
- Twist Scripture to fit their agenda
- Focus on prosperity and health instead of holiness
- Claim special revelation beyond the Bible

*"But even if we or an angel from heaven should preach a gospel other than the one we preached to you, let them be under God's curse!"* - Galatians 1:8

**How to Protect Yourself**:
- Know the Bible for yourself
- Test everything against Scripture (Acts 17:11)
- Ask mature Christians about teachers you're unsure of

### Toxic Christian Culture

Not everything done in the name of Christianity is healthy.

**Watch Out For**:
- **Legalism**: Adding rules beyond Scripture
- **Judgmentalism**: Harsh, condemning attitudes
- **Manipulation**: Using guilt or fear to control
- **Spiritual abuse**: Leaders who dominate and harm
- **Hyper-spirituality**: Ignoring wisdom and common sense

**Remember**: Jesus came to give life abundantly (John 10:10), not to burden you with man-made rules.

### Isolation

Don't try to follow Jesus alone.

**Dangers of Isolation**:
- Easier to fall into sin
- No accountability
- Miss out on God's design for community
- Vulnerable to deception

*"Two are better than one... If either of them falls down, one can help the other up."* - Ecclesiastes 4:9-10

**Stay Connected**: To a local church, a small group, and individual believers.

## You're Not Alone

### You Have the Holy Spirit

The same Spirit who raised Jesus from the dead lives in you (Romans 8:11).

**He Will**:
- Guide you into truth (John 16:13)
- Convict you of sin (John 16:8)
- Comfort you in trials (John 14:26)
- Empower you for service (Acts 1:8)
- Transform you into Christ's image (2 Corinthians 3:18)

### You Have the Church

You're part of a global family of believers.

**The Church Will**:
- Teach you God's Word
- Encourage you when you're discouraged
- Correct you when you stray
- Celebrate with you in victories
- Mourn with you in losses

### You Have Scripture

*"All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness, so that the servant of God may be thoroughly equipped for every good work."* - 2 Timothy 3:16-17

God has given you everything you need in His Word.

### You Have God Himself

*"Never will I leave you; never will I forsake you."* - Hebrews 13:5

God will never abandon you. You are His child forever.

## Final Encouragement

You've just begun the greatest adventure of your life. Following Jesus is not always easy, but it's always worth it.

**Remember**:
- Growth takes time
- You will stumble, but God will lift you up
- You're not alone
- God is faithful to complete what He started in you (Philippians 1:6)

**Stay the Course**:
- Read your Bible daily
- Pray continually
- Connect with a local church
- Serve others
- Share your faith

**Trust the Process**: God is working in you, even when you can't see it.

*"Being confident of this, that he who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus."* - Philippians 1:6

**Welcome to the family! You're home.**
