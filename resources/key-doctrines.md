# Key Bible Doctrines

A comprehensive guide to essential Christian beliefs based on Scripture. Understanding what the Bible teaches about core doctrines strengthens your faith and equips you to explain what you believe and why.

---

## Why Doctrine Matters

**"All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness." - 2 Timothy 3:16**

**Doctrine is simply organized biblical truth.** It's not dry theology—it's understanding who God is, who you are, and how you're saved. Sound doctrine:
- Protects you from false teaching
- Strengthens your faith
- Equips you to teach others
- Provides confidence in what you believe
- Transforms how you live

## The Bible (Scripture)

### What We Believe

**The Bible is God's inspired, inerrant, authoritative, and sufficient Word.**

### Biblical Support

**Inspiration - God-breathed**
- **2 Timothy 3:16** - "All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness"
- **2 Peter 1:20-21** - "No prophecy of Scripture came about by the prophet's own interpretation of things. For prophecy never had its origin in the human will, but prophets, though human, spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit"

**Authority - God's Word is our final authority**
- **Matthew 4:4** - "Man shall not live on bread alone, but on every word that comes from the mouth of God"
- **2 Timothy 3:17** - "So that the servant of God may be thoroughly equipped for every good work"
- **Hebrews 4:12** - "For the word of God is alive and active. Sharper than any double-edged sword"

**Inerrancy - Without error in original manuscripts**
- **Psalm 19:7** - "The law of the LORD is perfect, refreshing the soul"
- **Proverbs 30:5** - "Every word of God is flawless"
- **John 17:17** - "Your word is truth"

**Sufficiency - Everything we need for faith and life**
- **Psalm 119:105** - "Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path"
- **2 Peter 1:3** - "His divine power has given us everything we need for a godly life"

### Key Points

- The Bible is God's self-revelation
- Written by human authors, but ultimately authored by God
- 66 books, written over 1,500 years, yet unified in message
- Old and New Testaments both essential
- Scripture interprets Scripture

### Common Questions

**Q: What about translations?**
A: Original manuscripts (Hebrew/Aramaic/Greek) were inspired and inerrant. Good translations faithfully represent the originals.

**Q: Hasn't the Bible been changed over time?**
A: No. Manuscript evidence shows remarkable preservation. Minor copyist errors don't affect any major doctrine.

**Q: How do we know these 66 books belong?**
A: The canon was recognized (not created) by the church based on apostolic authorship, widespread acceptance, and consistency with known revelation.

### Application

- Read the Bible as God speaking to you
- Trust it completely
- Obey what it teaches
- Let it be your final authority for faith and practice

## God (Theology Proper)

### What We Believe

**There is one God, eternal, infinite, perfect, unchanging, all-knowing, all-powerful, present everywhere, completely holy, just, and loving.**

### Biblical Support

**One God (Monotheism)**
- **Deuteronomy 6:4** - "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one"
- **Isaiah 45:5** - "I am the LORD, and there is no other; apart from me there is no God"
- **1 Timothy 2:5** - "For there is one God and one mediator between God and mankind"

**Eternal**
- **Psalm 90:2** - "From everlasting to everlasting you are God"
- **Revelation 1:8** - "I am the Alpha and the Omega... who is, and who was, and who is to come"

**Omniscient (All-Knowing)**
- **Psalm 139:1-4** - "You know when I sit and when I rise... Before a word is on my tongue you, LORD, know it completely"
- **1 John 3:20** - "God is greater than our hearts, and he knows everything"

**Omnipotent (All-Powerful)**
- **Jeremiah 32:17** - "Ah, Sovereign LORD, you have made the heavens and the earth by your great power... Nothing is too hard for you"
- **Matthew 19:26** - "With God all things are possible"

**Omnipresent (Everywhere Present)**
- **Psalm 139:7-10** - "Where can I go from your Spirit? Where can I flee from your presence?"
- **Jeremiah 23:24** - "Do not I fill heaven and earth?"

**Holy**
- **Isaiah 6:3** - "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD Almighty"
- **1 Peter 1:15-16** - "Be holy, because I am holy"

**Just**
- **Deuteronomy 32:4** - "All his ways are just. A faithful God who does no wrong"
- **Romans 2:6** - "God will repay each person according to what they have done"

**Loving**
- **1 John 4:8, 16** - "God is love"
- **Romans 5:8** - "God demonstrates his own love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us"

**Gracious and Merciful**
- **Exodus 34:6-7** - "The LORD, the LORD, the compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, abounding in love and faithfulness"

**Unchanging**
- **Malachi 3:6** - "I the LORD do not change"
- **James 1:17** - "Every good and perfect gift is from above... who does not change like shifting shadows"

### Key Attributes

**Incommunicable** (belong to God alone):
- Eternal, infinite, unchanging, self-existent, omniscient, omnipotent, omnipresent

**Communicable** (shared in limited way with humans):
- Love, holiness, justice, mercy, grace, wisdom, faithfulness

### Application

- Worship God for who He is
- Trust His power, wisdom, and love
- Rest in His unchanging character
- Live holy as He is holy

## The Trinity

### What We Believe

**There is one God who eternally exists in three distinct persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Each is fully God, yet there is only one God.**

### Biblical Support

**One God in Three Persons**
- **Matthew 28:19** - "Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"
- **2 Corinthians 13:14** - "May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all"
- **1 Peter 1:2** - "Who have been chosen according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, through the sanctifying work of the Spirit, to be obedient to Jesus Christ"

**The Father is God**
- **John 6:27** - "God the Father has set his seal of approval"
- **1 Corinthians 8:6** - "Yet for us there is but one God, the Father"

**The Son is God**
- **John 1:1** - "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God"
- **John 20:28** - Thomas: "My Lord and my God!"
- **Colossians 2:9** - "For in Christ all the fullness of the Deity lives in bodily form"
- **Hebrews 1:3** - "The Son is the radiance of God's glory and the exact representation of his being"

**The Holy Spirit is God**
- **Acts 5:3-4** - "You have lied to the Holy Spirit... You have not lied just to human beings but to God"
- **1 Corinthians 2:10-11** - "The Spirit searches all things, even the deep things of God"

**Three Distinct Persons**
- **Matthew 3:16-17** - At Jesus' baptism: Spirit descends, Father speaks, Son is baptized
- **John 14:16** - Jesus prays to Father to send "another" Helper (Spirit)
- **John 17** - Jesus prays to the Father

### Key Points

- Not three gods (that's tri-theism)
- Not one person in three modes (that's modalism)
- Mystery we accept by faith, though beyond full comprehension
- All three involved in creation, salvation, and ongoing work

### Application

- Know God as Father, Savior (Son), and indwelling presence (Spirit)
- Don't neglect any person of the Trinity
- Worship one God in three persons

## Jesus Christ (Christology)

### What We Believe

**Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God who became fully human while remaining fully God. He lived a sinless life, died as a substitutionary sacrifice for sin, rose bodily from the dead, ascended to heaven, and will return.**

### Biblical Support

**Deity of Christ - Fully God**
- **John 1:1, 14** - "The Word was God... The Word became flesh"
- **Philippians 2:6** - "Who, being in very nature God"
- **Colossians 1:15-16** - "The image of the invisible God... by him all things were created"
- **Hebrews 1:3** - "The exact representation of his being"

**Humanity of Christ - Fully Human**
- **John 1:14** - "The Word became flesh and made his dwelling among us"
- **Philippians 2:7-8** - "Made himself nothing... being made in human likeness"
- **Hebrews 2:14, 17** - "He too shared in their humanity... made fully human in every way"
- **Hebrews 4:15** - "Tempted in every way, just as we are"

**Virgin Birth**
- **Isaiah 7:14** - "The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son"
- **Matthew 1:22-23** - Fulfilled
- **Luke 1:34-35** - Mary: "How will this be?" Angel: "The Holy Spirit will come on you"

**Sinless Life**
- **2 Corinthians 5:21** - "God made him who had no sin to be sin for us"
- **Hebrews 4:15** - "Tempted in every way... yet he did not sin"
- **1 Peter 2:22** - "He committed no sin"

**Substitutionary Death**
- **Isaiah 53:5-6** - "He was pierced for our transgressions... the LORD has laid on him the iniquity of us all"
- **Mark 10:45** - "The Son of Man... came to give his life as a ransom for many"
- **1 Peter 2:24** - "He himself bore our sins in his body on the cross"
- **1 Peter 3:18** - "Christ died for sins once for all, the righteous for the unrighteous"

**Bodily Resurrection**
- **Matthew 28:6** - "He has risen, just as he said"
- **Luke 24:39** - "Touch me and see; a ghost does not have flesh and bones, as you see I have"
- **1 Corinthians 15:3-4** - "Christ died... was buried... was raised on the third day"

**Ascension**
- **Luke 24:51** - "He was taken up into heaven"
- **Acts 1:9-11** - Disciples watched Him ascend
- **Hebrews 1:3** - "He sat down at the right hand of the Majesty in heaven"

**Second Coming**
- **Acts 1:11** - "This same Jesus... will come back in the same way"
- **Revelation 19:11-16** - Christ returns as conquering King

### Key Points

**Hypostatic Union** - Two natures (fully God and fully man) in one person
- Not half-God, half-man
- Not God disguised as man
- Fully both, yet one person

**Titles of Christ:**
- Son of God (deity)
- Son of Man (humanity)
- Messiah/Christ (Anointed One)
- Savior, Lord, Redeemer, Lamb of God, Lion of Judah, King of Kings

### Application

- Jesus is the only way to God (John 14:6)
- His death paid for your sins completely
- His resurrection guarantees your resurrection
- Trust Him as Lord and Savior

## The Holy Spirit (Pneumatology)

### What We Believe

**The Holy Spirit is the third person of the Trinity, fully God, who convicts of sin, regenerates believers, indwells them, seals them, sanctifies them, and empowers them for service.**

### Biblical Support

**Personality** (Not just a force)
- **John 14:16** - "Another Helper" (person)
- **John 16:13** - "He will guide you" (personal pronouns)
- **Ephesians 4:30** - "Do not grieve the Holy Spirit" (can be grieved)
- **Acts 5:3** - "You have lied to the Holy Spirit" (can be lied to)

**Deity** (Fully God)
- **Acts 5:3-4** - Lying to Spirit = lying to God
- **1 Corinthians 2:10-11** - Knows thoughts of God
- **1 Corinthians 3:16** - "You yourselves are God's temple... God's Spirit dwells in your midst"

**Work of the Holy Spirit:**

**Conviction**
- **John 16:8** - "He will prove the world to be in the wrong about sin and righteousness and judgment"

**Regeneration** (New birth)
- **John 3:5-6** - "No one can enter the kingdom of God unless they are born of... the Spirit"
- **Titus 3:5** - "He saved us through the washing of rebirth and renewal by the Holy Spirit"

**Indwelling**
- **Romans 8:9** - "The Spirit of God lives in you"
- **1 Corinthians 6:19** - "Your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit"

**Sealing**
- **Ephesians 1:13-14** - "You were marked in him with a seal, the promised Holy Spirit, who is a deposit guaranteeing our inheritance"
- **Ephesians 4:30** - "Do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, with whom you were sealed"

**Baptism** (Placed into body of Christ)
- **1 Corinthians 12:13** - "For we were all baptized by one Spirit so as to form one body"

**Filling** (Ongoing experience of control)
- **Ephesians 5:18** - "Be filled with the Spirit"
- **Acts 4:31** - "They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke the word of God boldly"

**Sanctification**
- **2 Thessalonians 2:13** - "God chose you... to be saved through the sanctifying work of the Spirit"
- **Galatians 5:22-23** - Produces fruit: "love, joy, peace, forbearance, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control"

**Empowerment**
- **Acts 1:8** - "You will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you"
- **1 Corinthians 12:7-11** - Distributes spiritual gifts

**Teaching**
- **John 14:26** - "The Holy Spirit... will teach you all things and will remind you of everything I have said to you"
- **1 Corinthians 2:12-13** - "The Spirit... teaches, expressing spiritual truths in spiritual words"

**Intercession**
- **Romans 8:26-27** - "The Spirit helps us in our weakness... the Spirit intercedes for God's people"

### Application

- Don't grieve the Spirit (Ephesians 4:30) - Don't sin
- Don't quench the Spirit (1 Thessalonians 5:19) - Don't resist His work
- Be filled with the Spirit (Ephesians 5:18) - Let Him control you
- Walk by the Spirit (Galatians 5:16) - Depend on Him daily
- Keep in step with the Spirit (Galatians 5:25) - Follow His leading

## Humanity and Sin

### What We Believe About Humanity

**Humans are created in God's image, with inherent dignity and value, but are fallen and sinful, separated from God, and in need of redemption.**

### Biblical Support

**Created in God's Image**
- **Genesis 1:26-27** - "Let us make mankind in our image... So God created mankind in his own image"
- **Genesis 2:7** - "The LORD God formed a man from the dust... and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life"

**Purpose**
- **Genesis 1:28** - "Be fruitful and increase... fill the earth and subdue it"
- **Psalm 8:5-6** - "You made them a little lower than the angels... You made them rulers over the works of your hands"

**The Fall**
- **Genesis 3** - Adam and Eve sinned; humanity fell
- **Romans 5:12** - "Sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin, and in this way death came to all people"

### What We Believe About Sin

**Sin is rebellion against God, falling short of His glory, breaking His law, and missing the mark.**

**Universal**
- **Romans 3:23** - "All have sinned and fall short of the glory of God"
- **1 John 1:8** - "If we claim to be without sin, we deceive ourselves"

**Inherited Sin Nature**
- **Psalm 51:5** - "Surely I was sinful at birth, sinful from the time my mother conceived me"
- **Romans 5:19** - "Through the disobedience of the one man the many were made sinners"

**Personal Sin**
- **James 4:17** - "If anyone, then, knows the good they ought to do and doesn't do it, it is sin for them"
- **1 John 3:4** - "Everyone who sins breaks the law; in fact, sin is lawlessness"

**Consequences of Sin**
- **Spiritual Death:** Ephesians 2:1 - "You were dead in your transgressions and sins"
- **Physical Death:** Romans 6:23 - "The wages of sin is death"
- **Eternal Separation:** Revelation 20:15 - "Anyone whose name was not found written in the book of life was thrown into the lake of fire"

### Application

- You are valuable (made in God's image)
- You are sinful (fallen nature)
- You need a Savior (can't save yourself)
- God provides redemption (Jesus)

## Salvation (Soteriology)

### What We Believe

**Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. It is entirely God's work, not earned by human effort.**

### Biblical Support

**By Grace Alone**
- **Ephesians 2:8-9** - "For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith—and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God—not by works, so that no one can boast"
- **Titus 3:5** - "He saved us, not because of righteous things we had done, but because of his mercy"

**Through Faith Alone**
- **Romans 3:28** - "For we maintain that a person is justified by faith apart from the works of the law"
- **Galatians 2:16** - "Know that a person is not justified by the works of the law, but by faith in Jesus Christ"
- **John 3:16** - "Whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life"

**In Christ Alone**
- **Acts 4:12** - "Salvation is found in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to mankind by which we must be saved"
- **John 14:6** - "I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me"

**Key Aspects of Salvation:**

**Justification** (Declared Righteous)
- **Romans 5:1** - "Since we have been justified through faith, we have peace with God"
- **Romans 3:24** - "Justified freely by his grace through the redemption that came by Christ Jesus"
- **2 Corinthians 5:21** - "God made him who had no sin to be sin for us, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God"

**Regeneration** (Born Again)
- **John 3:3, 7** - "You must be born again"
- **2 Corinthians 5:17** - "Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!"
- **Titus 3:5** - "He saved us through the washing of rebirth and renewal by the Holy Spirit"

**Sanctification** (Made Holy)
- **1 Thessalonians 5:23** - "May God himself, the God of peace, sanctify you through and through"
- **Hebrews 10:14** - "By one sacrifice he has made perfect forever those who are being made holy"
- **Philippians 1:6** - "He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion"

**Glorification** (Future Perfection)
- **Romans 8:30** - "Those he justified, he also glorified"
- **1 John 3:2** - "When Christ appears, we shall be like him"
- **Philippians 3:21** - "He will transform our lowly bodies so that they will be like his glorious body"

**Eternal Security**
- **John 10:28-29** - "I give them eternal life, and they shall never perish; no one will snatch them out of my hand"
- **Romans 8:38-39** - "Nothing... will be able to separate us from the love of God"
- **Philippians 1:6** - "He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion"

### Application

- You cannot save yourself
- Trust Christ alone, not works
- Salvation is a gift—receive it
- Once saved, you're secure in Christ
- Live in light of your salvation

## The Church (Ecclesiology)

### What We Believe

**The church is the body of all believers in Christ, called out to worship, fellowship, discipleship, and mission.**

### Biblical Support

**Nature of the Church**
- **Matthew 16:18** - "I will build my church"
- **Ephesians 1:22-23** - "The church, which is his body"
- **1 Corinthians 12:13** - "We were all baptized by one Spirit so as to form one body"

**Purpose of the Church**
- **Worship:** Ephesians 1:12 - "For the praise of his glory"
- **Edification:** Ephesians 4:12 - "To equip his people for works of service, so that the body of Christ may be built up"
- **Evangelism:** Matthew 28:19-20 - "Go and make disciples"
- **Fellowship:** Acts 2:42 - "They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and to fellowship"

**Leadership**
- **Elders/Overseers/Pastors:** 1 Timothy 3:1-7; Titus 1:5-9; 1 Peter 5:1-4
- **Deacons:** 1 Timothy 3:8-13; Acts 6:1-6

**Ordinances**
- **Baptism:** Matthew 28:19 - "Baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"
- **Lord's Supper/Communion:** 1 Corinthians 11:23-26 - "Do this in remembrance of me"

**Unity and Diversity**
- **1 Corinthians 12:12-27** - One body, many parts
- **Ephesians 4:4-6** - "One body and one Spirit... one Lord, one faith, one baptism"
- **Romans 12:4-8** - Different gifts, one body

### Application

- Join a local church
- Use your gifts to serve
- Submit to godly leadership
- Pursue unity
- Participate in evangelism

## Last Things (Eschatology)

### What We Believe

**Jesus Christ will return visibly and bodily. There will be a resurrection of all people, final judgment, and an eternal state of either heaven or hell.**

### Biblical Support

**Second Coming of Christ**
- **Acts 1:11** - "This same Jesus, who has been taken from you into heaven, will come back in the same way"
- **1 Thessalonians 4:16** - "The Lord himself will come down from heaven"
- **Revelation 19:11-16** - Christ returns as conquering King

**Resurrection**
- **John 5:28-29** - "All who are in their graves will hear his voice and come out—those who have done what is good will rise to live, and those who have done what is evil will rise to be condemned"
- **1 Corinthians 15:52** - "The dead will be raised imperishable, and we will be changed"

**Judgment**
- **Hebrews 9:27** - "People are destined to die once, and after that to face judgment"
- **Revelation 20:11-15** - Great White Throne Judgment
- **2 Corinthians 5:10** - "We must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ"

**Heaven**
- **John 14:2-3** - "My Father's house has many rooms... I am going there to prepare a place for you"
- **Revelation 21:1-4** - New heaven and new earth; no more death, pain, or tears
- **1 Corinthians 2:9** - "No eye has seen, no ear has heard... what God has prepared for those who love him"

**Hell**
- **Matthew 25:46** - "They will go away to eternal punishment, but the righteous to eternal life"
- **Revelation 20:14-15** - "The lake of fire... Anyone whose name was not found written in the book of life was thrown into the lake of fire"
- **Matthew 25:41** - "Depart from me, you who are cursed, into the eternal fire"

### Application

- Live in light of Christ's return
- Share the gospel urgently
- Don't fear death if you're in Christ
- Heaven is real—so is hell
- This world is not your home
