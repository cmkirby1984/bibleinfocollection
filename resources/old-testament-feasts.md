# Old Testament Feasts and Festivals

Understanding how Jewish feasts foreshadow Christ and reveal God's redemptive plan.

---

## Why Study the Feasts?

**"These are the LORD's appointed festivals... which you are to proclaim as sacred assemblies." - Leviticus 23:2**

The seven feasts of Israel are not just ancient history—they're prophetic pictures of Christ's work:
- **Spring Feasts** (fulfilled at Christ's first coming)
- **Fall Feasts** (point to Christ's second coming)

---

## The Seven Feasts of Israel

### 1. Passover (Pesach) - Leviticus 23:5

**When:** 14th day of Nisan (March/April)

**Historical Meaning:**
- Commemor

ates Israel's deliverance from Egypt
- Lamb's blood on doorposts saved firstborn
- Death "passed over" homes with blood (Exodus 12)

**Fulfilled in Christ:**
- **John 1:29** - "Behold, the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world"
- **1 Corinthians 5:7** - "Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed"
- Jesus crucified on Passover
- His blood saves from spiritual death
- Perfect, spotless Lamb

**Application:** Christ is your Passover. His blood protects you from God's judgment.

---

### 2. Unleavened Bread (Hag HaMatzot) - Leviticus 23:6-8

**When:** 15-21 Nisan (immediately after Passover)

**Historical Meaning:**
- Seven days eating bread without yeast
- Reminded Israel of quick exodus (no time for bread to rise)
- Leaven symbolizes sin

**Fulfilled in Christ:**
- Jesus buried during Feast of Unleavened Bread
- His body in tomb (like grain in earth)
- **John 6:35** - "I am the bread of life"
- **1 Corinthians 5:7-8** - "Get rid of the old yeast... Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed. Therefore let us keep the Festival... with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth"
- Jesus lived sinless life (no leaven/sin)

**Application:** Remove sin from your life. Live in purity through Christ.

---

### 3. Firstfruits (Yom HaBikkurim) - Leviticus 23:9-14

**When:** Day after Sabbath during Unleavened Bread week

**Historical Meaning:**
- First sheaf of barley harvest brought to priest
- Waved before the Lord
- Beginning of harvest

**Fulfilled in Christ:**
- **1 Corinthians 15:20, 23** - "Christ has indeed been raised from the dead, the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep"
- Jesus rose on Firstfruits (Sunday after Sabbath)
- His resurrection guarantees ours
- Beginning of resurrection harvest

**Application:** Because Christ rose, you will too. He is the firstfruits; you're part of the harvest.

---

### 4. Pentecost / Weeks (Shavuot) - Leviticus 23:15-22

**When:** 50 days after Firstfruits

**Historical Meaning:**
- Celebrated wheat harvest
- Commemorates giving of Law at Sinai
- Two loaves of leavened bread waved

**Fulfilled in Christ:**
- **Acts 2** - Holy Spirit came on Pentecost
- 50 days after Jesus' resurrection
- Birth of the church
- Two loaves = Jew and Gentile united
- Law written on hearts by Spirit

**Application:** The Holy Spirit indwells you. You're part of the harvest. Jews and Gentiles are one in Christ.

---

*50-day gap between Spring and Fall Feasts represents church age*

---

### 5. Trumpets / Rosh Hashanah - Leviticus 23:23-25

**When:** 1st day of Tishrei (Sept/Oct) - Jewish New Year

**Historical Meaning:**
- Day of trumpet blasts
- Call to repentance
- Beginning of ten "Days of Awe"

**Future Fulfillment:**
- **1 Thessalonians 4:16** - "The Lord himself will come down from heaven, with a loud command, with the voice of the archangel and with the trumpet call of God"
- **1 Corinthians 15:52** - "In a flash, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound, the dead will be raised imperishable, and we will be changed"
- Rapture/gathering of believers
- Awakening call

**Application:** Be ready. The trumpet will sound. Christ is coming for His church.

---

### 6. Day of Atonement (Yom Kippur) - Leviticus 23:26-32

**When:** 10th of Tishrei (10 days after Trumpets)

**Historical Meaning:**
- Holiest day of year
- High priest entered Holy of Holies
- Blood sprinkled on mercy seat
- Scapegoat sent into wilderness
- National cleansing from sin

**Fulfilled/Future:**
**Past:** **Hebrews 9:12** - "He did not enter by means of the blood of goats and calves; but he entered the Most Holy Place once for all by his own blood, thus obtaining eternal redemption"

**Future:** Israel's national salvation
- **Zechariah 12:10** - "They will look on me, the one they have pierced, and they will mourn for him"
- **Romans 11:26** - "All Israel will be saved"
- Christ's second coming to Israel

**Application:** Christ made atonement once for all. At His return, Israel will recognize their Messiah.

---

### 7. Tabernacles / Booths (Sukkot) - Leviticus 23:33-43

**When:** 15-21 Tishrei (5 days after Yom Kippur)

**Historical Meaning:**
- Lived in temporary booths (sukkot)
- Remembered wilderness wanderings
- Celebrated fall harvest
- Joyful feast

**Future Fulfillment:**
- **John 1:14** - "The Word became flesh and made his dwelling [tabernacled] among us"
- **Revelation 21:3** - "Look! God's dwelling place is now among the people, and he will dwell with them"
- **Zechariah 14:16** - "Then the survivors from all the nations... will go up year after year to worship the King, the LORD Almighty, and to celebrate the Festival of Tabernacles"
- Millennium / Eternal state
- God dwelling with humanity

**Application:** Anticipate when God will dwell with us forever. This world is temporary; we're heading to permanent tabernacle.

---

## Summary: Prophetic Fulfillment

### Spring Feasts (FULFILLED at First Coming)
1. **Passover** → Christ's crucifixion
2. **Unleavened Bread** → Christ's sinless life & burial
3. **Firstfruits** → Christ's resurrection
4. **Pentecost** → Holy Spirit's coming

### Fall Feasts (FUTURE fulfillment at Second Coming)
5. **Trumpets** → Rapture / Gathering
6. **Atonement** → Israel's salvation / Second Coming
7. **Tabernacles** → Millennium / Eternal dwelling

**All feasts fulfilled with exact precision on the actual feast day!**

---

## Additional Feasts (Not in Leviticus 23)

### Purim - Esther 9:26-28
- Celebrates deliverance from Haman's plot
- Queen Esther saved the Jews
- Picture of God's providence

### Hanukkah (Dedication) - John 10:22
- Rededication of temple (164 BC)
- Jesus walked in temple during this feast
- Jesus is Light of the World

---

## How to Study the Feasts

1. **Read Leviticus 23** (primary chapter)
2. **Study Exodus 12** (Passover details)
3. **Find NT fulfillment** (Gospels, Acts, Revelation)
4. **Note timing** - Jesus fulfilled Spring feasts precisely
5. **Anticipate future** - Fall feasts point to Second Coming

---

## Application for Christians

**We don't have to celebrate these feasts** (Colossians 2:16-17 - they're shadows; Christ is reality)

**But studying them helps us:**
- See Christ throughout the Old Testament
- Understand Jewish context of the Gospels
- Anticipate Christ's return
- Appreciate God's meticulous planning
- Worship Jesus as fulfillment

---

**"These are a shadow of the things that were to come; the reality, however, is found in Christ." - Colossians 2:17**
