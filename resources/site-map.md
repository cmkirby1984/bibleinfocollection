## Old Testament (39 Books)

### The Law (Torah) - 5 Books

1. [Genesis](../old-testament/01-genesis.md) - Creation, fall, patriarchs, Joseph
2. [Exodus](../old-testament/02-exodus.md) - Slavery, Moses, Passover, Ten Commandments
3. [Leviticus](../old-testament/03-leviticus.md) - Sacrifices, holiness, priesthood
4. [Numbers](../old-testament/04-numbers.md) - Wilderness wandering, censuses
5. [Deuteronomy](../old-testament/05-deuteronomy.md) - Moses' final sermons, law repeated

### History - 12 Books

6. [Joshua](../old-testament/06-joshua.md) - Conquest of Canaan
7. [Judges](../old-testament/07-judges.md) - Cycle of sin and deliverance
8. [Ruth](../old-testament/08-ruth.md) - Kinsman-redeemer, loyalty
9. [1 Samuel](../old-testament/09-1-samuel.md) - Samuel, Saul, David's rise
10. [2 Samuel](../old-testament/10-2-samuel.md) - David's reign, Bathsheba
11. [1 Kings](../old-testament/11-1-kings.md) - Solomon, divided kingdom, Elijah
12. [2 Kings](../old-testament/12-2-kings.md) - Elisha, fall of Israel and Judah
13. [1 Chronicles](../old-testament/13-1-chronicles.md) - David's reign (priestly perspective)
14. [2 Chronicles](../old-testament/14-2-chronicles.md) - Solomon to exile
15. [Ezra](../old-testament/15-ezra.md) - Return from exile, temple rebuilt
16. [Nehemiah](../old-testament/16-nehemiah.md) - Walls rebuilt, revival
17. [Esther](../old-testament/17-esther.md) - Providence, courage, "for such a time"

### Wisdom & Poetry - 5 Books

18. [Job](../old-testament/18-job.md) - Suffering, God's sovereignty
19. [Psalms](../old-testament/19-psalms.md) - Worship, prayers, songs
20. [Proverbs](../old-testament/20-proverbs.md) - Practical wisdom for life
21. [Ecclesiastes](../old-testament/21-ecclesiastes.md) - Meaning, "life under the sun"
22. [Song of Solomon](../old-testament/22-song-of-solomon.md) - Love, marriage

### Major Prophets - 5 Books

23. [Isaiah](../old-testament/23-isaiah.md) - Messianic prophecies, "Suffering Servant"
24. [Jeremiah](../old-testament/24-jeremiah.md) - Judgment on Judah, new covenant
25. [Lamentations](../old-testament/25-lamentations.md) - Grief over Jerusalem's fall
26. [Ezekiel](../old-testament/26-ezekiel.md) - Visions, dry bones, glory of God
27. [Daniel](../old-testament/27-daniel.md) - Lions' den, prophecy, faithfulness

### Minor Prophets - 12 Books

28. [Hosea](../old-testament/28-hosea.md) - God's faithful love despite unfaithfulness
29. [Joel](../old-testament/29-joel.md) - Day of the Lord, Spirit poured out
30. [Amos](../old-testament/30-amos.md) - Social justice, judgment
31. [Obadiah](../old-testament/31-obadiah.md) - Judgment on Edom
32. [Jonah](../old-testament/32-jonah.md) - Reluctant prophet, God's mercy
33. [Micah](../old-testament/33-micah.md) - Justice, Bethlehem prophecy
34. [Nahum](../old-testament/34-nahum.md) - Judgment on Nineveh
35. [Habakkuk](../old-testament/35-habakkuk.md) - "Why?", living by faith
36. [Zephaniah](../old-testament/36-zephaniah.md) - Day of the Lord, restoration
37. [Haggai](../old-testament/37-haggai.md) - Rebuild the temple
38. [Zechariah](../old-testament/38-zechariah.md) - Messianic visions, future hope
39. [Malachi](../old-testament/39-malachi.md) - Faithfulness, tithing, Elijah coming

## New Testament (27 Books)

### The Gospels - 4 Books

1. [Matthew](../new-testament/01-matthew.md) - Jesus the King, Sermon on the Mount
2. [Mark](../new-testament/02-mark.md) - Jesus the Servant, action-packed
3. [Luke](../new-testament/03-luke.md) - Jesus the Son of Man, detailed narrative
4. [John](../new-testament/04-john.md) - Jesus the Son of God, "I AM" statements

### History - 1 Book

5. [Acts](../new-testament/05-acts.md) - Early church, Holy Spirit, Paul's journeys

### Paul's Letters to Churches - 9 Books

6. [Romans](../new-testament/06-romans.md) - Systematic theology, justification by faith
7. [1 Corinthians](../new-testament/07-1-corinthians.md) - Church problems, love chapter, spiritual gifts
8. [2 Corinthians](../new-testament/08-2-corinthians.md) - Paul's ministry, suffering, giving
9. [Galatians](../new-testament/09-galatians.md) - Freedom from law, faith not works
10. [Ephesians](../new-testament/10-ephesians.md) - In Christ, armor of God
11. [Philippians](../new-testament/11-philippians.md) - Joy, humility of Christ
12. [Colossians](../new-testament/12-colossians.md) - Christ's supremacy
13. [1 Thessalonians](../new-testament/13-1-thessalonians.md) - Christ's return, encouragement
14. [2 Thessalonians](../new-testament/14-2-thessalonians.md) - Day of the Lord, work ethic

### Paul's Pastoral Letters - 4 Books

15. [1 Timothy](../new-testament/15-1-timothy.md) - Church leadership, sound doctrine
16. [2 Timothy](../new-testament/16-2-timothy.md) - Paul's final words, perseverance
17. [Titus](../new-testament/17-titus.md) - Church order, good works
18. [Philemon](../new-testament/18-philemon.md) - Forgiveness, Christian brotherhood

### General Epistles - 8 Books

19. [Hebrews](../new-testament/19-hebrews.md) - Christ superior to all, faith hall of fame
20. [James](../new-testament/20-james.md) - Practical faith, faith and works
21. [1 Peter](../new-testament/21-1-peter.md) - Suffering, living hope
22. [2 Peter](../new-testament/22-2-peter.md) - False teachers, Christ's return
23. [1 John](../new-testament/23-1-john.md) - Assurance, love, light and darkness
24. [2 John](../new-testament/24-2-john.md) - Truth and love
25. [3 John](../new-testament/25-3-john.md) - Hospitality
26. [Jude](../new-testament/26-jude.md) - Contend for the faith, false teachers

### Prophecy - 1 Book

27. [Revelation](../new-testament/27-revelation.md) - Christ's return, new heaven/earth

## How to Use This Collection

### For Personal Study
1. Start with [Quick Start Guide](quick-start.md) if new
2. Pick a book to study
3. Use [Bible Study Methods](bible-study-methods.md)
4. Reference indexes as needed
5. Journal insights

### For Small Groups
1. Choose a book
2. Use book overview for context
3. Add discussion questions (coming soon to more books!)
4. Reference topical indexes for deeper dives
5. Apply together

### For Teaching/Preaching
1. Read book overview for big picture
2. Consult commentaries (listed in each overview)
3. Use indexes for illustrations
4. Check [Difficult Passages](difficult-passages.md) for objections
5. Cross-reference related topics

### For Evangelism
1. **[The Gospel Explained](gospel-explained.md)** - Your go-to resource
2. [Messianic Prophecies](../indexes/messianic-prophecies.md) - For conversations with Jews
3. [How We Got the Bible](bible-origins.md) - Reliability questions
4. [Difficult Passages](difficult-passages.md) - Answer objections
5. Any Gospel (especially [John](../new-testament/04-john.md))
