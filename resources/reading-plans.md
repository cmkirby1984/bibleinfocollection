# Bible Reading Plans

Practical plans to help you read through Scripture systematically. Choose a plan that fits your goals and schedule.

---

## Why Use a Reading Plan?

**"Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path." - Psalm 119:105**

A reading plan helps you:
- Read the entire Bible, not just favorite parts
- Stay consistent and accountable
- Understand the flow of Scripture
- Build a daily habit
- Complete what you start

**The goal isn't to finish quickly—it's to encounter God in His Word daily.**

---

## One-Year Plans

### 1. Canonical Order (Genesis to Revelation)

**Best for:** First-time Bible readers; want to see biblical storyline

**How it works:** Read straight through, Genesis to Revelation, in 365 days

**Daily reading:** ~3-4 chapters per day

**Advantages:**
- Simple and straightforward
- Follows the Bible's structure
- Easy to track progress

**Challenges:**
- Can slow down in Leviticus/Numbers
- Long prophetic books take time

**Schedule Example:**
- **Jan:** Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus (start)
- **Feb-Mar:** Finish Law, start Historical books
- **Apr-Jun:** Historical books through Chronicles
- **Jul-Aug:** Psalms, Proverbs, wisdom literature
- **Sep-Oct:** Major and Minor Prophets
- **Nov:** Gospels and Acts
- **Dec:** Epistles and Revelation

**Tips:**
- Don't give up in Leviticus! Push through
- Keep reading even if you miss days
- Use audio Bible for difficult sections

---

### 2. Chronological Order

**Best for:** Second-time readers; want to understand biblical timeline

**How it works:** Read events in the order they happened historically

**Examples:**
- Job during patriarchs
- Psalms with David's life
- Prophets alongside Kings/Chronicles
- Paul's letters in order written

**Advantages:**
- Historical context clearer
- See how events connect
- Prophets make more sense alongside history

**Challenges:**
- Jumps between books
- Need a chronological Bible or guide

**Resources:**
- **The One Year Chronological Bible** (NLT or NIV)
- **Blue Letter Bible** has chronological reading plans
- **YouVersion Bible App** (free plan)

---

### 3. M'Cheyne Reading Plan

**Best for:** Motivated readers; want depth

**How it works:** Four chapters per day
- 2 from OT
- 1 from Psalms/Gospels
- 1 from rest of NT

**Result:** OT once, NT & Psalms twice in one year

**Advantages:**
- Variety each day
- Always reading from different sections
- Psalms and NT twice reinforces key truths

**Challenges:**
- Four chapters daily requires commitment
- Can be overwhelming if you miss days

**Download:** Search "M'Cheyne Reading Plan" - many free PDFs available

---

### 4. Discipleship Reading Plan

**Best for:** New believers; want practical focus

**How it works:** Start with foundational books before tackling whole Bible

**Year 1 Focus:**
1. **Gospels** (Mark, John, Matthew, Luke)
2. **Acts** (early church)
3. **Key Epistles** (Romans, Ephesians, Philippians, 1-2 Timothy, James)
4. **Genesis** (beginnings)
5. **Psalms & Proverbs** (devotional)
6. **Selected OT stories** (Ruth, Esther, Daniel)

**Year 2:** Fill in remaining books

**Advantages:**
- Starts with Jesus
- Builds foundation before complex books
- High success rate

## Fast-Track Plans

### 90-Day Bible Reading Challenge

**Best for:** Disciplined readers; want overview quickly

**Daily reading:** 12-15 chapters (~60-90 minutes)

**Schedule:**
- **Days 1-30:** Genesis through 2 Samuel
- **Days 31-60:** 1 Kings through Song of Songs
- **Days 61-75:** Isaiah through Daniel
- **Days 76-82:** Minor Prophets
- **Days 83-87:** Gospels & Acts
- **Days 88-90:** Epistles & Revelation

**Tips:**
- Read fast—don't stop for deep study
- Goal is the big picture
- Listen to audio Bible
- Block out time daily
- Find an accountability partner

---

### 30-Day New Testament

**Best for:** Beginners; want to know Jesus and the gospel

**Daily reading:** ~8 chapters

**Perfect starting point** before tackling whole Bible

## Topical Reading Plans

### Life of Christ

**Focus:** Four Gospels in harmony

**Order:**
1. Mark (shortest, action-packed)
2. Matthew (teaching focused)
3. Luke (detailed, compassionate)
4. John (theological, "I AM" statements)

**Time:** 4-6 weeks (1 Gospel per 7-14 days)

---

### Paul's Letters (Chronological)

**Order written:**
1. Galatians
2. 1 Thessalonians
3. 2 Thessalonians
4. 1 Corinthians
5. 2 Corinthians
6. Romans
7. Ephesians
8. Philippians
9. Colossians
10. Philemon
11. 1 Timothy
12. Titus
13. 2 Timothy

**Best for:** Understanding Paul's developing theology

---

### Wisdom Literature

**Books:** Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Solomon

**Plan:**
- 1 Psalm + 1 Proverbs per day
- Job (42 chapters = 6 weeks)
- Ecclesiastes (12 chapters)
- Song of Solomon (8 chapters)

**Time:** 5-6 months total

**Perfect for:** Daily devotional reading

---

### Prophets and Kings

**Read together:**
- 1-2 Kings alongside prophets
- 1-2 Chronicles for fuller picture

**Example:**
- 1 Kings 12-22 → Obadiah, Joel, Jonah
- 2 Kings 1-17 → Amos, Hosea, Isaiah, Micah
- 2 Kings 18-25 → Nahum, Zephaniah, Jeremiah, Habakkuk, Daniel, Ezekiel

## Specialized Plans

### Psalms & Proverbs (Daily)

**Simple plan:** Read 1 Psalm + 1 Proverbs daily

**Psalms:** 150 psalms = 5 months
**Proverbs:** 31 chapters = read the chapter matching today's date

**Perfect for:** Morning devotions

---

### One Chapter Per Day

**Flexibility:** Read 1 chapter wherever you are in Bible

**Time to complete:** ~3.5 years for whole Bible

**Advantages:**
- Low pressure
- Manageable for busy schedules
- Better than nothing!

---

### "Best of" Beginner Plan

**Great starter if overwhelmed:**

**Weeks 1-2:** Gospel of John
**Week 3:** Acts 1-14
**Week 4:** Romans, Galatians
**Weeks 5-6:** Genesis 1-25 (patriarchs)
**Week 7:** Exodus 1-20
**Week 8:** Psalms (select favorites: 1, 23, 51, 139, etc.)
**Week 9:** Proverbs (wisdom)
**Week 10:** Isaiah 40-55 (Suffering Servant)
**Week 11:** Mark
**Week 12:** Revelation 1-5, 19-22

## Using This Study Guide's Resources

### Book-by-Book Study

**Use our book overviews for deep study:**

1. **Choose a book** from Table of Contents
2. **Read the overview** first
3. **Read the biblical text** with overview as guide

### Topical Study

**Use our Thematic Index:**
1. Choose a topic (faith, prayer, grace, etc.)
2. Look up all references
3. Study in context
4. Synthesize what the whole Bible teaches

**Example: "Prayer" study**
- Week 1: OT prayer passages
- Week 2: Jesus' teaching on prayer
- Week 3: Prayers in Epistles
- Week 4: Personal application

### Character Study

**Use our Character Index:**
- Pick a character
- Read all passages about them
- Note lessons from their life
- Apply to your situation

## Reading Plan Tips

### How to Succeed

**1. Start Small**
- Better to read 1 chapter consistently than 10 chapters sporadically
- Build the habit first, increase later

**2. Same Time, Same Place**
- Morning works for most people
- Before social media!
- Have Bible and coffee ready

**3. Use Multiple Formats**
- Physical Bible for home
- Audio for commute
- App for waiting rooms

**4. Don't Be Legalistic**
- Miss a day? Keep going—don't quit
- Behind schedule? Jump back in wherever
- Goal is relationship, not just completion

**5. Journal As You Go**
- One verse that stood out
- One thing you learned about God
- One application for today

**6. Get Accountability**
- Read with a friend
- Join a Bible reading group
- Use apps that track progress

## When You Get Stuck

**Leviticus/Numbers too detailed?**
- Read summaries in this study guide
- Skim genealogies and measurements
- Focus on key passages
- Push through—it gets better!

**Prophets confusing?**
- Read book overview first
- Understand historical context
- Look for Messianic prophecies
- Don't worry about every detail

**Behind schedule?**
- Don't quit!
- Jump to current date
- Or just keep reading from where you are
