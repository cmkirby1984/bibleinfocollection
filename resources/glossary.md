# Glossary of Biblical Terms

Your guide to understanding theological, biblical, and historical terms

---

## How to Use This Glossary

This glossary defines terms you'll encounter in Bible study. Terms are organized alphabetically and include:
- **Theological terms** (justification, sanctification, etc.)
- **Biblical terms** (covenant, atonement, etc.)
- **Historical/cultural terms** (Pharisees, synagogue, etc.)
- **Hebrew/Greek terms** (Shalom, Agape, etc.)
- **Church terms** (sacrament, canon, etc.)

**Scripture references** are provided where relevant.

---

## A

**Abba** - Aramaic word for "Father," expressing intimacy (like "Daddy"). Used by Jesus (Mark 14:36) and by believers through the Spirit (Romans 8:15).

**Abyss** - The bottomless pit; place of confinement for demons (Luke 8:31; Revelation 9:1-2).

**Advocate** - One who pleads another's case. Jesus is our advocate before the Father (1 John 2:1). The Holy Spirit is our advocate/helper on earth (John 14:16).

**Agape** (Greek: ἀγάπη) - Unconditional, self-sacrificing love. The highest form of love. God's love for us and the love Christians are to have for others (John 3:16; 1 Corinthians 13).

**Alien/Sojourner** - A foreigner living temporarily in a land. Metaphor for Christians living in this world (1 Peter 2:11; Hebrews 11:13).

**Alleluia/Hallelujah** - "Praise the LORD" (Hebrew: *hallelu* = praise + *Yah* = shortened form of Yahweh). Expression of worship.

**Alpha and Omega** - First and last letters of Greek alphabet. Title for Christ emphasizing He is the beginning and end (Revelation 1:8; 21:6; 22:13).

**Amen** - "So be it" or "truly." Affirmation of truth. Jesus used it to emphasize His words ("Truly, truly I say to you...").

**Angel** - "Messenger." Spiritual beings created by God to serve Him and assist believers (Hebrews 1:14). Different from God; not to be worshiped.

**Anoint/Anointing** - To pour oil on someone, symbolizing God's blessing, consecration, or empowerment. Kings, priests, and prophets were anointed. "Messiah" and "Christ" both mean "Anointed One."

**Antichrist** - Opponent of Christ. Can refer to a specific end-times figure (1 John 2:18; 2 Thessalonians 2:3) or spirit of opposition to Christ (1 John 2:22; 4:3).

**Apocalypse** - "Unveiling" or "revelation" (Greek: *apokalypsis*). Refers to the book of Revelation or the literary genre revealing end-times events.

**Apocrypha** - Books written between OT and NT (~400 BC - 100 AD). Included in Catholic Bibles but not Protestant. Not considered inspired by Protestants. See [How We Got the Bible](bible-origins.md).

**Apostle** - "Sent one." Refers to the Twelve chosen by Jesus (Matthew 10:2-4) and Paul (Galatians 1:1). Also used more broadly for missionaries/sent ones (Acts 14:14; Romans 16:7).

**Aramaic** - Language Jesus spoke. Portions of Daniel and Ezra written in Aramaic. Related to Hebrew.

**Archangel** - High-ranking angel. Michael is the only one called archangel in Scripture (Jude 1:9; 1 Thessalonians 4:16).

**Ark of the Covenant** - Sacred chest containing the Ten Commandments, Aaron's rod, and manna. Represented God's presence with Israel. The mercy seat (lid) was where God met with the high priest (Exodus 25:10-22).

**Ascension** - Jesus' return to heaven 40 days after His resurrection (Acts 1:9-11).

**Atonement** - Making amends for sin; reconciliation between God and humanity. "At-one-ment"—making us one with God. Accomplished through Christ's sacrifice (Romans 5:11; Hebrews 2:17).

---

## B

**Baptism** - Immersion in water symbolizing death to sin and new life in Christ. Commanded by Jesus (Matthew 28:19). Identifies believer with Christ's death and resurrection (Romans 6:3-4).

**Beatitudes** - The "blessed are" statements in Jesus' Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 5:3-12).

**Beelzebub** - Name for Satan (Matthew 12:24). Means "lord of the flies."

**Believe/Faith** - Trust and reliance upon. Biblical faith is not mere intellectual assent but active trust in God and His promises (John 3:16; Hebrews 11:1).

**Beloved** - Dearly loved. God's description of Jesus (Matthew 3:17) and of believers (Ephesians 1:6).

**Birthright** - Special privileges of the firstborn son, including double inheritance and family leadership (Genesis 25:31-34).

**Blasphemy** - Speaking against God; claiming God's attributes for oneself. The unforgivable sin is blasphemy against the Holy Spirit—permanently rejecting Him (Matthew 12:31-32).

**Bless/Blessing** - God's favor and goodness bestowed on someone. Can also mean to praise or worship God.

**Blood** - Represents life (Leviticus 17:11). Blood sacrifice required for atonement. Christ's blood cleanses from sin (Hebrews 9:22; 1 John 1:7).

**Body of Christ** - The Church; all believers collectively (1 Corinthians 12:27; Ephesians 1:22-23).

**Born Again** - Spiritual rebirth through the Holy Spirit when one trusts Christ. Necessary for salvation (John 3:3-7; 1 Peter 1:23).

**Bread of Life** - Jesus' description of Himself as the one who satisfies spiritual hunger (John 6:35).

**Bride of Christ** - The Church in its relationship to Christ (Ephesians 5:25-27; Revelation 19:7-9).

---

## C

**Canon** - "Measuring rod" or "standard." The 66 books recognized as inspired Scripture. See [How We Got the Bible](bible-origins.md).

**Centurion** - Roman military officer commanding 100 soldiers.

**Charisma/Charismatic** (Greek: χάρισμα) - Spiritual gift; grace-gift from God (Romans 12:6; 1 Corinthians 12:4).

**Cherubim** - High-ranking angelic beings associated with God's holiness and glory (Genesis 3:24; Ezekiel 10).

**Christ** - Greek for "Anointed One" (Hebrew: Messiah). Jesus is THE Christ, the promised Savior-King.

**Church** (Greek: ἐκκλησία, *ekklesia*) - "Called-out ones." The assembly of believers. Can refer to local congregation or universal body of all believers.

**Circumcision** - Removal of foreskin. Sign of God's covenant with Abraham and his descendants (Genesis 17:10-14). Represents being set apart for God. In NT, spiritual circumcision is "of the heart" (Romans 2:29; Colossians 2:11).

**Concubine** - Secondary wife with lower status. Common in ancient cultures; not God's ideal.

**Confession** - (1) Admitting sin to God (1 John 1:9); (2) Publicly declaring faith in Christ (Romans 10:9).

**Consecrate/Consecration** - To set apart as sacred; dedicate to God's service.

**Conversion** - Turning from sin to God; becoming a Christian through repentance and faith.

**Cornerstone** - The foundation stone of a building. Christ is the cornerstone of the Church (Ephesians 2:20; 1 Peter 2:6).

**Covenant** - Binding agreement between two parties. God made covenants with Noah, Abraham, Moses, David, and the New Covenant through Christ. See [Biblical Covenants](../indexes/covenants.md).

**Covenant of Works** - Theological term for God's arrangement with Adam: obey and live.

**Covenant of Grace** - Theological term for God's plan of salvation through faith, not works.

**Create/Creation** - To make something from nothing. Only God creates *ex nihilo* (Latin: "out of nothing"). Genesis 1-2.

**Cross** - Roman execution method. Instrument of Jesus' death. Symbol of atonement and Christian faith (1 Corinthians 1:18).

**Crucifixion** - Execution by nailing or tying to a cross. Jesus was crucified under Pontius Pilate (Matthew 27:35).

---

## D

**Deacon** - "Servant." Church office focused on practical service (Philippians 1:1; 1 Timothy 3:8-13).

**Dead Sea Scrolls** - Ancient manuscripts discovered 1947-1956 near Qumran. Include oldest OT manuscripts, proving remarkable preservation of Scripture. See [How We Got the Bible](bible-origins.md).

**Demon** - Evil spirit; fallen angel who follows Satan. Jesus cast out demons (Mark 1:34).

**Denarius** - Roman silver coin; a day's wage for a laborer (Matthew 20:2).

**Devil** - "Slanderer." Another name for Satan.

**Diaspora** - "Dispersion." Jews living outside Israel, scattered after exiles.

**Disciple** - "Learner" or "follower." One who learns from and follows a teacher. Christians are disciples of Jesus (Matthew 28:19).

**Discipline** - Training and correction. God disciplines His children for their good (Hebrews 12:5-11).

**Dispensation** - Period of time in which God deals with humanity in a particular way. Dispensationalism is a theological system emphasizing these periods.

**Doctrine** - Teaching; body of beliefs. "Sound doctrine" is biblical truth (1 Timothy 4:6; 2 Timothy 4:3).

---

## E

**Eden** - Location of the Garden where God placed Adam and Eve (Genesis 2:8). Paradise.

**Elder** (Greek: *presbyteros*) - Church leader/overseer. Same as "bishop" or "overseer" (Acts 20:17, 28; 1 Timothy 3:1-7; Titus 1:5-9).

**Elect/Election** - Those chosen by God for salvation. Debated whether God's choice is based on foreseen faith or unconditional (Romans 8:33; Ephesians 1:4; 1 Peter 1:1).

**Emmanuel/Immanuel** - "God with us" (Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:23). Name/title for Jesus.

**Epistle** - Letter. 21 NT books are epistles (Romans through Jude).

**Eschatology** - Study of end times; last things (death, judgment, heaven, hell, Christ's return).

**Eternal Life** - Life with God forever, beginning now and continuing eternally. Not just endless existence but quality of life knowing God (John 17:3).

**Eternity** - Timelessness; forever. God exists outside time (Psalm 90:2).

**Evangelist** - One who proclaims the gospel. Can be a spiritual gift (Ephesians 4:11) or general calling for all believers.

**Everlasting** - Eternal; without end.

**Evil One** - Satan (Matthew 6:13; John 17:15).

**Exile** - Forced removal from homeland. Israel exiled to Assyria (722 BC); Judah to Babylon (586 BC).

**Exodus** - "Way out." Israel's deliverance from Egypt under Moses. Type of salvation (Exodus 1-15).

**Expiation** - Removal of sin and guilt through sacrifice.

---

## F

**Faith** - Trust and confidence in God and His promises. "Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see" (Hebrews 11:1). Saving faith involves knowledge, assent, and trust.

**Faithful/Faithfulness** - Trustworthy; reliable; loyal. God is faithful (1 Corinthians 1:9). Christians are called to be faithful (Revelation 2:10).

**Fall, The** - Adam and Eve's sin in the Garden, bringing sin and death into the world (Genesis 3; Romans 5:12).

**Fasting** - Abstaining from food for spiritual purposes. Practiced for prayer, repentance, seeking God's guidance (Matthew 6:16-18).

**Fear of the Lord** - Reverent awe and respect for God. "Beginning of wisdom" (Proverbs 9:10). Not terror but proper respect.

**Firstborn** - The first male child. Had special rights and privileges. Jesus is "firstborn" in the sense of supremacy, not creation (Colossians 1:15, 18).

**Firstfruits** - First portion of harvest offered to God. Christ is "firstfruits" of resurrection (1 Corinthians 15:20, 23).

**Flesh** - (1) Physical body; (2) Sinful nature opposed to the Spirit (Romans 8:5-8; Galatians 5:16-17).

**Forgiveness** - Pardoning sin; releasing from penalty. God forgives those who repent and trust Christ (Ephesians 1:7; 1 John 1:9).

---

## G

**Gentile** - Non-Jewish person. Gospel is for both Jews and Gentiles (Romans 1:16; Ephesians 2:11-22).

**Glorify** - To honor; make glorious; show the glory of. Christians are to glorify God (1 Corinthians 10:31).

**Glory** - Weighty importance; splendor; honor. God's glory is His manifested presence and character (Exodus 33:18-23; John 1:14).

**Gnosticism** - Heresy teaching salvation through secret knowledge. Denied Christ's full humanity. Addressed in 1 John and Colossians.

**Gospel** - "Good news." The message of salvation through Jesus Christ's death and resurrection (1 Corinthians 15:1-4). See [The Gospel Explained](gospel-explained.md).

**Grace** - Unmerited favor; undeserved kindness. God's grace saves us (Ephesians 2:8-9). See also CHARIS.

**Great Commission** - Jesus' command to make disciples of all nations (Matthew 28:18-20).

---

## H

**Hades** - Greek word for realm of the dead (Luke 16:23). Not the same as Gehenna (hell).

**Hallelujah** - See ALLELUIA.

**Heaven** - (1) Sky/atmosphere; (2) Outer space; (3) God's dwelling place; eternal home of believers (John 14:2-3; Revelation 21-22).

**Hebrew** - (1) Language of OT; (2) Descendant of Abraham; Israelite.

**Heir** - One who inherits. Believers are heirs of God and co-heirs with Christ (Romans 8:17; Galatians 4:7).

**Hell** (Gehenna) - Place of eternal punishment for the wicked (Matthew 5:22, 29-30; Revelation 20:15). See also HADES.

**Heresy** - False teaching that denies essential Christian doctrine (2 Peter 2:1).

**Hesed** (Hebrew: חֶסֶד) - Covenant love; loyal love; steadfast love; lovingkindness. God's faithful, committed love (Exodus 34:6; Psalm 136).

**High Priest** - Chief priest who entered Most Holy Place once a year on Day of Atonement. Christ is our great High Priest (Hebrews 4:14-16).

**Holiness/Holy** - Set apart; morally pure; without sin. God is holy (Isaiah 6:3). Believers are called to be holy (1 Peter 1:15-16).

**Holy of Holies/Most Holy Place** - Innermost room of tabernacle/temple where God's presence dwelt. Only high priest entered, once a year (Hebrews 9:3-7).

**Holy Spirit** - Third person of the Trinity. God's presence dwelling in believers. Comforter, teacher, guide (John 14:16-17; Acts 1:8).

**Hope** - Confident expectation of future good. Christian hope is certain because based on God's promises (Romans 5:2-5; Hebrews 11:1).

**Hosanna** - "Save now!" or "Save, please!" Cry of praise (Matthew 21:9).

**Hypocrite** - One who pretends to be what they're not; insincere. Jesus condemned religious hypocrisy (Matthew 23).

---

## I

**I AM** - God's name revealed to Moses (Exodus 3:14). Jesus used "I AM" to claim deity (John 8:58; see also seven "I AM" statements in John).

**Idolatry** - Worship of idols/false gods; putting anything before God. Spiritual adultery (Exodus 20:3-5; 1 John 5:21).

**Image of God** (*Imago Dei*) - Humans created in God's likeness, bearing His image (Genesis 1:26-27). Includes rationality, morality, relationality, creativity.

**Immanuel** - See EMMANUEL.

**Immutability** - God's unchanging nature. "I the LORD do not change" (Malachi 3:6; Hebrews 13:8).

**Impute/Imputation** - To credit or assign to someone's account. Our sin was imputed to Christ; His righteousness is imputed to us (Romans 4:3-8; 2 Corinthians 5:21).

**Incarnation** - "In flesh." God the Son taking on human nature; Jesus becoming man (John 1:14; Philippians 2:7).

**Incense** - Fragrant substance burned in worship, symbolizing prayers rising to God (Psalm 141:2; Revelation 5:8).

**Inerrancy** - Belief that the Bible, in its original manuscripts, is without error in all it teaches.

**Infallibility** - The Bible is completely trustworthy and reliable in all it teaches.

**Inherit/Inheritance** - What believers receive from God: salvation, eternal life, kingdom (Ephesians 1:14; Colossians 1:12; 1 Peter 1:4).

**Iniquity** - Sin; moral crookedness; perversion of what's right.

**Inspiration** - "God-breathed." The Holy Spirit's work in guiding biblical authors to write God's Word without error (2 Timothy 3:16; 2 Peter 1:20-21).

**Intercession** - Praying on behalf of others. Christ and the Holy Spirit intercede for believers (Romans 8:26-27, 34; Hebrews 7:25).

**Israel** - (1) Name given to Jacob (Genesis 32:28); (2) Jacob's descendants; (3) Northern kingdom after the split; (4) All God's people (spiritual Israel).

---

## J

**Jehovah** - English rendering of God's name YHWH. More accurately "Yahweh." The covenant name of God.

**Jerusalem** - Holy city; capital of Israel. Historically significant. Destroyed 586 BC (Babylon) and 70 AD (Rome). Future "New Jerusalem" (Revelation 21).

**Jew** - Descendant of Judah; member of Jewish people; one who follows Judaism.

**Jubilee** - Every 50th year when debts were forgiven, slaves freed, land returned. Picture of salvation (Leviticus 25).

**Judgment** - God's evaluation and sentencing. Final judgment determines eternal destiny (Hebrews 9:27; Revelation 20:11-15).

**Justification** - Legal declaration that a sinner is righteous through faith in Christ. God declares believers "not guilty" and credits Christ's righteousness to them (Romans 3:24; 5:1). See [Key Doctrines](key-doctrines.md).

---

## K

**Kingdom of God/Heaven** - God's rule and reign. Present now in believers' hearts; future in fullness (Matthew 6:10; Luke 17:21).

**Kinsman-Redeemer** - Family member who rescues/redeems relatives from trouble. Boaz was Ruth's kinsman-redeemer. Christ is ours (Ruth 3:9; 4:14).

---

## L

**Lamb of God** - Title for Jesus emphasizing His sacrificial death for sin (John 1:29; Revelation 5:6).

**Last Days** - Period from Christ's first coming to His return (Acts 2:17; Hebrews 1:2; 1 John 2:18).

**Law** - (1) God's moral commands (Ten Commandments); (2) First five books of the Bible (Torah); (3) Entire OT; (4) Principle or rule.

**Legalism** - Attempting to earn salvation or favor with God through rule-keeping. Opposed to grace (Galatians 3:2-3).

**Leviathan** - Large sea creature, possibly whale or crocodile. Sometimes symbolic of chaos/evil (Job 41; Psalm 74:14).

**Levitical Priesthood** - OT priesthood from tribe of Levi. Offered sacrifices; mediated between God and people. Superseded by Christ's priesthood (Hebrews 7-10).

**Logos** (Greek: λόγος) - "Word." Title for Christ emphasizing He is God's ultimate revelation (John 1:1, 14).

**Lord** - Master; one with authority. Title for God (Yahweh in OT; Jesus in NT). Confession "Jesus is Lord" central to Christianity (Romans 10:9).

**Lord's Day** - Sunday; day of Christ's resurrection. Day Christians gather for worship (Acts 20:7; Revelation 1:10).

**Lord's Supper** - Communion; Eucharist. Memorial meal instituted by Jesus, remembering His death (1 Corinthians 11:23-26).

**Love** - Self-giving commitment to others' good. God IS love (1 John 4:8). See AGAPE.

---

## M

**Manna** - Bread God provided Israel in wilderness (Exodus 16). Type of Christ, the bread of life (John 6:31-35).

**Mediator** - Go-between who reconciles parties. Jesus is the one mediator between God and humanity (1 Timothy 2:5; Hebrews 9:15).

**Mercy** - Compassion; not giving punishment deserved. God is rich in mercy (Ephesians 2:4).

**Mercy Seat** - Lid of the Ark of the Covenant. Where God met with high priest; where blood was sprinkled on Day of Atonement (Exodus 25:17-22; Hebrews 9:5).

**Messiah** - Hebrew for "Anointed One" (Greek: Christ). The promised Savior-King. Jesus is THE Messiah (John 1:41; 4:25-26).

**Millennium** - 1,000-year reign mentioned in Revelation 20. Debated: literal vs. symbolic; timing relative to Christ's return.

**Minister/Ministry** - Service; one who serves. All believers have a ministry (Ephesians 4:12).

**Miracle** - Supernatural act of God; sign pointing to God's power and presence.

**Monotheism** - Belief in one God. Core of Jewish and Christian faith (Deuteronomy 6:4; 1 Timothy 2:5).

**Mystery** - Truth once hidden, now revealed. Not mysterious but previously unknown (Ephesians 3:3-9; Colossians 1:26-27).

---

## N

**New Covenant** - God's covenant through Christ's blood, replacing the old covenant. Promises forgiveness, new hearts, Holy Spirit (Jeremiah 31:31-34; Luke 22:20; Hebrews 8-10).

**New Creation** - What believers become in Christ (2 Corinthians 5:17). Also refers to future new heavens and new earth (Revelation 21:1).

---

## O

**Omnipotent** - All-powerful. Attribute of God (Revelation 19:6).

**Omnipresent** - Present everywhere simultaneously. Attribute of God (Psalm 139:7-10).

**Omniscient** - All-knowing. God knows everything actual and possible (Psalm 147:5; 1 John 3:20).

**Oracle** - Divine utterance; message from God.

**Ordain** - (1) Set apart for ministry; (2) Decree or determine.

**Orthodox/Orthodoxy** - Right belief; sound doctrine. Conforming to biblical truth.

---

## P

**Parable** - Earthly story with spiritual meaning. Teaching method Jesus used (Matthew 13). See [Parables Index](../indexes/parables.md).

**Paraclete** - Greek: *parakletos* - "one called alongside to help." Title for Holy Spirit: Comforter, Helper, Advocate (John 14:16, 26).

**Paradise** - Place of blessing with God. Used for Garden of Eden; intermediate state; heaven (Luke 23:43; 2 Corinthians 12:4; Revelation 2:7).

**Passover** - Jewish feast commemorating deliverance from Egypt (Exodus 12). Christ our Passover lamb (1 Corinthians 5:7). See [OT Feasts](old-testament-feasts.md).

**Pastor** - "Shepherd." Church leader who cares for and guides congregation (Ephesians 4:11; 1 Peter 5:2).

**Pentecost** - Jewish feast 50 days after Passover. Day Holy Spirit came upon the church (Acts 2).

**Pharisees** - Jewish religious sect emphasizing strict observance of the Law and oral tradition. Often opposed Jesus (Matthew 23).

**Phileo** (Greek: φιλέω) - Brotherly love; friendship love. Different from agape.

**Polytheism** - Belief in many gods. Opposed by Scripture.

**Predestination** - God's sovereign choice of who will be saved. Debated: unconditional vs. based on foreseen faith (Romans 8:29-30; Ephesians 1:5, 11).

**Priest** - One who mediates between God and people; offers sacrifices. All believers are priests (1 Peter 2:9). Christ is our High Priest (Hebrews 4:14).

**Prophet** - One who speaks God's message. Includes foretelling (prediction) and forthtelling (proclaiming truth).

**Propitiation** - Satisfaction of God's wrath through sacrifice. Christ's death propitiated (satisfied) God's justice (Romans 3:25; 1 John 2:2; 4:10).

**Proverb** - Short, wise saying. Book of Proverbs contains practical wisdom.

**Providence** - God's sovereign care and governance of all creation.

**Psalm** - Song of praise or prayer. Book of Psalms contains 150 psalms.

**Purgatory** - Roman Catholic teaching of place/state where souls are purified before heaven. Not taught in Scripture; rejected by Protestants.

---

## R

**Rabbi** - "My teacher" or "my master." Jewish title of respect for teachers (John 1:38).

**Ransom** - Price paid to free a slave or captive. Christ paid ransom for our sins (Mark 10:45; 1 Timothy 2:6).

**Rapture** - "Catching up." Belief that Christ will take believers to heaven, either before, during, or after tribulation (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17).

**Reconciliation** - Restoration of broken relationship. God reconciles sinners to Himself through Christ (Romans 5:10-11; 2 Corinthians 5:18-21).

**Redeem/Redemption** - To buy back; purchase freedom. Christ redeemed us from sin's slavery (Galatians 3:13; Ephesians 1:7; Colossians 1:14).

**Regeneration** - Spiritual rebirth; being "born again." Work of Holy Spirit making a person alive spiritually (John 3:3-8; Titus 3:5).

**Remnant** - Small group remaining faithful when majority falls away (Romans 11:5).

**Repentance** - Turning from sin to God; change of mind leading to change of action (Acts 3:19; 2 Corinthians 7:10). Essential for salvation.

**Resurrection** - Rising from the dead. Christ's resurrection is foundation of Christian faith (1 Corinthians 15). Believers will be resurrected.

**Revelation** - God's disclosure of Himself and His will. General revelation (creation); special revelation (Scripture).

**Righteous/Righteousness** - Right standing before God; moral perfection. Believers receive Christ's righteousness through faith (Romans 3:22; 2 Corinthians 5:21).

---

## S

**Sabbath** - Seventh day; day of rest. Saturday for Jews. Christians worship on Sunday (Lord's Day) to commemorate resurrection.

**Sacrament** - Sacred ordinance. Protestants recognize two: baptism and Lord's Supper. Catholics recognize seven.

**Sacrifice** - Offering to God, especially for sin. OT sacrifices pointed to Christ's once-for-all sacrifice (Hebrews 9:26; 10:12).

**Sadducees** - Jewish sect denying resurrection, angels, spirits. Accepted only Torah. Often allied with temple priesthood (Acts 23:8).

**Saint** - "Holy one." All Christians, not just exceptionally holy people (Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 1:2). Set apart for God.

**Salvation** - Deliverance from sin and its consequences through Christ. Includes justification, sanctification, glorification. See [The Gospel Explained](gospel-explained.md).

**Sanctification** - Process of being made holy; growing in Christlikeness. Begins at conversion; continues throughout life (1 Thessalonians 4:3; 2 Thessalonians 2:13).

**Sanctuary** - Holy place; place of worship (tabernacle, temple). Believers are God's sanctuary (1 Corinthians 3:16-17).

**Sanhedrin** - Jewish ruling council; 71 members. Tried Jesus (Matthew 26:59).

**Satan** - "Adversary." The devil; leader of fallen angels; enemy of God and His people (1 Peter 5:8; Revelation 12:9).

**Savior** - One who saves/rescues. Jesus is THE Savior (Luke 2:11; John 4:42; Titus 2:13).

**Scribe** - Copyist and teacher of the Law. Often linked with Pharisees in NT.

**Scripture** - Sacred writings; the Bible. "All Scripture is God-breathed" (2 Timothy 3:16).

**Seal/Sealing** - Mark of ownership and protection. Believers are sealed by Holy Spirit (Ephesians 1:13; 4:30).

**Second Coming** - Christ's return to earth in glory to judge and reign (Acts 1:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:16; Revelation 19:11-16).

**Selah** - Musical or liturgical term in Psalms. Possibly means "pause" or "interlude."

**Septuagint (LXX)** - Greek translation of Hebrew OT (~250-150 BC). Used by NT writers. See [How We Got the Bible](bible-origins.md).

**Seraphim** - Angelic beings associated with God's holiness (Isaiah 6:2-6).

**Sermon on the Mount** - Jesus' teaching in Matthew 5-7. Includes Beatitudes, Lord's Prayer, Golden Rule.

**Servant** - One who serves. Jesus is the Suffering Servant (Isaiah 53; Philippians 2:7). Christians are servants of Christ.

**Shalom** (Hebrew: שָׁלוֹם) - Peace; wholeness; completeness. Not just absence of conflict but fullness of well-being.

**Shekinah** - Glory of God; visible manifestation of His presence (though the word itself isn't in Scripture).

**Sheol** - Hebrew word for realm of the dead (Psalm 16:10). Similar to Hades.

**Shepherd** - One who cares for sheep. God is our shepherd (Psalm 23). Jesus is the Good Shepherd (John 10:11). Pastors are shepherds (1 Peter 5:2).

**Sin** - Missing the mark; falling short of God's standard; rebellion against God (Romans 3:23; 1 John 3:4).

**Son of God** - Title for Jesus emphasizing His deity and unique relationship to the Father (Matthew 3:17; John 1:34).

**Son of Man** - Jesus' favorite self-designation. Emphasizes His humanity; also messianic title from Daniel 7:13-14 (Mark 2:10).

**Soul** - The immaterial part of a person; often synonymous with "spirit." Seat of emotions, will, personality.

**Sovereignty** - God's supreme authority and control over all creation (Psalm 115:3; Daniel 4:35).

**Spirit** - (1) Immaterial part of humans; (2) The Holy Spirit; (3) Disposition or attitude.

**Steward/Stewardship** - Manager of someone else's property. Christians are stewards of God's resources (1 Corinthians 4:2; 1 Peter 4:10).

**Substitution** - Taking another's place. Christ died as our substitute, bearing our punishment (Isaiah 53:5-6; 2 Corinthians 5:21).

**Synagogue** - Jewish place of worship and teaching. Developed during exile.

## T

**Tabernacle** - Portable tent-sanctuary where God dwelt with Israel in wilderness (Exodus 25-40). Type of Christ. See [Types & Shadows](../indexes/types-and-shadows.md).

**Temple** - Permanent structure replacing tabernacle. Solomon's temple, rebuilt by Zerubbabel, expanded by Herod, destroyed 70 AD. Christ is the true temple (John 2:19-21). Believers are God's temple (1 Corinthians 3:16).

**Temptation** - Enticement to sin. Not sin itself. Jesus was tempted but sinless (Hebrews 4:15). God provides escape from temptation (1 Corinthians 10:13).

**Testament** - Covenant. "Old Testament" and "New Testament" refer to the two major covenants.

**Testimony** - Witness; declaration of what one has experienced. Christians testify about Jesus (Acts 1:8).

**Tetragrammaton** - Four Hebrew letters (YHWH) representing God's name. Jews don't pronounce it, saying "Adonai" (Lord) instead.

**Theology** - Study of God; systematic understanding of biblical truth.

**Theophany** - Visible appearance of God (e.g., burning bush, Exodus 3).

**Tithe** - "Tenth." Giving 10% to God. Commanded in OT (Malachi 3:10); principle of generous giving continues in NT (2 Corinthians 9:7).

**Transgression** - Overstepping boundaries; violating God's law; sin.

**Transfiguration** - Event where Jesus' glory was revealed to Peter, James, and John (Matthew 17:1-13).

**Tribulation** - Suffering, trial. "The Great Tribulation" refers to end-times period of intense suffering (Matthew 24:21; Revelation 7:14).

**Trinity** - One God existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Not three gods; one God in three persons (Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14). See [Key Doctrines](key-doctrines.md).

**Truth** - Reality as God defines it. Jesus is "the truth" (John 14:6). God's Word is truth (John 17:17).

**Type/Typology** - OT person, event, or object foreshadowing NT reality, especially Christ. See [Types & Shadows](../indexes/types-and-shadows.md).

## U

**Unclean** - Ceremonially impure; unable to approach God or participate in worship. Cleansing rituals required (Leviticus 11-15). Christ declared all foods clean (Mark 7:19).

**Ungodly** - Wicked; lacking reverence for God; living contrary to God's ways.

**Unity** - Oneness. God is one (Deuteronomy 6:4). Believers should maintain unity (Ephesians 4:3-6).

**Unleavened** - Without yeast. Unleavened bread used in Passover, symbolizing haste and purity (Exodus 12:8).

---

## V

**Veil** - Curtain separating Holy Place from Most Holy Place in temple. Tore when Jesus died, symbolizing access to God (Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:19-20).

**Vineyard** - Often symbolic of Israel or God's people (Isaiah 5:1-7; Matthew 21:33-44).

**Virgin Birth** - Jesus conceived by Holy Spirit, born of virgin Mary (Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:18-25; Luke 1:26-38). Essential doctrine affirming Jesus' divine and human natures.

**Vision** - Supernatural revelation; seeing spiritual realities. God communicated through visions (Acts 10:9-16; Revelation 1:10-20).

---

## W

**Witness** - (1) One who testifies to what they've seen/experienced; (2) To testify. Christians witness about Christ (Acts 1:8).

**Word** - (1) God's message/Scripture; (2) Jesus as the eternal Word (Logos) who reveals God (John 1:1, 14).

**Works** - Actions; deeds. Good works result from faith but don't save us (Ephesians 2:8-10; James 2:17).

**Worship** - Ascribing worth to God; honoring and adoring Him. Involves both lifestyle and corporate gatherings (John 4:23-24; Romans 12:1).

**Wrath** - God's holy anger against sin. Jesus bore God's wrath for us (Romans 5:9; 1 Thessalonians 1:10).

---

## Y

**Yahweh/YHWH** - God's personal covenant name revealed to Moses (Exodus 3:14). Often translated "LORD" in English Bibles. Means "I AM WHO I AM" or "I WILL BE WHO I WILL BE."

**Yoke** - Wooden frame joining two animals to pull together. Metaphor for burden or bondage. Jesus offers an easy yoke (Matthew 11:29-30).

---

## Z

**Zeal** - Passionate enthusiasm; fervor for God and His purposes (Romans 12:11; Titus 2:14).

**Zion** - (1) Hill in Jerusalem where temple stood; (2) Jerusalem itself; (3) God's people; (4) Heaven. Term of great significance in prophecy and poetry (Psalm 48; Isaiah 2:3; Hebrews 12:22).
